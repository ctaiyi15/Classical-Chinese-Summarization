其改正朔，易服色，变牺牲，殊徽帜，异器制。
以十二月朔癸酉为建国元年正月之朔，以鸡鸣为时。
服色配德上黄，牺牲应正用白，使节之旄幡皆纯黄，其署曰 新使王威节 ，以承皇天上帝威命也。

translation:
The first day of the first lunar month should be changed, the colors of carriages, horses and clothing should be changed, the coat color of animals used for sacrifice should be changed, signs such as flags should be changed, and the shapes of utensils should be redesigned.
The first day of December this year is regarded as the first day of the first lunar month of the founding of the People's Republic of China, and the day starts with the rooster's crow.
The color of carriages, horses, and clothing is yellow. The animals used for sacrifices and the first month of the year should be white. The yak tails used for decoration on the envoys are all pure yellow, with the words "New Envoys and Five Powers Festival" written on them to show the majesty of the emperor.

summary:
He declared a new country, changed the calendar, and set the colors of carriages, horses, and clothing to yellow.
