Feng Shen, cautious and dignified, was made Marquis of Yixiang but committed suicide under Emperor Ai after being framed for treason, along with seventeen family members. The Feng family then returned to their hometown. The historical commentary laments their tragic fate, comparing it to ancient figures like Boqi and Qu Yuan.
