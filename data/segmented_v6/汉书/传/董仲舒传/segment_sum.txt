From the time when Emperor Wu of the Han Dynasty first came to the throne, Wei Qihou Dou Ying and Wu'an Hou Tian successively became prime ministers and began to promote Confucianism. Dong Zhongshu responded by promoting Confucius and suppressing hundreds of schools of thought.
It was Dong Zhongshu who set up officials to manage schools and recommended talents, filial piety and honesty in the prefectures and counties.
Dong Zhongshu died at home in old age. Later, his family moved to Maoling County, and his son and grandson became high officials based on their knowledge.
Dong Zhongshu's works all clarify the purpose of Confucian classics. Together with memorials and teachings, there are a total of 123 works.
There are dozens of articles explaining the gains and losses of the "Spring and Autumn" records, as well as "Wen Ju", "Jade Cup", "Fan Dew", "Qingming" and "Bamboo Forest", with more than 100,000 words, all of which have been passed down to later generations.
I selected the content that is relevant to today's society and the imperial court and wrote it in the article.

Liu Xiang's son Liu Xin believed that Yi Yin and Lu Wang were the companions of a saint, and a king could not rise without them.

After the Western Han Dynasty succeeded the Qin Dynasty and the burning of scholarship, Dong Zhongshu's "Six Classics" fell apart. So he put down his work and devoted himself to studying Confucian classics, which enabled later scholars to have a systematic and consistent understanding of Confucianism and became the leader of the group of Confucian scholars.
However, when examining the origins of his mentors and friends and looking at their influence on each other, Dong Zhongshu could not catch up with Zi You and Zi Xia, but it is wrong to say that Guan Zhong and Yan Ying were not as good as him, and Yi Yin and Lu Wang were not as good as him.
Liu Xiang's great-grandson Liu Gong is a gentleman who is good at commenting on figures. He believes that Liu Xin's evaluation of Dong Zhongshu is appropriate.
