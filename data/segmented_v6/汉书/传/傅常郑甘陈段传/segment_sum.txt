天子赐会宗爵关内侯，黄金百斤。
是时，小昆弥季父卑爰疐拥众欲害昆弥，汉复遣会宗使安辑，与都护孙建并力。
明年，会宗病死乌孙中，年七十五矣，城郭诸国为发丧立祠焉。
赞曰：自元狩之际，张骞始通西域，至于地节，郑吉建都护之号，讫王莽世，凡十八人，皆以勇略选，然其有功迹者具此。
廉褒以恩信称，郭舜以廉平著，孙建用威重显，其余无称焉。
陈汤傥，不自收敛，卒用困穷，议者闵之，故备列云。

translation:
Duan Huizong said: If you tell King Wusun in advance and let Fanqiu escape and hide, you will commit a serious crime.
If I provided him with food and then handed it over to me, it would be a disservice to my family members, so I didn't inform him in advance.
King Wusun and his men wept and withdrew their troops.
Duan Huizong went back to report the situation. The ministers discussed that Duan Huizong should act according to the situation and use a small force to go deep into the Wusun Kingdom and kill Fanqiu. This demonstrated the country's prestige and should be rewarded heavily.
The emperor gave Duan Huizong the title of Marquis of Guannei and one hundred catties of gold.
At this time, Bei Yuanxu, the younger brother of King Wusun's father, led everyone to harm King Wusun. The Han Dynasty sent Duan Huizong there to work with Sun Jian, the protector, to appease him.
The next year, Duan Huizong died of illness in Wusun Kingdom at the age of seventy-five. All countries in the city mourned him and built ancestral halls for him.
Zan said: From the Yuanshou period, Zhang Qian began to communicate with the Western Regions. In the Dijie period, Zheng Ji established the title of Protector. Until the era of Wang Mang, there were 18 people in total. They were all elected for their bravery and strategy. However, those who had meritorious deeds among them are all recorded here.
Lian Guo was praised for his kindness and trust, Guo Shun was famous for his honesty and fairness, Sun Jian was famous for his dignity in handling affairs, and the rest of them had nothing to praise.
Chen Tang behaved unruly and did not restrain himself, and finally fell into hardship and embarrassment. The commentators pitied him, so they also listed him here.
