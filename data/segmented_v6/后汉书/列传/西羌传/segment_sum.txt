Sometimes when the border reconnaissance is peaceful, they will be angry and try to cause chaos; once drums and drums and military revolution suddenly arise, they will hang their quivers and panic like birds.
Therefore, during the Yongchu period, the various tribes of the Qiang people protruded like a swarm of bees.
So they resolved their hatreds, made alliances and swore an oath, attracted powerful people from the mountains, and then greeted each other and gathered together, holding up trees as weapons and carrying firewood as shields.
The military chariots and war horses raised dust and ran rampant in the Sanfu area; they usurped the name of the emperor and wreaked havoc in the north.
