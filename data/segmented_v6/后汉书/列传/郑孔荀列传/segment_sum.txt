When Dong Zhao proposed making Cao Cao Duke of Jin, Xun Yu opposed it, saying Cao Cao should remain loyal to Han. Cao Cao grew resentful. During a southern campaign, Cao Cao kept Xun Yu at his camp, then sent him an empty food box. Xun Yu took poison and died at fifty. The next year, Cao Cao declared himself Duke of Wei.
