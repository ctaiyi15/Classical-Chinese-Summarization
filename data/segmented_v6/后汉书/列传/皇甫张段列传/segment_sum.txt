在位月余，会日食自劾，有司举奏，诏收印绶，诣廷尉。
时司隶校尉阳球奏诛王甫，并及颎，就狱中诘责之，遂饮鸩死，家属徙边。

translation:
Duan Jiong was granted the title of Marquis of Xinfeng County, with ten thousand households in the city.
Duan Yan marched with benevolence, and when his soldiers were sick, he personally expressed condolences and bandaged their wounds.
I have been on the border for more than ten years and have never had a good night's sleep.
He shares the joys and sorrows with the soldiers, so the soldiers are willing to fight to the death for him.
In the spring of the third year, he summoned back the capital and led more than 50,000 Qin and Hu infantry and cavalry, as well as thousands of horses with sweat and blood, and captured more than 10,000 people.
The emperor sent Dahong to show condolences to Hao.
When the troops arrived, Duan Jiong was granted the title of Shizhong. He was transferred to Jinwu and Henan Yin.
Some thieves excavated Feng Guiren's tomb, and after being convicted, he was demoted to an admonishing doctor, and then promoted to a lieutenant.
Duan Juan relied on the eunuchs, so he could keep his wealth and honor. He became a party member with Wang Fu, the official of the Zhongchang, and unjustly killed Zheng Sa, Dong Teng and others.
In the second year, he replaced Li Xian as Taiwei. In the winter of this year, he was dismissed due to illness and was appointed as Sili Xiaowei again.
A few years later, he was transferred to the prefect of Yingchuan and recruited as a doctor in Taizhong.
In the second year of Guanghe's reign, he replaced Qiao Xuan as Taiwei.
After reigning for more than a month, he impeached himself due to a solar eclipse. Officials reported him and ordered him to take away his seal and ribbon and send him to the court for trial.
At this time, Captain Yang Qiu of the Sili School ordered Wang Fu to be killed, which implicated Duan Jiong, so he was questioned and reprimanded in prison. Duan Jiong then committed suicide by committing poison, and his family members moved to the border.

summary:
He later relied on eunuchs and unjustly killed several people. He served as Taiwei but committed suicide in prison after being implicated in a murder. The three men were collectively known as "Liangzhou Sanming.".
