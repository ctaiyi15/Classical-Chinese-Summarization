及琼卒归葬，稺乃负粮徒步到江夏赴之，设鸡酒薄祭，哭毕而去，不告姓名。

translation:
Xu Yan from Yuzhang, Jiang Gong from Pengcheng, Yuan Hong from Runan, Zhu from Jing Zhaowei, and Li Tan from Yingchuan were generally recognized as having pure virtues.
If they are promoted to three things and cooperate with heaven's work, they will surely be able to carry forward their virtues and make the sun and moon shine.
Emperor Huan then used a car, Xuanlin fumigation, and prepared a ceremony to invite them, but they didn't come out.
The emperor then asked Chen Fan: "Which one comes first, Xu Zhen, Yuan Hong or Wei Zhu?"
Chen Fan replied: Yuan Hong was born into a noble family, and he gradually learned the Tao.
Wei Zhu was good at the customs of Sanfu etiquette and righteousness. It is said that if the fluff grows in the hemp, it will straighten itself without support, and it will carve without carving.
As for Xu Zhen, he was born in a humble area in the south of the Yangtze River. He is as outstanding as a horn and should be the first.
Xu Zhen was once summoned by Taiwei Huang Qiong, but he refused to go.
After Huang Qiong died and was buried, Xu Zhu walked to Jiangxia carrying food on his back, held a small sacrifice of chicken and wine, and left after crying without telling his name.

summary:
He refused official posts, including from Taiwei Huang Qiong, and predicted the disaster of the Partisan Prohibitions, which he escaped by living in seclusion. He died at seventy-four, maintaining his principles through turbulent times.
