When the emperor returned, he waited in Taixue for an auspicious day to enter the palace. In the evening, he sent an envoy with an order to collect Taiwei Yang Zhen's seal and ribbon. Yang Zhen then closed the door to exclude guests.
Fan Feng and others still hated him, so they asked General Geng Bao to report that Minister Yang Zhen refused to plead guilty and was resentful, and ordered him to be sent back to his hometown.
Yang Zhen walked to Jiyang Pavilion in the west of the city and said generously to his son and student: Death is inevitable for a person.
I am favored and occupy the position, but I hate the traitorous ministers who are cunning but cannot be punished, and the evil mistresses who are causing chaos but cannot be restrained. How can I show my face to the world?
On the day of death, a coffin of mixed wood is used, and the body is only covered with a sheet. There is no burial place or a shrine.
So he took poison and died at the age of seventy.
In response to the orders of Fan Feng and others, the prefect of Hongnong moved to Liang and sent officials to stay at Yang Zhen's funeral in Shaanxi County. They exposed the coffin beside the road and ordered Yang Zhen's disciples to post and write letters on their behalf. Everyone on the road shed tears for it.
When Emperor Shun was over, Fan Feng, Zhou Guang and others were put to death. Yang Zhen was a student of Yu Fang, and Chen Yi went to the court to complain about Yang Zhen.
The imperial court called him Yang Zhenzhong. He issued an edict to confer the title of Lang to his two sons, gave him a million yuan in money, and buried Yang Zhen in Tongting, Huayin with proper ceremony.
Ten days before the burial, a large bird with a height of more than ten feet flew to Yang Zhen's funeral. It leaned down and screamed in mourning, weeping on the wet ground. After the burial, it flew away.
The county reported the situation.
At that time, disasters occurred one after another. The emperor felt that Yang Zhen was an unjust case, and issued an edict saying: The late Taiwei Yang Zhen was an upright person, so he assisted in current affairs.
My stupidity aggravated this sin. The mountains collapsed and the pillars were broken. How dangerous I was!
Now let the prefect and prime minister use the palace to have a shrine with soul and spirit, or come and enjoy these sacrifices of mine.
So people erected a stone bird statue in front of Yang Zhen's tomb to commemorate him.
Yang Zhen was framed, Gao Shu was also convicted, and he was accused of reducing his death.
When Yang Zhen was vindicated, Gao Shu paid homage to the censor and went to Jingzhou to judge the history.
Yang Zhen has five sons.
The eldest son Yang Mu was appointed Prime Minister of Fubo.

summary:
When Zhao Teng was arrested for criticizing the government, Yang Zhen pleaded for leniency, but the emperor ignored him, and Zhao Teng was killed. In the third year, Fan Feng framed Yang Zhen for resentment after Zhao Teng's death. The emperor stripped Yang Zhen of his position and ordered him home. At Jiyang Pavilion, Yang Zhen declared he could not face the world with traitors unpunished, took poison, and died at seventy. His coffin was exposed by the roadside. After Emperor Shun ascended, Fan Feng was executed. Yang Zhen's students petitioned, and the emperor posthumously honored him, granting titles and money, and buried him with ceremony. A large bird mourned at his funeral, and a stone bird was erected in his memory. His son Yang Mu became Prime Minister of Fubo.
