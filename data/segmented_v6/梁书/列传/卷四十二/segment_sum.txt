After the fall of Miyagi, Fu Qi escaped from the siege despite being ill and later died at home.
Yao Cha, the Minister of the Ministry of Civil Affairs of Chen, said: Those who preside over affairs must plan carefully first, so they will not make mistakes in anything they do. What Kuo said is so true.
