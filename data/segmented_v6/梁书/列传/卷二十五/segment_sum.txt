给东园秘器，朝服一具，衣一袭。
赠钱二十万，布百匹。
皇太子亦举哀朝堂。
谥曰简肃公。
勉善属文，勤著述，虽当机务，下笔不休。
尝以起居注烦杂，乃加删撰为《别起居注》六百卷；《左丞弹事》五卷；在选曹，撰《选品》五卷；齐时，撰《太庙祝文》二卷；以孔释二教殊途同归，撰《会林》五十卷。
凡所著前后二集四十五卷，又为《妇人集》十卷，皆行于世。
大同三年，故佐史尚书左丞刘览等诣阙陈勉行状，请刊石纪德，即降诏许立碑于墓云。
悱字敬业，幼聪敏，能属文。
起家著作佐郎，转太子舍人，掌书记之任。
累迁洗马、中舍人，犹管书记。
出入宫坊者历稔，以足疾出为湘东王友，迁晋安内史。
陈吏部尚书姚察曰：徐勉少而厉志忘食，发愤修身，慎言行，择交游；加运属兴王，依光日月，故能明经术以绾青紫，出闾阎而取卿相。

In the third year of Zhongdatong, due to illness, he stated his own requirements and was granted the title of Tejin, Youguanglu, Shizhong, Zhongwei General, and auxiliary staff were set up. The rest remained as usual.
Added forty confidants.
The two palaces expressed condolences, and officials and vehicles connected with each other; food and medicine were all provided by the palace treasury.
Every time the emperor wanted to go there due to an order, Xu Mian frequently asked the emperor to stop leaving the palace because he could not complete the ceremony of prostrating himself on the ground. The edict allowed the emperor to stay in the palace.
In the first year of Datong, Xu Mian passed away at the age of seventy.
When Emperor Gaozu heard about it, he shed tears and took a car to the funeral home that day. So he sent a posthumous gift to Tejin, Dr. Youguanglu, and Kaifu Yitong in Xia Shao. The rest remained as before.
He was given a coffin, a set of robes, and a set of clothes.
Two hundred thousand coins and one hundred pieces of cloth were given as gifts.
The crown prince also held a funeral in the court hall.
Mi's nickname is Jian Sugong.
Xu Mian was good at writing articles and diligent in writing. Although he was in a confidential position, he never stopped writing.
Because the notes on daily life were complicated, they were edited and edited into six hundred volumes of "Notes on Living Things"; five volumes of "Tanshi of Zuo Cheng"; five volumes of "Selections" were written in the department of selecting officials; two volumes of "Blessings to the Imperial Ancestral Temple" were written in the Qi Dynasty; because Confucius and Buddhism achieved the same goal through different channels, fifty volumes of "Huilin" were written.
He wrote a total of 45 volumes in two volumes before and after, and also wrote "Women's Collection" in ten volumes, all of which are popular in the world.
In the third year of Datong, the former Zuocheng Liu Lan and others went to the court to report Xu Mian's conduct and achievements, and requested that a monument be engraved to record his merits. They immediately agreed to erect a monument in the cemetery.
Xu Qiu is a dedicated person, smart since he was a child, and able to write articles.
He became an official as Zuo Lang and was appointed as Prince Sheren, responsible for the responsibilities of document recording.
After being promoted many times to Xima and Zhongshe, he was still in charge of document records.
He had been in and out of the prince's palace for many years. Due to a foot disease, he was appointed as King You of Crocodile and was transferred to the internal history of Jin'an.
Chen Libu Shangshu fled the fortress and said: When Xu Mian was young, he sharpened his ambitions and forgot about food, worked hard to cultivate his body and mind, was careful in his words and deeds, and chose friends. In addition, when he encountered the fortune of the rise of the king, he relied on the light of the sun and the moon, so he was able to gain a high position because of his knowledge of the classics, and was appointed as a minister in the palace.
