诸将以其故不亲附，此其所以败也。
陈胜虽已死，其所置遣侯王将相竟亡秦，由涉首事也。
高祖时为陈涉置守冢三十家砀，至今血食。

translation:
After General Lu Chen failed to escape, he regrouped his troops.
He joined forces with the army led by Yingbu, who had been a thief in Poyang and was named Dangyang Lord, and attacked the army of Qin's left and right captains again, defeated them at Qingbo, and once again made Chen County the capital of Chu.
Just when Xiang Liang supported King Huai of Chu, his grandson Xin became King of Chu.
Chen Sheng was king for a total of six months.
After becoming king, Chen County was made the capital.
Once upon a time, a fellow who had been hired to plow fields with him heard that he had become king. He came to Chen County, knocked on the palace door and said, "I want to see Chen She."
The officer guarding the palace gate wanted to tie him up.
After he repeatedly explained, he was released, but still refused to inform him.
When King Chen went out, he blocked the road and called Chen She's name.
After hearing this, King Chen summoned him and took a carriage back to the palace with him.
After walking into the palace and seeing the halls, houses and curtains, the guest said: Hello!
King Chen She's palace is so tall and profound!
The people of Chu called Duo Huo She the king, so the saying that Huo She was the king started from Chen She.
This guest came and went in and out of the palace more and more freely, and often told people about Chen She's past events.
Someone said to King Chen: Your guest is ignorant and talks nonsense, which is detrimental to your majesty.
King Chen killed the visitor.
From then on, all the old and close friends of King Chen left voluntarily, and no one was close to King Chen anymore.
King Chen appointed Zhu Fang to be the Zhongzheng and Hu Wu to be the Supervisor of Supervision to supervise the faults of the ministers.
The generals captured the place and returned to Chen County. If they disobeyed the orders, they would arrest and punish them, and they would harshly seek the faults of the officials as a sign of loyalty to King Chen.
Anyone they don't like, once they make a mistake, they will punish them without giving it to the officials in charge of justice. King Chen trusted them very much.
Because of these reasons, the generals no longer stayed close to him. This is the reason why King Chen failed.
Although Chen Sheng was dead, the princes and generals he appointed and sent finally destroyed the Qin Dynasty. This was due to Chen She's first uprising against Qin.
During the reign of Emperor Gaozu of the Han Dynasty, thirty families were placed in Dang County to guard Chen She's tomb, and they still kill animals on time to offer sacrifices to him.
