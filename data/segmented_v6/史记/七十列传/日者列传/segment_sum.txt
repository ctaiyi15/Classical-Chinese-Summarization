自古受命而王，王者之兴何尝不以卜筮决於天命哉！
其於周尤甚，及秦可见。
代王之入，任於卜者。
太卜之起，由汉兴而有。
司马季主者，楚人也。
卜於长安东市。
宋忠为中大夫，贾谊为博士，同日俱出洗沐，相从论议，诵易先王圣人之道术，究遍人情，相视而叹。
贾谊曰： 吾闻古之圣人，不居朝廷，必在卜医之中。
今吾已见三公九卿朝士大夫，皆可知矣。
试之卜数中以观采。
二人即同舆而之市，游於卜肆中。
天新雨，道少人，司马季主间坐，弟子三四人侍，方辩天地之道，日月之运，阴阳吉凶之本。
二大夫再拜谒。
司马季主视其状貌，如类有知者，即礼之，使弟子延之坐。
坐定，司马季主复理前语，分别天地之终始，日月星辰之纪，差次仁义之际，列吉凶之符，语数千言，莫不顺理。
宋忠、贾谊瞿然而悟，猎缨正襟危坐，曰： 吾望先生之状，听先生之辞，小子窃观於世，未尝见也。
今何居之卑，何行之汙？
司马季主捧腹大笑曰： 观大夫类有道术者，今何言之陋也，何辞之野也！
今夫子所贤者何也？
所高者谁也？
今何以卑汙长者？
二君曰： 尊官厚禄，世之所高也，贤才处之。
今所处非其地，故谓之卑。
言不信，行不验，取不当，故谓之汙。
夫卜筮者，世俗之所贱简也。

translation:
Since ancient times, only those who have received destiny can become kings, and the rise of kings does not depend on destiny through divination!
This situation was particularly prevalent in the Zhou Dynasty and can still be seen in the Qin Dynasty.
When the kings of the Han Dynasty came to Beijing to inherit the throne, they were also left to the fortune tellers.
As for the emergence of diviners, they began to appear since the rise of the Han Dynasty.
Lord Sima Ji is from Chu State.
He was doing divination in the east city of Chang'an.
At this time, Song Zhong was appointed as a doctor, and Jia Yi was appointed as a doctor. One day, the two of them went out to the court to take a bath and take a break. While walking, they discussed and learned the governance methods of the previous kings and saints, and extensively explored the world and human affairs. The two looked at each other and sighed.
Jia Yi said: I heard that ancient saints, if they were not officials in the court, must be among the ranks of diviners and doctors.
Now, I have seen the three princes, nine ministers and the sergeants and officials of the court, and I can know their talents, learning and character.
Let’s try to observe the soothsayer’s style.
The two of them went to the city in the same car and visited the divining restaurant.
It had just rained, and there were very few people on the road. Lord Sima Ji was sitting leisurely in the pavilion, with three or four disciples accompanying him, explaining the principles of heaven and earth, the movement of the sun and the moon, and the origin of good and bad luck in yin and yang.
The two doctors bowed twice to Lord Sima Ji.
Master Sima Ji looked at their appearance and saw that they seemed to be knowledgeable people, so he returned the courtesy and asked his disciples to lead them to sit down.
After sitting down, Sima Jizhu re-explained what he had said before, analyzed the origin and end of heaven and earth, the movement of the sun, moon and stars, distinguished the differences between benevolence and righteousness, listed the signs of good and bad luck, and talked about thousands of words, all of which were logical.
Song Zhong and Jia Yi were surprised and understood. They adjusted their crowns and belts, straightened their clothes, sat respectfully, and said, "I see your appearance and listen to your conversation. I have never seen anything like this in the world of today."
But why is your current status so low and why is your career so dirty?
Sima Jizhu laughed loudly and said: "It seems that the two doctors are people with Taoism. How could they say such superficial words and use such crude words now?"
What kind of person do you think a sage is?
Who do you think is a noble person?
Why do you regard me, an elder, as humble and dirty?
Song Zhong and Jia Yi said: Noble official positions and generous salaries are valued by the world, and worthy people enjoy them.
The position your husband is in now is not that kind of status, so he is considered to be in a lowly position.
What is said is not true, what is done is not effective, and what is taken is inappropriate, so it is dirty.
Diviners are despised by the world.

summary:
The role of divination in ancient China, particularly during the Zhou and Han dynasties. Kings historically received destiny without relying on divination, but by the Han dynasty, diviners had emerged. Lord Sima Ji, a diviner from Chu, practiced in Chang'an's east city. Two officials, Song Zhong and Jia Yi, visited him after discussing governance and saints. They questioned why Sima Ji, despite his wisdom, held a lowly position. Sima Ji defended divination, contrasting it with corrupt officials who exploit power for personal gain. He argued that diviners follow moral principles, aiding people through predictions and advice, unlike officials who engage in deceit and greed. He cited historical figures like Fuxi and King Wen of Zhou to highlight divination's value in governance. Sima Ji criticized the hypocrisy of those who judge diviners while ignoring their own ethical failings. He emphasized that diviners serve society by promoting virtue and preventing harm, often without recognition.

今夫卜者，导惑教愚也。
夫愚惑之人，岂能以一言而知之哉！
言不厌多。
故骐骥不能与罢驴为驷，而凤皇不与燕雀为群，而贤者亦不与不肖者同列。
故君子处卑隐以辟众，自匿以辟伦，微见德顺以除群害，以明天性，助上养下，多其功利，不求尊誉。
公之等喁喁者也，何知长者之道乎！
宋忠、贾谊忽而自失，芒乎无色，怅然噤口不能言。
於是摄衣而起，再拜而辞。
行洋洋也，出门仅能自上车，伏轼低头，卒不能出气。
居三日，宋忠见贾谊於殿门外，乃相引屏语相谓自叹曰： 道高益安，势高益危。
居赫赫之势，失身且有日矣。
夫卜而有不审，不见夺糈；为人主计而不审，身无所处。
此相去远矣，犹天冠地屦也。
此老子之所谓 无名者万物之始 也。
天地旷旷，物之熙熙，或安或危，莫知居之。
我与若，何足预彼哉！
彼久而愈安，虽曾氏之义未有以异也。
久之，宋忠使匈奴，不至而还，抵罪。
而贾谊为梁怀王傅，王堕马薨，谊不食，毒恨而死。

Today's diviners answer people's questions and educate people's ignorance.
How can those who are ignorant and confused become wise with just one sentence!
Therefore, never tire of talking.

You two are just casual commentators, how can you know the truth of the elders!
Song Zhong and Jia Yi were in a daze and seemed to be lost after hearing this. They were at a loss, looked melancholy, and could not speak.
So he got up and dressed, bowed twice and said goodbye to Lord Sima Ji.
The two of them walked without knowing the difference between east, west, north and south. When they went out, they had to get into the car by themselves, lying on the railing, not daring to raise their heads, and could never catch their breath.
Three days later, Song Zhong saw Jia Yi outside the palace gate, and took him away from everyone to talk about it. He said with emotion: The higher the morality, the safer it is, and the higher the power, the more dangerous it is.
In a prominent position, death is imminent.
Even if the divination does not work, it will not be deprived of the refined rice that it deserves; if the advice for the king is not thorough, there will be no place for him to stand.
The two are too different, just like the sky and the earth are completely different.
This is just as Lao Tzu said: Namelessness is the origin of all things in the world. Ha!
The sky and the earth are vast and boundless, and all things are prosperous and happy. Some are safe and some are dangerous. No one knows where he should live.
How qualified are you and I to comment on them?
The longer they live, the happier they will be. Even Zhuangzi's ideas are not different from this.
After a long time, Song Zhong went on a mission to the Xiongnu but returned before he arrived, and was therefore convicted of a crime.
Jia Yi was the tutor of King Huai of Liang. King Huai of Liang accidentally fell off his horse and died. Jia Yi took the blame and went on a hunger strike and died with hatred.
