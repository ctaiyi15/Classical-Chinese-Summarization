As crown prince tutor in the ninth year, Shusun Tong opposed Emperor Gao's plan to replace Crown Prince Liu Ying with Zhao Wang Liu Ruyi, citing historical disasters from such actions. He emphasized the prince's virtue and Empress Lu's shared hardships, threatening death if the change proceeded. Emperor Gao relented, especially after seeing four scholars supporting the prince. Under Emperor Xiaohui, Shusun Tong served as Taichang, formulating ancestral temple rituals.
