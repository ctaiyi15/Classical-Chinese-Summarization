哙还至荥阳，益食平阴二千户，以将军守广武。
一岁，项羽引而东。从高祖击项籍，下阳夏，虏楚周将军卒四千人。围项籍於陈，大破之。
屠胡陵。
项籍既死，汉王为帝，以哙坚守战有功，益食八百户。
从高帝攻反燕王臧荼，虏荼，定燕地。
楚王韩信反，哙从至陈，取信，定楚。
更赐爵列侯，与诸侯剖符，世世勿绝，食舞阳，号为舞阳侯，除前所食。
以将军从高祖攻反韩王信於代。
自霍人以往至云中，与绛侯等共定之，益食千五百户。
因击陈豨与曼丘臣军，战襄国，破柏人，先登，降定清河、常山凡二十七县，残东垣，迁为左丞相。
破得綦毋卬、尹潘军於无终、广昌。
破豨别将胡人王黄军於代南，因击韩信军於参合。
军所将卒斩韩信，破豨胡骑横谷，斩将军赵既，虏代丞相冯梁、守孙奋、大将王黄、将军、太仆解福等十人。
与诸将共定代乡邑七十三。
其後燕王卢绾反，哙以相国击卢绾，破其丞相抵蓟南，定燕地，凡县十八，乡邑五十一。
益食邑千三百户，定食舞阳五千四百户。
从，斩首百七十六级，虏二百八十八人。
别，破军七，下城五，定郡六，县五十二，得丞相一人，将军十二人，二千石已下至三百石十一人。
哙以吕后女弟吕须为妇，生子伉，故其比诸将最亲。
先黥布反时，高祖尝病甚，恶见人，卧禁中，诏户者无得入群臣。
群臣绛、灌等莫敢入。

translation:
When Fan Kuai returned to Xingyang, the king of Han added 2,000 households in Pingyin as his settlement, and asked him to guard Guangwu as a general.
A year later, Xiang Yu led his troops eastward, and Fan Kuai followed Gaozu to attack Xiang Ji, occupy Yangxia, and captured 4,000 soldiers of General Zhou of Chu. They surrounded Xiang Ji in Chen County and defeated him.
Bloodbath in Hu Ling.
After Xiang Yu's death, the King of Han became the emperor. Because of Fan Kuai's meritorious service in holding on to the city and attacking in battles, he granted 800 households a new enfeoffment.
Fan Kuai followed Gaozu to attack the rebellious King of Yan Zang Tu, captured Zang Tu, and pacified the Yan territory.
King Han Xin of Chu launched a rebellion. Fan Kuai followed Gaozu to Chen County, arrested Han Xin, and pacified Chu.
Emperor Gaozu changed Fan Kuai's title to that of a noble prince and established a trustworthy relationship with the princes, so that they could be passed down from generation to generation. Emperor Gaozu removed Fan Kuai's former food town and gave him food in Wuyang, naming him Marquis of Wuyang.
Later, Fan Kuai followed Gaozu to Daidi as a general to attack the rebellious King Xin of Han.
From the Huo people to Yunzhong, Fan Kuai, Jianghou Zhoubo and others jointly pacified it, so an additional 1,500 households were added.
Later, Fan Kuai led his troops to attack the armies of the rebels Chen Xi and Manqiuchen, and fought in Xiangguo. When he captured Bairen County, he took the lead in ascending the city. He also surrendered and pacified twenty-seven counties in Qinghe and Changshan counties, and destroyed Dongyuan County. He was promoted to Prime Minister Zuo for his merits.
In Wujing and Guangchang, he defeated the armies of Qi Wuyan and Yin Pan and captured them alive.
In the south of Daidi, the army led by the Hu general Wang Huang under Chen Xi was defeated. Then, he marched to join forces and attacked Han Wangxin's army. Fan Kuai's men killed Han Wangxin.
In Henggu, Chen Xi's Hu cavalry troops were defeated, general Zhao Ji was killed, and ten people including the Prime Minister Han Liang, the governor Sun Fen, the general Wang Huang, Tai Pu Xie and the general were captured.
Together with the generals, he pacified seventy-three villages and towns in Dai.
After that, King Lu Wan of Yan rebelled. Fan Kuai led the army to attack Lu Wan as prime minister, defeated the army led by Prime Minister Lu Wan in the south of Jixian County, and pacified a total of 18 counties and 51 townships in Yan.
So Emperor Gaozu gave Fan Kuai an additional 1,300 households, confirming that he would be the Marquis of Wuyang with a total of 5,400 households.
When Fan Kuai followed Gaozu in the battle, he beheaded 176 enemies and captured 188 enemy soldiers.
He led the troops to fight alone, defeated seven enemy armies, captured five cities, pacified six counties and fifty-two counties, and captured one enemy prime minister, 12 generals, and 11 officials ranging from 2,000 to 300 shi.
Because Fan Kuai married Empress Lu's sister Lu Xu and gave birth to a son, Mr. and Mrs. Fan, Gaozu was closer to Fan Kuai than other generals.
During the Yingbu rebellion, Gaozu was very ill and hated seeing people. He lay in the palace confinement and ordered the gatekeepers not to let the officials in to see him.
Among the officials, such as Jianghou Zhou Bo, Guan Ying and others did not dare to enter the palace.

summary:
Guan Ying, a silk merchant from Suiyang, joined Pei Gong and fought in numerous campaigns, including against Qin forces and Xiang Yu. He distinguished himself as a cavalry commander, defeating Chu forces and capturing key figures like Zhou Lan. After Xiang Yu's death, Guan Ying pursued and killed him, leading to his enfeoffment as Marquis of Yingyin with 5,000 households. He later suppressed rebellions by Zang Tu, Han Xin, Chen Xi, and Yingbu. During the Lü clan crisis, he feigned support but secretly allied with Zhou Bo to establish Emperor Wen, becoming Taiwei and later Prime Minister. He died in office, posthumously titled Yihou. His descendants held the title until it was revoked for bribery. Sima Qian notes that these figures, originally humble commoners, rose to prominence and established lasting legacies through their service to the Han dynasty.
