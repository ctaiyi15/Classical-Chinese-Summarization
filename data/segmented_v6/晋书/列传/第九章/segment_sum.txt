Feng You calmly served the emperor, discussed the history of Jin and Wei, and took advantage of the situation to persuade the emperor, saying that he could not entrust Zhang Hua with important tasks. The emperor did not say anything and no longer appointed him.
The details of the incident are recorded in the book "Zhang Hua Biography".
In the seventh year of Taikang's reign, Feng You fell ill and issued an edict to appoint him as a regular servant of Sanqi. He was given 200,000 yuan and a set of bed tents.
Died soon.
He has two sons: Feng Bo and Feng Xiong.

He opposed the attack on Wu but later followed Wang Jun into Moling. He advised the emperor to send Qi King Sima You to his feudal state, leading to Sima You's death. He hated Zhang Hua and prevented his promotion. He died in the seventh year of Taikang, leaving two sons, Feng Bo and Feng Xiong. The historian comments that Wang Chen's disloyalty and Wang Jun's violence brought disaster, while Xunjie and Xun Xu, despite their talents, contributed to court strife. Feng You's flattery led to Sima You's death and Jia's rise, causing endless civil unrest.
