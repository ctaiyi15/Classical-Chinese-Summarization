The text also records instances of fish falling on arsenal roofs, interpreted as a sign of war, and locust plagues, linked to the lord's greed and harshness. Pig disasters, such as wild boars entering military camps, are seen as portents of failure and emptiness. Black and yellow mists are recorded as signs of impending doom or usurpation. Landslides and ground collapses are interpreted as signs of the emperor's way collapsing and the people losing favor. The appearance of dragons in wells is considered a bad omen, indicating that the ruler's virtue is at odds with fate. Finally, the text records numerous human anomalies, such as women turning into men, the dead coming back to life, and births of monstrous children, all interpreted as signs of social and political disorder, with subordinates overtaking superiors.
