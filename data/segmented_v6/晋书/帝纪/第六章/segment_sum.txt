己巳，帝傅檄天下曰： 逆贼石勒，肆虐河朔，逋诛历载，游魂纵逸。
复遣凶党石季龙犬羊之众，越河南渡，纵其鸩毒。
平西将军祖逖帅众讨击，应时溃散。
今遣车骑将军，琅邪王裒等九军，锐卒三万，水陆四道，迳造贼场，受逖节度。

translation:
On the 18th, Emperor Yuan sent a message to the world: The traitor Shi Le carried out a large-scale robbery in the Heshuo area. After years of crusade, he was frightened and fled far away.
However, he also sent his evil party Shi Jilong to lead a group of dogs and sheep to cross the Henan River to invade and poison the people wantonly.
General Zu Ti of Pingxi led a crusade and was immediately defeated.
Now we dispatch nine armies, including General Chariot and Cavalry, King Lang Ya and King Sima Cai, with 30,000 elite soldiers, marching by land and water to attack the thief's lair and accept the sacrifice of Zu Ti.

summary:
Despite initial successes, the state's reliance on powerful generals led to internal conflicts, and Emperor Yuan's efforts to restore order ultimately failed, with later historians attributing the dynasty's decline to fate and flawed succession.
