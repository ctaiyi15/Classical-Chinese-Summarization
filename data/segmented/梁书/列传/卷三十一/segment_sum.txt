When Yuan Ang was five years old, his wet nurse hid him in Lushan Mountain. He left the mountain only when he was pardoned and moved to Jin'an. During the Yuanhui period, he was allowed to return to his hometown when he was fifteen years old.
At the beginning, Yuan Pinqi was defeated and his head was sent to the capital and hidden in the arsenal. It was only now sent back.
Yuan Ang cried and vomited blood and came back to life. His brother Yuan Yuzeng raised and educated him. Yuan Ang put on mourning clothes and built a house in the cemetery to live.
Later, he and Yuan Tuan went to visit his uncle, Situ Yuan Can. Yuan Can said to Yuan Tuan: "He can behave like this because he is young and alone, so he knows that his title and title have their own place."
In the early years of the Qi Dynasty, Wang Xing, the first champion of Ancheng, joined the army and moved to the post of chief warden, prince Sheren, and Wang Jianzhen, the military official's meritorious official.
Wang Jian was the Yin of Beijing at that time. He once introduced Yuan Ang alone in the back hall, pointed to the north hall and said to Yuan Ang: Your Majesty will definitely live here.
Lei moved to serve as Secretary Cheng and Huangmen Shilang.
Yuan Ang's real name was Qianli. During the Yongming Dynasty of the Qi Dynasty, Emperor Wu said to him: Angang, the horse of a thousand miles, your name contains this meaning. Now I will change your name to Yuan Ang, using the character Qianli.
He served as the chief historian of Poyang in Annan and the Duke of Xunyang.

summary:
summary line 0: Yuan Ang, courtesy name Qianli, was from Yangxia, Chen County. His grandfather and father were military officials in the Song Dynasty; his father was killed after supporting Prince Xun of Jin'an. At age five, Yuan Ang was hidden in Lushan by his wet nurse, returning home at fifteen after a pardon. He mourned his father deeply, vomiting blood and building a hut by the grave. Raised by his brother Yuan Yuzeng, he later served in various official posts under the Qi Dynasty, including Secretary Cheng and Huangmen Shilang. Emperor Wu of Qi renamed him Yuan Ang, using his courtesy name Qianli.
