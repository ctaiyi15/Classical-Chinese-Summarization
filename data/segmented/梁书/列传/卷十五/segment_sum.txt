translation: 
He put on his court clothes, walked out of Dongye Gate, got in his car, and returned to his residence.
One day later, Wang Jian was appointed as the servant to untie the jade seal.
Soon Emperor Wu asked Emperor Gao to kill Xie Bei.
Emperor Gao said: "Killing him will bring success to his reputation. He should be tolerated and excluded from the law."
So he was dismissed from office and stayed at home.
In the first year of Yongming Dynasty, Xie Chu was summoned from his home to be appointed as a regular servant of Tongzhi Sanqi. He was successively transferred to the position of servant and received the doctor of Guozi.
In the fifth year, he was appointed as the champion general and the prefect of Yixing, and received an additional salary of two thousand dan.
When he was in office in the county, he did not check the chores and handed over everything to the rules and regulations. He said: "I can't be a low-level official like the master, I can only be a prefect."
After working for three years, he was appointed as the Du Guan Shangshu and Zhongshu Ling.
In the first year of Longchang, he wrote a poem and accepted the post of master of King Xin'an. Xie Zhu did not accept the offer and insisted on applying for a job abroad.
So he became the general who conquered the captives and the governor of Wuxing. He took up his post after being summoned.
When Emperor Ming was sent to seek his succession to the throne, all the old ministers of the imperial court were summoned to discuss strategies.
Xie Li thought about stopping in his heart, but in fact it was to avoid trouble.
His younger brother Xie Man was the Minister of the Ministry of Personnel at that time.
When Xie Li came to the county, he gave Xie a lot of dendrobium wine and left a note saying: You can drink as much as you can, but don't get involved in personnel disputes.
When Xie Bei was in office in the county, he often ignored official affairs but devoted himself to amassing wealth. People ridiculed him, but he ignored him.
In the fourth year of Jianwu's reign, Xia Shao summoned Xie Bei to serve as Shizhong and Zhongshuling. He submitted a memorial to the emperor and refused to accept Shao's request.
He sent his disciples back to the capital, but he and his mother stayed behind and built a house in the west city of the county.

summary:
summary line 1: During Qi Emperor Gao's reign, Xie Bei was among the "four friends" of the emperor, serving as chief historian and later Shizhong. When Emperor Gao sought to usurp the throne, Xie Bei expressed reluctance, citing historical precedents, which displeased the emperor. He was eventually dismissed and stayed at home. In the Yongming era, he was recalled to serve as Tongzhi Sanqi Changshi and later as champion general and prefect of Yixing, where he neglected official duties but amassed wealth. He refused several appointments, including Shizhong and Zhongshuling, preferring to avoid political turmoil.
