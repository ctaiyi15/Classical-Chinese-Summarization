summary:
summary line 0: Emperor Gaozu had eight sons by various consorts, including Prince Zhaoming, Emperor Taizong Jianwen, and others. Among them, Xiao Ji, the fourth son, was titled King of Nankang Jian. At age seven, he demonstrated intelligence by catching a subordinate altering documents. He held various military and civil posts, including governor of South Xuzhou and Jiangzhou, and was praised for his frugality and personal handling of affairs. After his mother's death, he mourned strictly and insisted on resigning his post. He died in office at twenty-five, leaving behind substantial unnamed funds in the treasury. His son Xiao Hui succeeded him, showing early intelligence and literary talent. During the Hou Jing rebellion, Hui initially resisted but eventually surrendered to Hou Jing after the capital fell. He secretly plotted to restore the Liang dynasty but was betrayed and killed, along with his brother Xiao Congli, who was captured and executed at twenty-one.
