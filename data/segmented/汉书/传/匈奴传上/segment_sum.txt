头曼以为壮，令将万骑。
冒顿乃作鸣镝，习勒其骑射，令曰： 鸣镝所射而不悉射者斩。
行猎兽，有不射鸣镝所射辄斩之。
已而，冒顿以鸣镝自射善马，左右或莫敢射，冒顿立斩之。
居顷之，复以鸣镝自射其爱妻，左右或颇恐，不敢射，复斩之。
顷之，冒顿出猎，以鸣镝射单于善马，左右皆射之。
于是冒顿知其左右可用，从其父单于头曼猎，以鸣镝射头曼，其左右皆随鸣镝而射杀头曼，尽诛其后母与弟及大臣不听从者。
于是冒顿自立为单于。
冒顿既立，时东胡强，闻冒顿杀父自立，乃使使谓冒顿曰： 欲得头曼时号千里马。
冒顿问群臣，群臣皆曰： 此匈奴宝马也，勿予。
冒顿曰： 奈何与人邻国爱一马乎？
遂与之。
顷之，东胡以为冒顿畏之，使使谓冒顿曰： 欲得单于一阏氏。
冒顿复问左右，左右皆怒曰： 东胡无道，乃求阏氏！

The Xiongnu Chanyu was named Touman. Touman could not resist Qin, so he moved north.
More than ten years later, Meng Tian died. The descendants of the original princes rebelled against the Qin State. The Central Plains area was in chaos. Those who had been exiled by the Qin Dynasty to guard the border left. As a result, the Xiongnu's situation improved and they slowly crossed the Yellow River. In the south, they were adjacent to the Central Plains with their original border fortress. Shan Yuli had a prince named Maodun.
Later, the Chanyu's favored Ban clan gave birth to a son. Chanyu Touman wanted to depose the crown prince Mao Dun and make his younger son the crown prince, so he sent Mao Dun to Yuezhi as a hostage.
After Maodun arrived at Yuezhi and became a hostage, Touman Chanyu immediately sent troops to attack Yuezhi.
Yuezhi wanted to kill Mao Dun, so Mao Dun stole Yuezhi's good horse and rode it back to the Huns.
Touman Chanyu thought that Mao Dun was brave, so he asked him to lead 10,000 cavalry.
Mao Dun then made the Ming Dy, or ringing arrow, and trained and constrained his cavalry in archery. He ordered them to say: If anyone does not follow and shoot the thing shot by my Ming Dy, he will be killed.
Mao Dun led his men out for hunting. Anyone who did not follow the target shot by She Ming Dy was immediately killed.
Soon, Mao Dun shot his own good horse with Wu Dy. Some people around him did not dare to follow suit, so Mao Dun immediately killed these people.
A few days later, Mao Dun himself shot his beloved wife with a hammer. Some people around him were very frightened and did not dare to shoot arrows. Mao Dun killed these people again.
Soon, Mao Dun led his men out for hunting and shot Shanyu's fine horse with a wu-dy, and everyone around him followed suit.
So Mao Dun knew that the people around him could be used for his own purposes. When he went hunting with his father Touman Chanyu, he shot Touman with a hammer. The people around him followed the lead and shot Touman, killing the Chanyu. Mao Dun also killed his stepmother, his younger brother, and all the ministers who disobeyed him.
So Maodun established himself as Chanyu.
When Mao Dun established himself as a chanyu, Dong Hu was very powerful. When Cambodian Hu heard that Mao Dun killed his father and established himself, he sent an envoy to Mao Dun and said: Dong Hu wants to get Tou Man, a good horse known as a thousand-mile horse.
Mao Dun asked the ministers, and they all said: "This is the Xiongnu's BMW and cannot be given to them."
Mao Dun said: How can we be neighbors and yet pity a horse?
So he gave the thousand-mile horse to Donghu.
Soon, Shu Hu thought that Mao Dun was afraid of him, so he sent an envoy to Mao Dun and said: Shu Hu wants to get Shan Yu, a Ban clan.
Mao Dun asked the ministers again, and the people around him were very angry and said: "The Shu Hu people are unreasonable and want Banshi from us!"
