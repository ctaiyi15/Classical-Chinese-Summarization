During Emperor Wu’s reign, he presented elegant music and answered over thirty questions from the court with clarity and reason. After reigning for twenty-six years, he died, praised by officials for his uprightness, kindness, and frugality. His descendants ruled Hejian for generations, though later kings like Liu Yuan faced scandal and deposition for cruelty and misconduct, leading to the kingdom’s abolition and restoration under Emperor Cheng.
