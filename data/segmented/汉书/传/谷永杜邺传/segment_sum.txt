臣材朽学浅，不通政事。
translation: 
Gu Yong, named Ziyun, was from Chang'an.
His father, Gu Ji, served as Sima of the Wei Dynasty. As an envoy, he sent Shanyu Zhizhi's servant back to his country, but was killed by Zhizhi. This incident was recorded in the "Chen Tang Biography".
Gu Yong wrote a short history of Chang'an when he was young, and later studied the classics extensively.
During the reign of Emperor Jianzhao, Fan Yanshou, the imperial censor, heard that he had outstanding talents and appointed him as his subordinate. Later, he recommended him as Taichang Cheng. He wrote many memorials to discuss political gains and losses.
In the winter of the third year of Jianshi's reign, a solar eclipse and an earthquake occurred on the same day. The emperor ordered the selection of scholars who were upright, dared to speak out, and could try their best to persuade. Tai Chang Yangcheng Marquis Liu Qingji recommended Gu Yong to wait for the imperial edict.
Gu Yong replied:
Your Majesty has the purest moral character of the most sage. He is worried about the strange phenomena warned by heaven and earth. He organizes his own state affairs, inquires and adopts the suggestions of the ministers, and issues wise orders, asking the officials to recommend people who dare to speak out. He takes the time to summon them to explore the origins of the changes, to find the reasons for the heaven's punishment, so that we scholars can come to the court of the sage to accept the inquiry of the sage.
I am not very talented and have little knowledge, and I am not familiar with political affairs.
