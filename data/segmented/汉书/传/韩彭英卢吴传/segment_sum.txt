韩信，淮阴人也。
家贫无行，不得推择为吏，又不能治生为商贾，常从人寄食。
其母死无以葬，乃行营高燥地，令傍可置万家者。

食时信往，不为具食。
信亦知其意，自绝去。
至城下钓，有一漂母哀之，饭信，意漂数十日。
信谓漂母曰： 吾必重报母。
母怒曰： 大丈夫不能自食，吾哀王孙而进食，岂望报乎！
淮阴少年又侮信曰： 虽长大，好带刀剑，怯耳。
众辱信曰： 能死，刺我；不能，出胯下。
于是信孰视，俯出跨下。
一市皆笑信，以为怯。
translation: 
Han Xin was born in Huaiyin County.
The family is poor and has no good character. He cannot be elected to be an official, and he cannot do business to make a living. He often lives in other people's homes and eats nothing.
His mother passed away and was too poor to bury her, so he found a high, dry place with spacious surroundings as a cemetery so that tens of thousands of families could be placed next to the grave in the future.
Han Xin once went to the countryside to eat at the pavilion chief's house in Nanchang. The pavilion chief's wife hated him so much that she got up very early to prepare the meal and put it on the bed to eat.
When it was breakfast time, Han Xin went and no meal was prepared for him.
Han Xin understood her intention and left her, never to interact with her again.
Han Xin once went fishing in the city. An old woman was rinsing silk cotton wool. She took pity on him and gave him food. This continued for dozens of days until the rinsing was completed.
Han Xin said gratefully to his old mother: I will definitely repay you heavily in the future.
The old man was very angry after hearing this and said, "You are a man and can't support yourself. I gave you food out of pity for you, a young man. Do I want you to repay me?"
A young man in Huaiyin City bullied Han Xin and said: Although you are tall and like to wear swords, you are actually very timid.
He insulted Han Xin in public and said: If you are not afraid of death, stab me with a sword; if you are afraid of death, get under my crotch.
So Han Xin stared at the young man carefully, then bent down and crawled under his crotch.
People watching the excitement on the street laughed at Han Xin and thought he was cowardly.

translation: 
When Xiang Liang led his army across the Huai River to the north, Han Xin took his sword and went to him, and became an unknown soldier under Xiang Liang.
After Xiang Liang failed, he returned to Xiang Yu, who made him a doctor.
Han Xin repeatedly offered advice to Xiang Yu, but Xiang Yu did not adopt it.
Liu Bang, the king of Han, entered Hanzhong, and Han Xin escaped from the Chu army and joined the king of Han. At the beginning, he was still unknown and worked as a granary management clerk.
Later, he was sentenced to death for breaking the law. Thirteen of his accomplices were beheaded. When it was Han Xin's turn, Han Xin looked up and happened to see Teng Gong Xia Houying, so he asked: Doesn't the King of Han want to unify the world?
Why kill the strong man?
Teng Gong felt that Han Xin's words were unusual and he looked very powerful, so he did not kill him.
After talking to Han Xin, he was very happy and reported his situation to the King of Han.
The King of Han appointed Han Xin as the Commander-in-Chief of Su Su, but did not reuse him.
Han Xin talked with Xiao He many times, and Xiao He appreciated his talent very much.
When the Han army arrived at Nanzheng, dozens of generals escaped halfway.
Han Xin considered that Xiao He and others had recommended him to the King of Han several times, but he still could not be reused, so he ran away.
Xiao He heard that Han Xin had escaped. He had no time to report to the king of Han, so he went to chase him himself.
Someone reported to the King of Han: Prime Minister Xiao He has escaped.
The king of Han was furious, as if he had lost his left and right hands.
Two days later, Xiao He came to see the King of Han.
The King of Han was both angry and happy, and scolded Xiao He: "Why did you run away?"
Xiao He replied: How dare I run away? I am chasing those who run away.

summary:
summary line 1: Han Xin joined Xiang Liang's army as a soldier, then served Xiang Yu as a郎中, offering advice that was ignored. He later defected to Liu Bang, King of Han, as a仓管员. Sentenced to death for a crime, he appealed to Teng Gong Xia Houying, who spared him after a conversation. Liu Bang made him a治粟都尉 but didn't value him. Han Xin fled when not promoted, but Xiao He chased him back. Xiao He convinced Liu Bang to formally appoint Han Xin as a大将, with a ceremony. Liu Bang asked Han Xin for strategies. Han Xin analyzed Xiang Yu's weaknesses—his inability to delegate, stinginess with rewards, and cruelty—contrasting Liu Bang's potential to win hearts by appointing capable men, rewarding merit, and respecting the people's wishes. He predicted easy conquest of the Three Qin states. Liu Bang followed his plan, marching east through Chencang to pacify the region.
