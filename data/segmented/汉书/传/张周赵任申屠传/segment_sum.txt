张苍，阳武人也，好书律历。
秦时为御史，主柱下方书。
有罪，亡归。
及沛公略地过阳武，苍以客从攻南阳。
苍当斩，解衣伏质，身长大，肥白如瓠，时王陵见而怪其美士，乃言沛公，赦勿斩。
遂西入武关，至咸阳。
沛公立为汉王，入汉中，还定三秦。
陈馀击走常山王张耳，耳归汉。汉以苍为常山守。
从韩信击赵，苍得陈馀。
赵地已平，汉王以苍为代相，备边冠。
已而徙为赵相，相赵王耳。
耳卒，相其子敖。
复徙相代。
燕王臧荼反，苍以代相从攻荼有功，封为北平侯，食邑千二百户。
迁为计相，一月，更以列侯为主计四岁。
是时，萧何为相国，而苍乃自秦时为柱下御史，明习天下图书计籍，又善用算律历，故令苍以列侯居相府，领主郡国上计者。
黥布反，汉立皇子长为淮南王，而苍相之。
translation: 
Zhang Cang, a native of Yangwu, Henan, was fond of writing, music and calendars.
In the Qin Dynasty, he served as censor and was in charge of household registration albums all over the world.
He fled back to his hometown because he broke the law.
When Pei Gong was marching through Yangwu, Zhang Cang followed Pei Gong as a guest to attack Nanyang.
When Zhang Cang was about to be executed, he took off his clothes and leaned on the chopping block of the torture instrument, revealing his tall figure and fat muscles as white as gourds. At that time, Wang Ling saw Zhang Cang and was very surprised by this beauty, so he told Pei Gong that he would not be punished by beheading if he was pardoned.
So he entered Wuguan in the west and arrived in Xianyang.
Pei Gong became the king of Han, entered Hanzhong, and returned to the army to pacify the Sanqin area.
Chen Yu drove away Zhang Er, king of Changshan, and Zhang Er defected to the king of Han. The king of Han appointed Zhang Cang as the governor of Changshan County.
Following Han Xin to attack Zhao, Zhang Cang captured Chen Yu.
After the Zhao area was pacified, the King of Han appointed Zhang Cang as acting prime minister to guard against border bandits.
Soon he was moved to the state of Zhao and became the prime minister of Zhao Wang Zhang Er.
Zhang Er died, and Zhang Cang became his son Zhang Ao, the prime minister.
Later, he was appointed as the Prime Minister of Daiguo.
King Zang Tu of Yan rebelled, and Zhang Cang served as the Prime Minister and accompanied the army to attack Zang Lai. He was granted the title of Marquis of Beiping and was granted a settlement of 1,200 households.
Zhang Cang changed his plan, and one month later, he made Liehou the leader for four years.
At that time, Xiao He was appointed as the prime minister, and Zhang Cang had been the censor under the pillar since the Qin Dynasty. He was familiar with the world's atlas and household registration, and was good at calculating the calendar. Therefore, Zhang Cang was appointed to serve in the prime minister's mansion as a lieutenant, in charge of the books sent by the county.
Ying Bu rebelled, and the Han Dynasty established the prince Liu Chang as the King of Huainan, and Zhang Cang as the Prime Minister of Huainan.

summary:
summary line 0: Zhang Cang, from Yangwu, Henan, was a scholar of writing, music, and calendars. He served as a censor under the Qin Dynasty, managing household registries, but fled home after breaking the law. He later joined Pei Gong (Liu Bang) as a guest, and was spared execution after Wang Ling admired his physique. He followed Pei Gong to Xianyang, served as governor of Changshan, and helped capture Chen Yu. He became prime minister of Zhao and Dai, and was made Marquis of Beiping for suppressing a rebellion. As censor-in-chief, he used his expertise in calendars and registries to serve in the prime minister's office. He later became prime minister of Huainan and then censor-in-chief. He corrected the calendar, adopting the Qin system with October as the new year, and advocated for water virtue. He served Wang Ling like a father after being saved by him. He was dismissed after opposing Gongsun Chen's proposal for Han's earth virtue, which was later confirmed by a yellow dragon sighting. He died around age 100, leaving writings on yin-yang and calendars.

summary:
summary line 1: Zhou Chang, from Pei, served as a soldier under Pei Gong with his cousin Zhou Ke. Zhou Ke died defending Xingyang against Xiang Yu, and Zhou Chang became censor-in-chief. He was known for his bluntness, once calling Emperor Gaozu a tyrant. He opposed deposing the crown prince, earning gratitude from Empress Lü. He was appointed prime minister of Zhao to protect Prince Ruyi, despite his reluctance. After Gaozu's death, Empress Lü summoned and killed Ruyi; Zhou Chang died of illness three years later. His descendants lost the marquisate.
