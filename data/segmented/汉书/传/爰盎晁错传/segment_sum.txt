summary:
summary line 4: Chao Cuo, a native of Yingchuan, studied Legalist philosophy under Zhang Hui and became a doctor under Emperor Wen. He was sent to study the *Book of Documents* from Fu Sheng and later served as a tutor to the crown prince, earning the nickname "think tank." Chao Cuo wrote extensively on military strategy, advocating for selecting good generals, training soldiers, and using superior weapons. He proposed using surrendered barbarians and border settlers to defend against the Xiongnu, emphasizing the importance of terrain and equipment. He also criticized the Qin dynasty's harsh policies and recommended settling families in border fortresses with proper housing, tools, and incentives.
