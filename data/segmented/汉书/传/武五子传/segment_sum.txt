When he was twenty years old, he was crowned and lived in the Tai Palace. Emperor Wu of the Han Dynasty built Bowang Garden for him, allowing him to interact with guests and act according to his interests. As a result, many heresies came to him as tribute.
In the fourth year of Yuanding's reign, he married Shi Liangdi and gave birth to his son Liu Jin, who was called the grandson of Emperor Shi.
In the later years of Emperor Wu of the Han Dynasty, the imperial guard fell out of favor and Jiang Chong was reused. Jiang Chong had grudges against the prince and the Wei family, and was worried that the prince would execute him after Emperor Wu of the Han Dynasty. At this time, a witchcraft case occurred, and Jiang Chong took advantage of this to seek personal revenge.
