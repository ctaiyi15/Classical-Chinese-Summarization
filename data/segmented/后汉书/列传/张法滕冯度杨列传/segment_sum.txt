translation: 
◆Zhang Zongchuan, also known as Zhang Zongzi Zhujun, was born in Luyang, Nanyang.
During the reign of Wang Mang, he served as the assistant of Yangquan Township in this county.

When Deng Yu's army arrived at the city, the Red Eyebrow Army was approaching. Deng Yu thought that the city was not strong enough to defend and wanted to lead his troops to defend the city. However, everyone was afraid of being chased by thieves and afraid of being a rearguard.
Deng Yu then wrote the names of the generals on the bamboo slips in order, put them randomly in the box, and asked everyone to draw lots.
Zhang Zongdu refused to smoke. He said: Life and death are determined by fate. Is Zhang Zong a person who will give up when it is difficult? Deng Yu sighed and said: "The general has old relatives and weak sons in the camp, why don't you consider it?" Zhang Zong said: I heard that if a soldier tries his best, a hundred people cannot stop him; if ten thousand soldiers are willing to die, they can dominate the world.
Zhang Zongjin has thousands of troops and can withstand the power of the army. Why should he immediately expect defeat? So I stayed and refused.
After all the camps had led their troops and set off, Zhang Zong urged his soldiers to build strongholds and defend them without retreating.
When Deng Yu arrived at Qianxian County, he heard people commenting: "With General Zhang's manpower, trying to stop an army of millions is like throwing Xiaoxue into boiling water. No matter how hard he tries, he can't do it." So he sent 2,000 infantry and cavalry back to greet Zhang Zong.
Zhang Zong led his troops to set out, but the Red Eyebrow Army hurriedly arrived. Zhang Zong fought with him, drove them back, and then returned to the camp. The generals admired his bravery.
After returning to Chang'an, Zhang Zong led his warriors into the city at night to attack Chimei. He was shot between the arms by the enemy.
When Deng Yu was recalled to the capital, Guangwu appointed Zhang Zong as the capital's auxiliary captain. He led the sudden cavalry and conquered the west general Feng Yi to attack the various camps in Guanzhong. After defeating them, he was promoted to the captain of Henan.
