◆Biography of Wu You, courtesy name Ji Ying, a native of Changyuan County, Chenliu County.
His father, Wu Hui, was the governor of Nanhai.
When Wu You was twelve years old, he followed his father to the official residence.
Wu Hui wanted to use bamboo slips to write scriptures, but Wu You dissuaded him and said: "Now that you have passed Wuling, you will see the seaside in the distance. The customs here are simple, but there are many rare and rare things. The imperial court is suspicious of them above, and the powerful and powerful people below want to possess them."
If your book is finished, it will need many vehicles to carry it.
Once upon a time, Ma Yuan was falsely accused of transporting coix back home, saying that all he transported were pearls and rhinoceros. Wang Yang was good at carriages and horses, and was well-dressed. He was very good at traveling, but he could not carry anything more than a bag, and others said he could make gold.
Therefore, the place of suspicion was the most cautious place for ancient sages. After hearing what he said, Wu Hui stopped writing scriptures, stroked Wu You's back and said, "My surname is Wu, and I have had a son for generations!" At the age of twenty, Wu You's father died, and the family did not have a single stone of food savings, but he did not accept gifts from others.
He often herds pigs in the Changyuan swamp and hums scriptures.
When he met his father's friend, he was asked: You are the son of Erqianshi but you are doing such a despicable job. Even if you don't feel ashamed, how can you be worthy of your underground father? Wu You said thank you and herded the pigs as before.
