桓帝时，以冉为莱芜长，遭母忧，不到官。
后辟太尉府，以狷急不能从俗，常佩韦于朝。
议者欲以为侍御史，因遁身逃命于梁沛之间，徒行敝服，卖卜于市。
translation: 
He went to Nanyang and studied under Fan Ying.
He traveled to Sanfu again and studied the art of menstruation with Ma Rong. After a few years, he returned.
Fan Ran is a maverick, and what he does often goes against the times and is unconventional. I often envy Liang Boluan and Min Zhongshu for their conduct.
He was very close to Li Gu of Hanzhong and Wang Huan of Hanoi. But he despises Jia Weijie, Guo Linzong and others.
Wang Huan later served as the magistrate of Kaocheng, and the county bordered Waihuang. He wrote to Fan Ran many times, asking him to go, but Fan Ran did not go.
Wang Huan was promoted to the Grand Administrator of Hanyang. When he was about to leave, Fan Rancai and his younger brother Fan Xie carried ale on foot and set up an altar beside the road to wait for Wang Huan.
When Fan Ran saw Wang Huan's entourage in his car, there was an endless stream of people, so he didn't tell Wang Huan and just argued with his younger brother on the roadside.
Wang Huan heard that it was Fan Ran's voice and immediately got out of the car to meet him.
Wang Huan said: "We are in a hurry on the road. This is not the place to talk about our farewell feelings. You can come with me to the front pavilion to rest and have a good talk."
Fan Ran said: You were in Kaocheng, and I wanted to go there, but because you were poor and lowly, you didn't dare to climb high, so you cut yourself off from wealthy friends.
Now you have traveled thousands of miles and there is no chance to meet you, so my brother and I are waiting for you here to say goodbye to you.
If I go with you, it will attract ridicule from Mu Gui.
Then he got up and said goodbye, brushed his clothes and left.
Wang Huan kept looking at Fan Ran until he could no longer see him, but Fan Ran left without looking back.
During the reign of Emperor Huan, Fan Ran was appointed as the chief of Laiwu, but his mother died and he could not take up the post.
Later, he was recruited to the Taiwei Mansion. Because he was too radical and could not follow the secular rules, he often encouraged himself in the court.
Public opinion wanted him to serve as an official, so he fled in hiding between Liang and Pei. He walked on the road with no clothes and sold divination in the market.
