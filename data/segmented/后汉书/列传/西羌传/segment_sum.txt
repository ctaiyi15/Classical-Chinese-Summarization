summary:
summary line 0: The Xiqiang people, originally from Sanmiao, a branch of the Jiang surname, lived in a region near Nanyue, stretching from the Cizhi River to the borders of Shu and Han in the south and Shanshan and Cheshi in the northwest. They were nomadic, migrating with water and grass, relying primarily on livestock rather than grain. Their society lacked fixed clans, with tribes named after paternal or maternal lines. After twelve generations, intermarriage occurred, and customs included marrying stepmothers after a father's death and widows after an elder brother's death, leading to rapid population growth. They had no central ruler; power determined leadership, with violence and plunder common. Their soldiers excelled in valley combat but not on plains, valued death in battle over illness, and endured harsh conditions.
