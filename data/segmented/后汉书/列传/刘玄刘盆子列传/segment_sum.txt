I am afraid that there will be no benefit to me even if I die, so I am willing to abdicate and become a common man, so that I can seek more wise and wise people as my emperor. Please review me. Fan Chong and others apologized and said, "This is all our fault." Liu Gong insisted again.
Some people said: Is this your business? Liu Gong was frightened and stood up and left.
Liu Penzi then got out of bed and took off his seal ribbon, kowtowed and said: Now that the emperor has been appointed, he is still a thief as before.
The officials and the people were often robbed when they contributed goods. This kind of scandal spread everywhere, and everyone was resentful, so they no longer trusted or yearned for us.
These are all the consequences of being disrespectful. I beg to save my life and make way for a sage.
If you must kill me to satisfy your guilt, I cannot escape. I sincerely hope that you all can sympathize with me and take pity on me.
So he burst into tears and lamented. Fan Chong and other hundreds of people present felt pity for him, so they all left their seats and kowtowed, saying: "I have failed your majesty. From now on, I will not dare to indulge again." So they picked up the basin together and tied a seal ribbon on him.
Liu Penzi called for help.
After the banquet, everyone closed their camps and defended themselves. The three assistants unanimously praised the emperor as wise.
