会征尚还，以骑都尉段禧为都护，西域长史赵博为骑都尉。
禧、博守它乾城。
它乾城小，以为不可固，乃谲说龟兹王白霸，欲入共保其城，白霸许之。
吏人固谏，白霸不听，懂既入，遣将急迎禧、博，合军八九千人。
龟兹吏人并叛其王，而与温宿、姑墨数万兵反，共围城。

The next year, Ban Yong wrote a letter requesting to attack Yuan and Meng, so the imperial court sent Zhang Lang, the governor of Dunhuang, to lead 3,000 soldiers from Hexi County to cooperate with Ban Yong.
Ban Yong mobilized more than 40,000 soldiers from various countries and divided them into two groups to attack.
Ban Yong walked from the south road, Zhang Lang walked from the north road, and agreed to meet at Yanqi on a date.
However, Zhang Lang was guilty first and wanted to make atonement, so he rushed to Jue Liguan first. Sima was sent to lead troops to attack the front station and captured more than 2,000 enemy people.
Yuan Meng was afraid of beheading, so he sent an envoy to ask for surrender. Zhang Lang went directly to Yanqi and surrendered.
Yuan Meng actually refused to be tied up in person, and only sent his son to the court to pay tribute.
Zhang Lang was pardoned from the death penalty.
Because Ban Yong did not arrive as scheduled, he was convicted and imprisoned. He was pardoned soon after, and later died at home.
◆In the biography of Liang Jin, Liang Jin was named Bowei and was a native of Yiju in the north.
His father, Liang Xi, served successively as governor of the state.
In the first year of Yongyuan, Dou Xian, the general of chariots and cavalry, went to conquer the Xiongnu. Liang Xi was granted the title of Army Sima, and he was asked to bring gold and silk as an envoy to Bei Chanyu to promote the country's might and virtue. More than ten thousand people joined him.
Later, because he violated Dou Xian's will, he was exiled to Wuwei, and the prefect of Wuwei ordered to kill him.
After the Dou family was destroyed, Emperor He knew that Liang Xi had been unjustly killed by Dou Xian, so he summoned him as a doctor.
Liang Jin was courageous and generous in his fame.
At first, he was the Sima of Deng Hong, the chariot and cavalry general. He was promoted again and became the deputy captain of the Western Regions in the first year of Yanping.
When Liang Jin went to Hexi, he encountered the rebellion of various countries in the Western Regions. He attacked the capital and protected Shang Yu in Shule.
Ren Shang wrote a letter asking for help, and the court summoned Commander Liang Jin to lead five thousand Qiang and Hu cavalry from the four counties of Hexi to run to the rescue. However, Liang Jin did not arrive and Ren Shang had already rescued the siege.
Just when Ren Shang was called back, the imperial court appointed Duan Xi, the Cavalry Commander, as the Protector, and Zhao Bo, the chief historian of the Western Regions, as the Cavalry Commander.
Duan and Zhao guarded Qiancheng.
Qiancheng was very small, and Liang Jin thought it was not very strong, so he cunningly persuaded Bai Ba, the king of Qiuci, to come in and protect the city together. Bai Ba agreed.
The officials tried to dissuade him, but Bai Ba refused to listen. After Liang Jin entered the city, he sent generals to greet Duan Xi and Zhao Bo, with a total of eight or nine thousand sergeants.
The officials of Qiuci all rebelled against their king, and they rebelled with tens of thousands of soldiers from Wensu and Gumo, and together they surrounded the city.
