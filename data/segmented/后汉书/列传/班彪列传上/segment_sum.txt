Wei Xiao disagreed, citing the Qin collapse as an unpredictable event. Ban Biao, disturbed by Wei Xiao's arrogance, wrote "On the King's Mandate" to argue that Han virtue succeeded Tang Yao, but Wei Xiao remained unmoved, so Ban Biao fled to Hexi.
