卓曰： 为者则己，有者则士。
乃悉分与吏兵，无所留。
稍迁西域戊己校尉，坐事免。

translation: 
Dong Zhuo, courtesy name Zhongying, was from Lintao, Longxi.
He has a rough and scheming temperament.
When he was young, he traveled among the Qiang and tried his best to make friends with the leaders.
Later, when he returned home to farm, some of the leaders came to join him. Dong Zhuo slaughtered cattle for them and had banquets with them. The leaders thanked him for his kindness and collected the remaining heads of various livestock as gifts to him after returning. From then on, he became famous for his bravery and chivalry.
The soldiers and horses who were appointed as state guards once patrolled and guarded under the fortress.
Dong Zhuo has extraordinary physical strength. He wears two bow boxes and shoots them left and right. The Qiang and Hu people are afraid of him.
In the last years of Emperor Huan's reign, as a son of a good family in six counties, he was appointed Yulin Lang, and followed Zhang Huan, the general of Zhonglang, as a military commander. Together they went to attack the rebellious Qiang people in Hanyang. After defeating them, he was named Langzhong and was rewarded with nine thousand bundles of silk.
Dong Zhuo said: I am the one who records the merits, and the soldiers are the ones who make the merits.
So they were all distributed to the soldiers, leaving nothing behind.
He was gradually promoted to the rank of captain of Wuji School in the Western Regions, but was dismissed for some reasons.
Later he became the governor of Bingzhou and the governor of Hedong.
In the first year of Zhongping, he was appointed as Shu Zhonglang General. With integrity, he went to Xiaquyang to attack Zhangjiao on behalf of Lu Zhi. He was defeated and punished.

summary:
summary line 0: Dong Zhuo, a man of rough and scheming temperament from Lintao, Longxi, rose to prominence through his interactions with Qiang leaders, distributing rewards to soldiers and gaining a reputation for bravery. He served as a military commander under Zhang Huan, defeating rebellious Qiang forces, and was later appointed to various posts, including Governor of Bingzhou and Hedong. During the Zhongping era, he was tasked with suppressing rebellions, including those led by Bian Zhang and Han Sui, but faced defeats. Despite setbacks, he managed to preserve his army and was granted the title of Marquis of Lianxiang.

Only Shangshu Lu Zhi said: In the past, after Taijia came to the throne, King Lu Yi had more than a thousand sins, so there was something about abolishing the throne.
The current emperor is still young and his behavior is not inappropriate and cannot be compared with previous cases.
Dong Zhuo was furious and left the table.
The next day, he summoned all the officials in the front hall of Chongde, and then threatened the Queen Mother, asking her to issue an edict to depose the young emperor.
Said: During the period of mourning for the late emperor, the emperor did not have the mentality of a son, and did not behave like a monarch. Now he is deposed as King Hongnong.
So he established King Chenliu and created Emperor Xian.
He also pointed out that the Queen Mother persecuted the Queen Mother Yongle, causing her to die of depression. She violated the etiquette of mother-in-law and daughter-in-law, and had no filial qualities. She moved her to Yong'an Palace, where she was subsequently murdered and died.
Dong Zhuo was promoted to Taiwei, acting as the agent for the affairs of the former general. He added the certificate of carriage and horse riding at the post station during the Xijie Festival and the warriors with axes and iron tigers, and was granted the title of County Lord.
Dong Zhuo, together with Situ Huang Wan and Sikong Yang Biao, all brought Zhuan to the court and wrote to the court, demanding that Chen Fan, Baowu and other party members be traced in order to comply with people's wishes.
So the titles of Chen Fan and others were completely restored, and their descendants were promoted and appointed.
Soon after, Dong Zhuo was granted the title of Prime Minister, and he could enter the court without any hesitation, and go to the palace with a sword and shoes.
He named his mother Chiyangjun and appointed Lingcheng and other officials for her.
At that time, the residences of the emperor's relatives in Luoyang City were connected, and every family had a lot of gold and silk property.
Dong Zhuo indulged his soldiers, rushed into their homes, raped and robbed women, and robbed their property. He called this "prison raids."
He also raped and harassed the princess, occupied and robbed the palace maids, and indiscriminately inflicted cruel punishments. Anyone with a little hatred would be killed. No one in the palace or outside the palace could protect himself.
Dong Zhuo once sent troops to Yangcheng. At that time, people were gathering in front of the Tutu Temple. Dong Zhuo ordered them to go forward and kill all the people, take away the belongings on the carts, load the robbed women, tie their heads to the shafts of the carts, and return to the capital singing and shouting.

summary:

He deposed the young emperor, established Emperor Xian, and assumed the role of Prime Minister, wielding immense power.
