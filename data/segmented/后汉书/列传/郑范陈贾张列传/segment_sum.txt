少学《公羊春秋》。晚善《左氏传》，遂积精深思，通达其旨，同学者皆师之。

translation: 
◆Zheng Xingzhuan, Zheng Xing, courtesy name Shaogan, was born in Kaifeng, Henan.
He studied "Gongyang Chunqiu" when he was young, and "Zuo Shi Zhuan" in his later years. He accumulated knowledge, thought deeply, and understood the gist of the book. All his classmates regarded Zheng as their teacher.
During the Tianfeng period, he led his students to teach Liu Xin the meaning of "Zuo Shi Zhuan". Liu Xin praised Zheng Xing's talent and asked Zheng Xing to write regulations, chapters, exegesis, and correct "Santong Li".
When Gengshi came to the throne, he appointed Si Zhi Li Song as acting prime minister and entered Chang'an first. Li Song appointed Zheng Xing as chief historian and sent him back to pick up the emperor and move the capital.
Some of the generals in Gengshi were from Shandong, and they all advised him to stay in Luoyang.
Zheng Xing advised the emperor: Your Majesty raised troops from Jingchu, and the government had not yet been implemented. Once the country was established, the heroes of Shanxi were vying to kill Wang Mang, and opened the door to welcome Your Majesty in the suburbs. What was the purpose?
This is because the people of the world were suffering from Wang Mang's tyranny and missed the old virtues of Han Emperor Gaozu.
If you don't appease the people for a long time now, I'm afraid that the people will be alienated and thieves will rise again.
"Spring and Autumn" records that Duke Huan of Qi ascended the throne as "Qi Xiaobai enters Qi", but is not called Qi Hou because he has not yet gone to the temple to worship his ancestors.
Those who are discussing now want to pacify Chimei first and then enter the pass. They do not know the root and fight for the ends. I am afraid that the country's guards will be transferred to Hangu Pass. Even if they sleep in Luoyang, how can they be content with pillow mats?
Emperor Gengshi said: I have made up my mind to go west.
He worshiped Zheng Xing as an admonishment official and sent him to gather in Guanxi, Shuofang, Liangzhou and Yizhou. After returning, he worshiped him as the governor of Liangzhou.
It happened that someone rebelled in Tianshui and attacked and killed the county guard. Zheng Xing was dismissed from his post.
At that time, Chimei entered the pass and the road to the east was blocked, so Zheng Xing went west to join Kaixiao. Weixiao humbly invited Zheng with courtesy, but Zheng Xing felt aggrieved and said he could not afford to be ill.
Wei Xiao boasted about himself and often compared himself to King Wen of Zhou, so he discussed with the generals and established himself as king.
When Zheng Xing heard about it, he dissuaded Wei Xiao and said: "Spring and Autumn" legend: It is called deafness if the mouth does not speak the words of loyalty, and the ear does not hear the sum of the five tones.
