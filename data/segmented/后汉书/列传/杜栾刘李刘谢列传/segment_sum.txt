◆The biography of Liu Yu, named Jiji, was from Guangling.
His great-great-grandfather was King Jing of Guangling.
His father, Liu Bian, was the prefect of Qinghe.
When Liu Yu was young, he was fond of studying Confucian classics, and was particularly good at the art of astronomy, calculus, and arithmetic. The prefecture and county invited him politely and refused to go.
In the eighth year of Yanxi's reign, Taiwei Yang Bing praised him as a virtuous and upright man. When he came to the capital, he wrote a letter saying: I think that the Eastern Kingdom is humble. Because I am a descendant of the Liu family, I am exempted from corvee service and not sent to the army.
The late Taiwei Yang Bing knew that I had read some classics, so he thought highly of me and recommended me as a virtuous and upright person. He really hoped that I could make some contribution to the country.
Yang Bing's talents for the country were not put to use, and unfortunately he died too early.
When I was in the countryside, I heard some songs about proud ministers who brutally harmed the people. There were sighs and sighs everywhere. The most bitter and unpleasant smell made people cry.
I am happy to be recommended. When answering the emperor's questions, I should express my feelings with complete loyalty and not dare to deceive others.

summary:
summary line 6: **Liu Yu** (courtesy name Jiji) was from Guangling, descendant of King Jing of Guangling. He was skilled in astronomy and mathematics. In Yanxi's eighth year, Taiwei Yang Bing recommended him as virtuous and upright. He submitted a memorial criticizing eunuchs for creating offspring to inherit titles, the harem's excess, and the people's suffering. He advised the emperor to open the path of remonstrance. When General Dou Wu failed to kill the eunuchs, Liu Yu was killed.

summary line 7: **Xie Bi** (courtesy name Fuxuan) was from Wuyang County, Dongjun. In Jianning's second year, he was recruited as a doctor. When a green snake appeared and strong winds blew, Xie Bi submitted a secret report blaming the emperor's weakness, the Queen Mother's imprisonment, and the execution of Chen Fan. Cao Shao, nephew of eunuch Cao Jie, hated Xie Bi and had him tortured to death in prison. In Chuping's second year, Zhao Qian avenged Xie Bi by arresting and killing Cao Shao.
