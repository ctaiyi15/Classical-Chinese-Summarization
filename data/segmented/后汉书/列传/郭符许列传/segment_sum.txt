summary line 0: Guo Taichuan, also known as Lin Zong, was born into a poor family in Jiexiu, Taiyuan. After his father's early death, he refused his mother's suggestion to work in the county, instead studying under Chenggao Qu Boyan. He became well-versed in many books, eloquent, and renowned for his loud voice. While traveling in Luoyang, he befriended Li Ying of Henan, gaining fame in the capital. Returning home, he crossed the river alone with Li Ying, impressing onlookers. Despite recruitment offers from officials like Situ Huang Qiong, he declined, citing his observations of heaven and society. He was known for his tall, majestic appearance and his habit of folding a corner of his towel, which others imitated as "Lin Zongjin." Fan Pang praised him as a man who could not be treated as a subject or friend. After his mother's death, he was known for extreme filial piety. During the party disaster, he avoided harm by refraining from passionate discussions, teaching behind closed doors with thousands of students. In the first year of Jianning, he mourned the deaths of Chen Fan and Dou Wu, predicting the country's danger. He died at home at forty-two, with over a thousand mourners. Cai Yong wrote his epitaph, feeling no shame in doing so.
