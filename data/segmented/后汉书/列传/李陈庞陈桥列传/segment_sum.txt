西域殷富，多珍宝，诸国侍子及督使贾胡数遗恂奴婢、宛马、金银、香罽之属，一无所受。

自是道路夷清，威恩并行。
迁武威太守。

恂因诣洛阳谢。

translation: 
◆In the biography of Li Xun, Li Xun was born in Linjing, Anding.
He studied "Han Shi" at a young age and taught hundreds of students.
Li Hong, the prefect of Yingchuan, asked Li Xun to be appointed as a meritorious official. However, he failed to take up the post, and the prefecture called him to work.
When Li Hong died, Li Xun responded to the state's order and sent Li Hong back to his hometown for his funeral.
He has been buried, and the grave has been left to mourn for three years.
Later Li Xun was called into Situ Huanyu's mansion.
Later he was promoted to the imperial censor, and he went to Youzhou as an envoy to declare the emperor's favor and comfort Beidi. Wherever he went, he described the mountains and rivers, as well as the conditions of villages and settlements, etc., in more than a hundred volumes, all of which were sealed. Emperor Suzong commended him and appointed him as the governor of Yanzhou.
He set an example by keeping promises, often using sheepskin as a mat and a quilt as a cover.
He was promoted to the governor of Zhangye and gained a prestigious reputation.
At that time, General Dou Xian and his soldiers were stationed in Wuwei. No matter how far or near the country was, no one in the country did not practice rituals and gifts of property. Li Xun was not loyal to his duty and was dismissed by Dou Xian.
Later, he was recruited as a visitor and made Chijie the captain of the Western Regions.
The Western Region was rich and rich, and there were many treasures. The servants and envoys of various countries and merchants from Hu Dynasty gave Li Xun many things such as slaves, horses, gold, silver, and fragrant yokes, but Li Xun refused to accept any of them.
The Northern Xiongnu cut off Cheshi and Yiwu in the Western Regions several times, and missions in the area west of Longsha were blocked. Li Xun offered a reward, so he beheaded the commander and hung his head at the military gate.
From then on, the road was clear and smooth, and kindness and power flowed side by side.
Later he moved to Wuwei to be the prefect.
He was dismissed from his post for some reason and walked back to his hometown. He lived in seclusion in the mountains and swamps, making a hut from grass and weaving mats with other people to support himself.
When the Xiqiang rebelled, Li Xun went to the farm and was captured by the Qiang people.
The Qiang people always heard Xun's name and sent him away.
Xun went to Luoyang to express his gratitude.
At that time, there was a famine. Sikong Zhang Min, Situ Lu Gong and other sons sent grain to Xun, but Xun did not accept it.
He moved to Xin'an Pass and picked up acorns to fill his stomach.
