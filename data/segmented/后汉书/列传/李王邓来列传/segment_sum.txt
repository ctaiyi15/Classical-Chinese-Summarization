Li Tong worked for General Wuwei and later became magistrate of Wu County, known for his competence. He became Grand Sikong but remained humble, often taking sick leave. Guangwu favored him, granting his son Li Xiong a marquisate. Li Tong died in Jianwu 18, posthumously titled Gonghou.
