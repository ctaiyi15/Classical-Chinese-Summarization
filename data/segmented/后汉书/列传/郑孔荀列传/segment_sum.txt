summary:
summary line 0: **Zheng Taichuan (Zheng Gongye)** was born in Kaifeng, Henan, as the great-grandson of Sinong Zheng Zhong. Talented from youth, his mother foresaw chaos in the final years of Emperor Ling's reign and secretly befriended heroes. Despite owning 400 hectares of land, he often lacked food. He was renowned throughout Shandong. Initially recommended for office, he declined multiple appointments. When General He Jin recruited him as Minister of the Secretariat and Censor, Zheng warned against recruiting Dong Zhuo, predicting his violent ambition would endanger the court. He Jin ignored this advice, so Zheng resigned. After He Jin was killed and Dong Zhuo rebelled, Zheng conspired with others to oppose Dong Zhuo. When Dong Zhuo wanted to attack the rebels, Zheng argued that governing depends on virtue, not military might. Fearing Dong Zhuo's anger, he fabricated a flattering analysis listing ten reasons why Dong Zhuo would defeat the rebels. Dong Zhuo appointed him general, but others warned of Zheng's cunning, so Dong Zhuo revoked the command. Zheng later plotted to kill Dong Zhuo, but the plan leaked. He escaped to Yuan Shu, who appointed him Governor of Yangzhou, but he died en route at age forty-one.
