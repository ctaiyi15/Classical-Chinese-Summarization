吴汉字子颜，南阳宛人也。
家贫，给事县为亭长。
王莽末，以宾客犯法，乃亡命至渔阳，资用乏，以贩马自业，往来燕、蓟间，所至皆交结豪杰。
更始立，使使者韩鸿徇河北。
或谓鸿曰： 吴子颜，奇士也，可与计事。 鸿召见汉，其悦之，遂承制拜为安乐令。
会王郎起，北州扰惑。
汉素闻光武长者，独欲归心。
乃说太守彭宠曰： 渔阳、上谷突骑，天下所闻也。
君何不合二郡精锐，附刘公击邯郸，此一时之功也。 宠以为然，而官属皆欲附王郎，宠不能夺。
汉乃辞出，止外亭，念所以谲众，未知所出。
望见道中有一人似儒生者，汉使人召之，为具食，问以所闻。
生因言刘公所过，为郡县所归；邯郸举尊号者，实非刘氏。
汉大喜，即诈为光武书，移檄渔阳，使生赍以诣宠，令县以所闻说之，汉复随后入。
宠甚然之。
于是遣汉将兵与上谷诸将并军而南，所至击斩王郎将帅。

Wu Hanzi Ziyan was born in Wan County, Nanyang County.

Gengshi ascended the throne and sent envoy Han Hong to Hebei to recruit surrender.
Someone said to Han Hong, "Wu Ziyan is a wonderful man. You can discuss matters with him." Han Hong summoned Wu Han and was very happy. He appointed him as the magistrate of Anle County according to the emperor's order.
Coinciding with the rise of Wang Lang, Beizhou was disturbed and confused.

Why don't you gather the elites from the two counties and join Liu Gong to attack Handan? This is a rare achievement. Peng Chong thought it was right, but the officials below all wanted to join Wang Lang, and Peng Chong could not take away his ambition.
Wu Han then resigned and stayed in the outer pavilion, trying to blackmail everyone, but he still couldn't figure out a way.
Seeing a man who looked like a Confucian scholar on the road, Wu Han sent people to summon him, provided him with food, and asked him what he had heard.

summary:
summary line 1: Wu Han, courtesy name Ziyan, was born into poverty in Wan County, Nanyang County, and served as a county pavilion chief. During Wang Mang's final years, he fled to Yuyang after his guests broke the law, supporting himself by selling horses and befriending heroes throughout Yan and Ji regions. During Wang Lang's uprising, Wu Han sought to join Guangwu, attempting to persuade Prefect Peng Chong to ally with Guangwu against Handan. When officials favored Wang Lang, Wu Han fabricated a letter from Guangwu and sent a Confucian scholar to convince Peng Chong, who ultimately agreed. Wu Han led troops south, killing Wang Lang's generals, and after pursuing Guangwu to Guang'a, was appointed partial general. Following the conquest of Handan, he was made Marquis Jiance.

Today is a good opportunity to make meritorious deeds and become a lord, please give me more encouragement. The sergeant was enraged and his morale doubled.

Wu Han selected four elite soldiers, including Huangtou Wu He and others, as well as more than 3,000 Wuhuan cavalry, and advanced together with great noise.
Zhou Jianjun was defeated and ran back to the city.
Wu Han pursued them with a long drive, fought for the gate and entered with all their strength, and greatly defeated Jian and Mao's army. Su Mao and Zhou Jian broke through and left.
