summary:
summary line 7: Wei Biao, courtesy name Mengda, was from Pingling. He was a descendant of the prime minister Wei Xian. He was known for his filial piety, mourning his parents for three years. He was a learned Confucian who lived in poverty. He was summoned by Emperor Xianzong and became the Prefect of Wei County. He later served as Da Honglu. He submitted a memorial arguing that the selection of officials should prioritize filial piety and conduct over talent. He also advised the emperor to reduce harsh punishments and select officials with experience as state governors. He was known for his frugality and charity, leaving his family with no money. He wrote a twelve-chapter work titled "Wei Qingzi."
