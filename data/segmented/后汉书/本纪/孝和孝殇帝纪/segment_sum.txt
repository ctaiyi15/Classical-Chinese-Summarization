司空陈宠薨。
五月辛卯，皇太后诏曰： 皇帝幼冲，承统鸿业，朕且权佐助听政，兢兢寅畏，不知所济。
translation: 
At this time, the head of Linwu County, Runan, was from Tang Qiang. Since the county was connected to the South China Sea, he knew the actual situation and wrote a letter explaining the situation.
The emperor issued an edict saying: The delicacies from distant mountains and seas were originally recommended for worship in ancestral temples. If there is any harm caused by this, it is not in line with the original intention of loving the people.
The eunuch is now ordered not to accept this kind of dedication again. From now on, the errand of transporting lychees and longans is saved.
Historians commented: From the resurgence of the Han Dynasty to the Yongyuan Dynasty, although state affairs were tense and relaxed, there were no disturbances. Therefore, the population increased year by year, territories were opened up, and the world was wide.
The partial troops left the fortress, the Xiongnu fled, and the land in Mobei was empty; the governor went on an expedition to the Western Regions, conquered more than 50 countries, and translated the language for 40,000 miles. Could it be that their governance has exceeded three generations, and their governance skills are better than those of the previous life?

Emperor Xiaoshang was named Long, and he was the youngest son of Emperor Xiaoshang.
On the night of December 22nd of the first year of Yuanxing, when he became emperor, he was only more than a hundred days old.
The queen is respected as the empress dowager, and the empress dowager governs the court.
The Northern Huns sent envoys to pay tribute to Dunhuang.
On the thirteenth day of the first spring of the first year of Yanping, Zhang Yu was appointed Taifu.
Situ Xu Fang was appointed as Taiwei, and he participated in the affairs of the general secretary. All officials had their own duties and obeyed his command.
The emperor's brother Liu Sheng was granted the title of King of Pingyuan.
On the 25th, Guang Luxun and Liang Tuna were appointed as Situ.
On the fifth day of March, Emperor Xiaohe was buried in Shenling, and the temple title was Muzong.
On the seventh day of the lunar month, King Liu Qing of Qinghe, King Liu Shou of Jibei, King Liu Kai of Hewen, and King Liu Zhang of Changshan arrived in Fengguo for the first time.
On the 13th day of the fourth lunar month in the summer, an edict was issued to remove the priests who were not at the ceremony.
The Xianbei bandits raided Yuyang, and Zhang Xian, the prefect of Yuyang, pursued them, but Zhang Xian died in the battle.
On the 19th, he appointed Huben Zhonglang, General Deng Zhi, as the Chariot and Cavalry General.
Sikong Chen Chong passed away.
On May 15, the Empress Dowager issued an edict saying: The emperor is young and has inherited a great cause. I will temporarily assist in political affairs and work conscientiously. I don't know how to do a good job in the cause.
