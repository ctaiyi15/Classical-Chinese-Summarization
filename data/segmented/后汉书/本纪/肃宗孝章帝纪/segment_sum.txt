translation: 
On the fifth day of September, the emperor arrived at Pengcheng, accompanied by Liu Zheng, King of Donghai, Liu Ding, King of Pei, and Liu Shang, King of Rencheng.
On the 16th, the emperor arrived in Shouchun.
On the 17th, the imperial edict ordered all the officials in the prefecture and state to reduce the death penalty to one level and send them to Jincheng for garrison; men who were guilty of beheading were to be sentenced to corruption and women to uterine punishment. Those who were imprisoned as ghosts and above were given a reduced sentence of one level, and were sent to the border areas to guard the border and work as hard labor.
The fugitive criminals will atone for their sins: twenty pieces of capital punishment will be tied, and seven pieces will be pounded by cutting off the right foot when they reach Kunxiang City, and three pieces will be pounded by Sikou once they finish the city; if the officials and the people commit crimes without being discovered, and the edict arrives and they surrender, half of them will be redeemed.
Then he named the Marquis of Fuling as the King of Fuling.
On the twenty-fourth, the emperor arrived in Ruyin.
On the twelfth day of the tenth month in winter, the emperor returned to Luoyang.
The Northern Xiongnu Wulan Chu and others led their troops to surrender.
This year, Shi Banchao, the commander of the Western Regions, attacked Shache and severely defeated him.
The Yuezhi Kingdom sent envoys to offer hornless lins and lions.
In the first month of the spring of the second year, Liu Kang, the king of Jinan, Liu Yan, the king of Fuling, and Liu Yan, the king of Zhongshan, came to Beijing to pay homage.
On February 30, the emperor passed away in the front hall of Zhangde at the age of thirty-three.
Those who cannot afford to sleep in temples according to the imperial edict will follow the system of the late emperor.
