六年春正月丙辰，改舂陵乡为章陵县。世世复徭役，比丰、沛，无有所豫。
辛酉，诏曰： 往岁水、旱、蝗虫为灾，谷价腾跃，人用困乏。
朕惟百姓无以自赡，恻然愍之。
其命郡国有谷者，给禀高年、鳏、寡、孤、独及笃癃、无家属贫不能自存者，如《律》。
二千石勉加循抚，无令失职。
扬武将军马成等拔舒，获李宪。
二月，大司马吴汉拔朐，获董宪、庞萌，山东悉平。诸将还京师，置酒赏赐。
三月，公孙述遣将任满寇南郡。
夏四月丙子，幸长安，始谒高庙，遂有事十一陵。
遣虎牙大将军盖延等七将军从陇道伐公孙述。
五月己未，至自长安。
隗嚣反，盖延等因与嚣战于陇阺，诸将败绩。
辛丑，诏曰： 惟天水、陇西、安定、北地吏人为隗嚣所诖误者，又三辅遭难赤眉，有犯法不道者，自殊死以下，皆赦除之。
六月辛卯，诏曰： 夫张官置吏，所以为人也。今百姓遭难，户口耗少，而县官吏职所置尚繁，其令司隶、州牧各实所部，省减吏员。
县国不足置长吏可并合者，上大司徒、大司空二府。 于是条奏并省四百余县，吏职减损，十置其一。
代郡太守刘兴击卢芳将贾览于高柳，战殁。
初，乐浪人王调据郡不服。
秋，遣乐浪太守王遵击之，郡吏杀调降。
遣前将军李通率二将军，与公孙述将战于西城，破之。
translation: 
On the 16th day of the first lunar month in the sixth year of spring, Chongling Township was renamed Zhangling County, and the corvee was exempted from generation to generation. According to the old practices in Fengyi and Pei County, the hometown of Emperor Gaozu, the people of Zhangling County no longer worried about the possibility of corvée in the future.
On the 21st, an edict was issued saying: Floods, droughts, and locust plagues in previous years caused grain prices to skyrocket and the people suffered.
I feel very sad when I see the people unable to feed themselves.
The counties and states that still have food reserves are ordered to provide relief to the elderly, widowers, widows, orphans, the old and childless, those who are seriously ill and needy, as well as those who are homeless and unable to support themselves. Everything must comply with the provisions of the Han Code.
The county governor should work hard to appease the people and not neglect his duties.
General Yangwu Ma Cheng and others captured Shu County and captured Li Xian.
In February, Grand Sima Wu Han captured Qu County, captured Dong Xian and Pang Meng, and pacified everything east of the Taihang Mountains. The generals returned to the capital, where they were given banquets and rewards.
In March, Gongsun Shu sent general Ren Man to invade Nanjun.
On the eighth day of the fourth lunar month in the summer, Emperor Guangwu arrived in Chang'an and began to pay homage to the Gaomiao Temple and worship the Eleven Tombs.
He sent General Huya General Gai Yan and other seven generals from Longdao to attack Gongsun Shu.
On May 21, Emperor Guangwu returned to Luoyang from Chang'an.
Kuai Xiao rebelled, and Gai Yan fought with Kuai Xiao in Longnandi, and Gai Yan and others were severely defeated.
Xin Chou, Emperor Guangwu issued an edict: Officials in Tianshui, Longxi, Anding, and Beidi who were implicated in Kaixiao's deception, as well as those who violated the law and did not follow the right path when the third assistant was in trouble with Chimei, will be exempted from punishment if they are sentenced to less than beheading.
On June 24, the edict stated: The state sets up officials, large and small, solely for the sake of the people. Now that the people are suffering and the household registration has been reduced, the state still has many officials. It orders the lieutenant and the governors of each state to verify the ministries under their jurisdiction and reduce the number of officials.
Those counties that are not enough for unit chiefs and can be merged will be reported to the two offices of Da Situ and Da Sikong. So Captain Si Su and the Mu Shou of each state petitioned to merge and reduce the number of more than 400 counties and cut officials, leaving only one-tenth of the previous number.
In June, Liu Xing, the prefect of Daijun, attacked Lu Fang's general Jia Lan in Gaoliu, and Liu Xing died in the battle.
In the past, Wang Tiao, a Lelang man, occupied the county and refused to accept it.
In the autumn of this year, Wang Zun, the prefect of Lelang, was sent to attack. The county officials killed Wang Tiao and surrendered.
He sent former general Li Tong and two generals to fight with general Gongsun Shu in Xicheng and defeated him.

summary:
summary line 0: In the sixth year of spring, Chongling Township was renamed Zhangling County, and its people were exempted from corvée, following the precedent of Fengyi and Pei County. An edict ordered counties with food reserves to relieve the elderly, widowers, widows, orphans, the childless, the seriously ill, and the homeless, in accordance with the Han Code. General Ma Cheng captured Shu County, and Grand Sima Wu Han pacified the area east of the Taihang Mountains. In March, Gongsun Shu invaded Nanjun. Emperor Guangwu visited Chang'an to pay homage at the Gaomiao Temple and the Eleven Tombs, then sent General Gai Yan to attack Gongsun Shu. After returning to Luoyang, Gai Yan was defeated by Kuai Xiao. An edict exempted officials in Tianshui, Longxi, Anding, and Beidi from punishment for crimes less than beheading if implicated in Kuai Xiao's rebellion. Another edict ordered the reduction of officials, resulting in the merger of over 400 counties and a tenfold reduction in staff. Liu Xing, the prefect of Daijun, died in battle against Lu Fang's general. Wang Tiao of Lelang was killed by his own officials, and General Li Tong defeated Gongsun Shu's forces. A locust plague struck, and a solar eclipse occurred. An edict called for recommendations of virtuous and upright individuals. Wang Mang decreed that non-slaves not complying with old laws be made commoners. Grand Sikong Song Hong was dismissed. Emperor Guangwu restored the thirty-to-one tax rate. Kuai Xiao's invasion of Fufeng was repelled by General Feng Yi. Officials began to be dismissed, and the Xiongnu sent envoys.
