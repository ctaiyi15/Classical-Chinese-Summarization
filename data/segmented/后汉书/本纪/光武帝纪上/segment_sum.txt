At that time, Wang Lang's general Li Yu was stationed in Bairen County. The Han army entered the area without detecting the enemy's situation. The advance guard generals Zhu Fu and Deng Yu were defeated by Li Yu, and all the supplies in the army were lost.
Guangwu learned the news later, gathered the defeated stragglers of Zhu Fu and Deng Yu, fought fiercely with Li Yu at the outer city gate, defeated Li Yu, and confiscated the grain, grass and supplies that Li Yu had obtained during the battle with Zhu and Deng.
Li Yu retreated to the small town, but Guangwu could not attack it, so he led his troops to capture Guang'a County.
At this time, it happened that Geng Kuang, the governor of Shanggu, and Peng Chong, the governor of Yuyang, respectively sent generals such as Wu Han and Kou Xun to lead elite cavalry to assist in attacking Wang Lang. Emperor Gengshi also sent Shangshu servant She Xie Gong to attack Wang Lang. Guangwu rewarded the soldiers well, and then marched eastward and surrounded Julu City.
Wang Lang's general Wang Rao defended the city, and Guangwu failed to capture it for more than a month.
Wang Lang also sent Ni Hong and Liu Feng to lead tens of thousands of troops to rescue Julu. Guangwu went to Nanxian to attack them head-on, killing thousands of enemy troops.
In April, he marched again and besieged Handan, winning consecutive battles.
On the day of Jiachen in the fifth month, Handan was captured and Wang Lang was killed.
The documents were seized and it was discovered that the Han military officials had colluded with Wang Lang to slander Guangwu's letter, which was called Qianzhang.
Without even looking at it, Guangwu summoned the generals to burn them, saying: "Let those who are tossing and turning calm down."
Emperor Gengshi sent his censor, Shi Zhifujie, to make Guangwu the King of Xiao, asking him to cease the war and return to the seat of Emperor Gengshi.
Guangwu declined, saying that Hebei had not yet been pacified and would not accept the order.
From then on, Guangwu began to have second thoughts about Emperor Gengshi.
At that time, the political situation in Chang'an was in chaos, and all parties rebelled against Gengshi Emperor.
Liu Yong, the king of Liang, acted exclusively in Suiyang County, Gongsun Shu proclaimed himself king in Bashu, Li Xian established himself as the king of Huainan, Qin Feng claimed to be the king of Chuli, Zhang Bu raised troops in Langxie, Dong Xian raised troops in Donghai, Yancen raised troops in Hanzhong, and Tian Rong raised troops in Yiling. They all appointed generals and invaded various counties.
