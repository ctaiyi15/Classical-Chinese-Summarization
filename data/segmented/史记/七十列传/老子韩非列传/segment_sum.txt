至关，关令尹喜曰： 子将隐矣，彊为我著书。
於是老子乃著书上下篇，言道德之意五千馀言而去，莫知其所终。
或曰：老莱子亦楚人也，著书十五篇，言道家之用，与孔子同时云。
盖老子百有六十馀岁，或言二百馀岁，以其脩道而养寿也。
自孔子死之後百二十九年，而史记周太史儋见秦献公曰： 始秦与周合，合五百岁而离，离七十岁而霸王者出焉。
或曰儋即老子，或曰非也，世莫知其然否。
老子，隐君子也。
老子之子名宗，宗为魏将，封於段干。
宗子注，注子宫，宫玄孙假，假仕於汉孝文帝。
而假之子解为胶西王卬太傅，因家于齐焉。
世之学老子者则绌儒学，儒学亦绌老子。 道不同不相为谋 ，岂谓是邪？
李耳无为自化，清静自正。
庄子者，蒙人也，名周。
周尝为蒙漆园吏，与梁惠王、齐宣王同时。
其学无所不闚，然其要本归於老子之言。
故其著书十馀万言，大抵率寓言也。作渔父、盗跖、胠箧，以诋訿孔子之徒，以明老子之术。
畏累虚、亢桑子之属，皆空语无事实。
然善属书离辞，指事类情，用剽剥儒、墨，虽当世宿学不能自解免也。

It is said that I lived for more than 160 years, and some people say that I lived for more than 200 years. This is because he can cultivate his mind and live a long life.
One hundred and twenty-nine years after the death of Confucius, historical records record that when Taishi Dan of Zhou met with Duke Xiang of Qin, he prophesied: When the Qin State and the Zhou Dynasty were united, they were united for five hundred years and then separated. After seventy years of separation, there will be people who will become kings and hegemons.
Some people say that Taishi Dan is Lao Tzu, and some people say that he is not. No one in the world knows which statement is correct.
In short, I am a true hidden gentleman.
Lao Tzu's son is called Li Zong. He served as a general of Wei State and his fiefdom was in Duanqian.
Li Zong's son is called Li Zhu, Li Zhu's son is called Li Gong, and Li Gong's great-great-grandson is called Li Jie. Li Jie served as an official during the reign of Emperor Wen of Han Dynasty.
Li Jie's son Li Jie served as the Taifu of Jiaoxi King Liu Xuan, so the Li family settled in Qidi.
Anyone in the world who studies Laozi's knowledge will disparage Confucian thought and doctrine; and those who study Confucianism will also disparage Laozi's doctrine. People with different opinions cannot agree with each other. Is this the case?
Lao Tzu believes that if you govern by doing nothing, the people will naturally become corrupt; if you are quiet and do nothing, the people will naturally return to righteousness.

He once served as a minor official in Mengdi Lacquer Garden, and was a contemporary of King Hui of Liang and King Xuan of Qi.
He is knowledgeable and has covered all-encompassing areas of research, but his central idea originated from the teachings of Laozi.

"Wei Leixu" and "Kang Sangzi" are all made up out of thin air and have no basis in fact.

summary:
summary line 0: A biographical account of Laozi, a native of Chu State who served as a Zhou dynasty librarian. When Confucius sought his advice on etiquette, Laozi dismissed the topic, noting that its proponents had long since perished, and advised Confucius to abandon arrogance and excessive desires. Confucius later likened Laozi to a dragon, elusive and transcendent. Laozi, observing Zhou's decline, wrote a book of over 5,000 words on morality at the request of a gatekeeper before disappearing. Some claim he lived over 160 years, while others identify him with the historian Taishi Dan, who prophesied about Qin and Zhou's future. Laozi's son, Li Zong, became a Wei general, and his descendants served in Han times. The text contrasts Laozi's philosophy of non-action with Confucianism, noting mutual criticism between followers.
