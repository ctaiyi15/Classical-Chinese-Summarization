乃往见子楚，说曰： 吾能大子之门。
子楚笑曰： 且自大君之门，而乃大吾门！
吕不韦曰： 子不知也，吾门待子门而大。
子楚心知所谓，乃引与坐，深语。
吕不韦曰： 秦王老矣，安国君得为太子。
窃闻安国君爱幸华阳夫人，华阳夫人无子，能立適嗣者独华阳夫人耳。
今子兄弟二十馀人，子又居中，不甚见幸，久质诸侯。即大王薨，安国君立为王，则子毋几得与长子及诸子旦暮在前者争为太子矣。

Lord An Guo had more than twenty sons.
Lord An Guo had a concubine whom he loved very much. He made her his wife and called her Mrs. Huayang.
Mrs. Huayang has no son.
An Guojun had a middle-ranking son named Zichu. Zichu's mother was named Xia Ji, who was not favored.
Zi Chu was sent to Zhao as a hostage as the son of Qin.
Qin State attacked Zhao State many times, and Zhao State did not treat Zi Chu politely.
Zichu was the grandson of the concubine of the King of Qin and was a hostage in the State of Zhao. He was not rich in carriages, horses and daily necessities, and his life was difficult and very unhappy.
Lu Buwei went to Handan to do business. After seeing Zi Chu, he felt very fond of him and said: Zi Chu is like a rare commodity that can be hoarded.
So he went to visit Zi Chu and persuaded him: I can expand your family.
Zi Chu smiled and said: "For now, let's expand your own family first, and then come and expand mine!"
Lu Buwei said: "You don't understand. My family has to wait for your family to grow before it can grow."
Zi Chu knew what Lu Buwei meant, so he sat him down together and had an in-depth conversation.
Lu Buwei said: The King of Qin is old, and Lord An Guo was made the crown prince.
I heard privately that Lord An Guo dotes on Mrs. Huayang very much. Mrs. Huayang has no son, so only Mrs. Huayang can choose the prince.
Now you have more than twenty brothers, and you are ranked in the middle. You are not favored by the King of Qin and have been left as a hostage in the vassal states for a long time. Even if the King of Qin dies and An Guojun succeeds to the throne, you should not expect to compete with your eldest brother and other brothers who are by the King of Qin's side sooner or later.
