春申君者，楚人也，名歇，姓黄氏。
游学博闻，事楚顷襄王。
顷襄王以歇为辩，使於秦。
秦昭王使白起攻韩、魏，败之於华阳，禽魏将芒卯，韩、魏服而事秦。
秦昭王方令白起与韩、魏共伐楚，未行，而楚使黄歇適至於秦，闻秦之计。
当是之时，秦已前使白起攻楚，取巫、黔中之郡，拔鄢郢，东至竟陵，楚顷襄王东徙治於陈县。
黄歇见楚怀王之为秦所诱而入朝，遂见欺，留死於秦。
顷襄王，其子也，秦轻之，恐壹举兵而灭楚。
歇乃上书说秦昭王曰：
天下莫彊於秦、楚。
今闻大王欲伐楚，此犹两虎相与斗。
两虎相与斗而驽犬受其弊，不如善楚。
translation: 
Chun Shenjun is a native of Chu State, his name is Xie and his surname is Huang.
He once traveled around to learn from his teachers, gained profound knowledge, and served King Qingxiang of Chu.
King Qingxiang thought Huang Xie was eloquent and asked him to go on a mission to Qin.
At that time, King Zhao of Qin sent Bai Qi to attack Hanhe, defeated Han and Wei in Huayang, and captured Wei general Mang Mao. Han and Wei surrendered to Qin and served Qin.
King Zhao of Qin had ordered Bai Qi to attack Chu with South Korea and Wei, but the Qin army had not yet set off. At this time, King Chu sent Huang Xie to Qin by chance and heard about Qin's plan.
At this time, the State of Qin had occupied a large area of ​​Chu State, because before this King Qin had sent Bai Qi to attack the State of Chu, captured Wu County, Qianzhong County, captured the capital of Yancheng and Ying, and marched eastward to Jingling. King Qingxiang of Chu had to move the capital eastward to Chen County.
Huang Xie saw that King Huai of Chu was lured by Qin and went to Qin's court for negotiation. As a result, he was deceived, detained and died in Qin.
King Qingxiang was the son of King Huai of Chu, and the state of Qin did not pay attention to him at all. Huang Xie was worried that the state of Chu would perish if a war started.
So he wrote a letter to persuade King Qin:
Among the princes in the world, no one is more powerful than Qin and Chu.
Now I heard that your Majesty is going to conquer Chu State, which is like two tigers fighting each other.
When two tigers fight each other and the inferior dog takes advantage of it, it is better to be friendly with Chu.

summary:
summary line 0: Chun Shenjun, a native of Chu named Huang Xie, served King Qingxiang of Chu. He was sent to Qin after King Qingxiang recognized his eloquence. At that time, Qin, under King Zhao, had been attacking Chu's allies, and Huang Xie learned of Qin's plan to invade Chu. Worried for Chu's survival, he wrote to King Zhao, arguing that Qin and Chu, as the two strongest states, should not fight each other, as this would benefit weaker states like Han and Wei. He warned that Qin's past victories had made it powerful, but attacking Chu would strengthen its enemies and lead to future trouble. Citing historical examples, he advised Qin to ally with Chu instead.

After reading Chun Shenjun's letter, King Zhao said: "It's really good."
So he stopped Bai Qi from going on an expedition and resigned from Han and Wei.
At the same time, he sent envoys to send generous gifts to Chu, and Qin and Chu formed an alliance to become friendly countries.
Huang Xie accepted the alliance and returned to Chu State. The King of Chu sent Huang Xie and Prince Wan to Qin State as hostages. Qin State detained them for several years.
Later, King Qingxiang of Chu fell ill, but Prince Wan was unable to return to Chu.
However, the prince had a very good personal relationship with the Qin Prime Minister Ying Hou, so Huang Xie persuaded Ying Hou: Prime Minister, are you really very good to the prince of Chu?
Ying Hou said: Yes.
Huang Xie said: Now the King of Chu may be ill, so it would be better for Qin to let the prince go back.
If the prince can be established as king, he will definitely serve the Qin State deeply and be grateful to the Prime Minister for his kindness that will never be exhausted. This is not only an expression of goodwill and friendship between the countries, but also retains an ally of a great country for the future.
If he is not allowed to go back, then he will be just a commoner in Xianyang City at best; Chu State will establish a new crown prince, and he will definitely not serve Qin State by then.
That would lose the trust of friendly countries and cut off the allies of a powerful country. This is not the best policy.

summary:
summary line 1: King Zhao agreed, halted the attack, and formed an alliance with Chu. Huang Xie returned with Prince Wan of Chu as a hostage in Qin. When King Qingxiang fell ill, Huang Xie persuaded the Qin prime minister to let Prince Wan return, arguing it would secure Chu's loyalty. When Qin hesitated, Huang Xie helped Prince Wan escape disguised as a charioteer, staying behind to face punishment. King Zhao initially wanted to execute him but, on advice, spared him and sent him back. Three months later, King Qingxiang died, and Prince Wan became King Kaolie of Chu, appointing Huang Xie as prime minister and granting him the title Lord Chunshen with twelve counties.

当是时，楚复彊。

In his eighth year, he conquered Lu for Chu, restoring Chu's strength. 春申君曰： 何谓毋望之祸？
曰： 李园不治国而君之仇也，不为兵而养死士之日久矣，楚王卒，李园必先入据权而杀君以灭口。
此所谓毋望之祸也。
春申君曰： 何谓毋望之人？
对曰： 君置臣郎中，楚王卒，李园必先入，臣为君杀李园。
此所谓毋望之人也。
春申君曰： 足下置之，李园，弱人也，仆又善之，且又何至此！
硃英知言不用，恐祸及身，乃亡去。
後十七日，楚考烈王卒，李园果先入，伏死士於棘门之内。
春申君入棘门，园死士侠刺春申君，斩其头，投之棘门外。
於是遂使吏尽灭春申君之家。
而李园女弟初幸春申君有身而入之王所生子者遂立，是为楚幽王。
是岁也，秦始皇帝立九年矣。
嫪毐亦为乱於秦，觉，夷其三族，而吕不韦废。
太史公曰：吾適楚，观春申君故城，宫室盛矣哉！
初，春申君之说秦昭王，及出身遣楚太子归，何其智之明也！
後制於李园，旄矣。
translation: 
Chun Shenjun asked again: What is an unexpected disaster?
Zhu Ying replied: Li Yuan is your enemy if he is not in charge of national affairs. He has been raising assassins for a long time without caring about the military. As soon as the King of Chu is born, Li Yuan will definitely enter the palace first to seize power and kill you to silence you.
This is what is called an unexpected disaster.
Chun Shenjun then asked: What do you mean by someone who comes unexpectedly?
Zhu Ying replied: If you arrange for me to be the doctor, when the King of Chu is born, Li Yuan will be the first to enter the palace. I will kill Li Yuan for you.
This is what is called an unexpected person.
After hearing this, Chun Shenjun said: "You have to give up this plan." Li Yuan is a weak person, and I am very friendly to him. Besides, how could he get to this point!
Zhu Ying knew that his suggestions were not adopted, and he was afraid that disaster would affect him, so he ran away.
Seventeen days later, King Chu Kaolie passed away. As expected, Li Yuan entered the palace first and ambushed the assassin at Thorny Gate.
When Chun Shenjun entered the Thorny Gate, the assassins raised by Li Yuan caught him from both sides and killed him. He cut off his head and threw it outside the Thorny Gate.
At the same time, he sent officials to kill everyone in Chunshenjun's family.
Li Yuan's sister was favored by Lord Chun Shen and became pregnant. Then she entered the palace and was favored by Queen Chu Kaolie. The son born to her was established as King of Chu. This was King Chu You.
This year, Qin Shihuang has been on the throne for nine years.
Lao Ai also had an affair with the Queen Mother of Qin. After being discovered, the three tribes were destroyed by the barbarians, and Lu Buwei was deposed because of his involvement.
Taigong Shi said: I went to the Chu region and visited Chun Shenjun's old city. The palace buildings were very magnificent!
At that time, Chun Shenjun persuaded King Qin Zhao and risked his life to send people to send the Prince of Chu back to Chu State. What a smart and brilliant move it was!
But later he was controlled by Li Yuan and became confused.

summary:
summary line 3: Chun Shenjun later became involved with Li Yuan, who presented his pregnant sister to him. She convinced Chun Shenjun to offer her to the childless King Kaolie, ensuring his son would become king. The king favored her, and she bore a son, who became prince. Li Yuan, fearing Chun Shenjun would reveal the secret, secretly raised assassins. When King Kaolie fell ill, a guest named Zhu Ying warned Chun Shenjun of Li Yuan's plot, but he dismissed it. Seventeen days after the king's death, Li Yuan ambushed and killed Chun Shenjun at the palace gate, then massacred his family. Li Yuan's sister's son became King You of Chu.
