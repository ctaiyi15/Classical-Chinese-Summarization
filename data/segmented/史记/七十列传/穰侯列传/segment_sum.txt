穰侯魏冄者，秦昭王母宣太后弟也。
其先楚人，姓羋氏。
秦武王卒，无子，立其弟为昭王。
昭王母故号为羋八子，及昭王即位，羋八子号为宣太后。
宣太后非武王母。
武王母号曰惠文后，先武王死。
宣太后二弟：其异父长弟曰穰侯，姓魏氏，名厓；同父弟曰羋戎，为华阳君。
而昭王同母弟曰高陵君、泾阳君。
而魏厓最贤，自惠王、武王时任职用事。
武王卒，诸弟争立，唯魏厓力为能立昭王。
昭王即位，以厓为将军，卫咸阳。
诛季君之乱，而逐武王后出之魏，昭王诸兄弟不善者皆灭之，威振秦国。
昭王少，宣太后自治，任魏厓为政。
昭王七年，樗里子死，而使泾阳君质於齐。
translation: 
Ranghou Wei Ran was the younger brother of the Queen Mother Xuan, the mother of King Zhao of Qin.
His ancestor was from the Chu state and his surname was Mi.
After the death of King Qin Wu, he had no sons, so he made his younger brother the king of the country, namely King Zhao.
King Zhao's mother was originally a female official in the palace and was called Mi Bazi. When King Zhao came to the throne, Mi Bazi was revered as Queen Mother Xuan.
Queen Mother Xuan was not King Wu’s biological mother.
King Wu's mother was Queen Huiwen, who died before King Wu died.
Empress Dowager Xuan has two younger brothers: her eldest half-brother is named Ranghou, whose surname is Wei and given name is Ran; her half-brother is named Mirong, who is Huayang Jun.
King Zhao also has two younger brothers with the same mother: one is called Gaolingjun and the other is named Jingyangjun.
Among many people, Wei Ran was the most virtuous. He had been in power since the time of King Hui and King Wu.
After King Wu died, his younger brothers competed to inherit the throne. Only Wei Ran was capable and supported King Zhao.
After King Zhao ascended the throne, he appointed Wei Ran as general to guard Xianyang.
He once put down the rebellion of Ji Jun Gongzi Zhuang and some ministers, and expelled Queen Wu to the state of Wei. All the brothers of King Zhao who had evil intentions were killed. Wei Ran's prestige shocked the state of Qin for a while.
At that time, King Zhao was still young, so Empress Dowager Xuan personally presided over the affairs of the court, leaving Wei Ran in charge.
In the seventh year of King Zhao, Chuilizi died, and the State of Qin sent Lord Jingyang to Qi State as a hostage.

summary:
summary line 0: Ranghou Wei Ran, younger brother of Queen Mother Xuan, was a key figure in the Qin state during King Zhao's reign. After King Wu died without heirs, Wei Ran supported King Zhao's ascension, quelled rebellions, and eliminated rivals. King Zhao appointed him general to guard Xianyang, and later prime minister, a position he held multiple times. Wei Ran's military campaigns expanded Qin's territory significantly, including victories over Han, Wei, and Chu, capturing numerous cities and killing hundreds of thousands of enemies. His wealth and power rivaled the royal family's.
