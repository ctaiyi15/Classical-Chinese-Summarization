Chuilizi, named Ji, was the younger brother of King Hui of Qin. He and King Hui were not born to the same mother.
His mother is a Korean woman.

In the eighth year of King Hui of Qin, Chuilizi was granted the title of Yougeng. The King of Qin sent him to lead an army to attack Quwo in the State of Wei. He drove away all the people there and occupied the city. The land around Quwo was annexed to the State of Qin.
In the twenty-fifth year of King Hui of Qin, the King of Qin appointed Chuilizi as a general to attack the State of Zhao. He captured General Zhuang Bao of the State of Zhao and captured Linyi.
The next year, he assisted Wei Zhang in attacking Chu, defeated Chu general Qu Gai, and captured the Hanzhong area.
The King of Qin granted him the title Chuilizi, with the title Yan Jun.
After the death of King Hui of Qin, Prince Wu came to the throne, expelled Zhang Yi and Wei Zhang, and appointed Chuilizi and Gan Mao as the left and right prime ministers.
The King of Qin sent Gan Mao to attack South Korea and captured Yiyang in one fell swoop. At the same time, he sent Chuilizi to lead a hundred chariots to the Zhou Dynasty capital.
The king of Zhou sent soldiers to line up to greet him with a very respectful attitude.
When the King of Chu learned about it, he was furious and scolded the King of Zhou because the King of Zhou should not be so respectful to Qin's uninvited guests.

Why?
It’s just because I’m unprepared.

Now the Qin State is a country as fierce as a tiger and a wolf. He sent Chuilizi into the Zhou capital with hundreds of chariots, with ulterior motives. The King of Zhou viewed this matter based on the lessons of Qiu You and Cai Guo, so he sent soldiers with halberds in front and sergeants with strong bows in the back. On the surface, they were guarding Chuilizi, but in fact they were keeping him under guard to prevent any unexpected events.
Besides, how could the King of Zhou not worry about the Zhou Dynasty?
