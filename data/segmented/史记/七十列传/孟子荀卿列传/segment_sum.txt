天下方务於合从连衡，以攻伐为贤，而孟轲乃述唐、虞、三代之德，是以所如者不合。
退而与万章之徒序诗书，述仲尼之意，作孟子七篇。
translation: 
Tai Shigong said: When I read "Mencius", whenever I read that King Hui of Liang asked how to make profits for our country, I couldn't help but put down the book and sigh.
Said: Alas, seeking profit is really the beginning of all troubles!
Confucius rarely talked about the issue of profit. The reason was that he wanted to fundamentally prevent this source of trouble.
So he said that acting based on personal interests will lead to a lot of resentment.
From the emperor down to the common people, the disadvantages of being greedy for profit exist. What's the difference?
Meng Ke was from the state of Zou.
He once studied with Zisi's disciples.
After he became proficient in his studies, he went to lobby King Xuan of Qi, but King Xuan of Qi did not appoint him.
So when he arrived in Wei State, King Hui of Liang not only did not listen to his ideas, but also believed that his ideas were unrealistic and far from reality.
At that time, all the vassal states were implementing reforms. Qin appointed Shang Yang, which made the country rich and powerful; Chu and Wei also appointed Wu Qi, who defeated some countries and weakened powerful enemies; King Wei and Xuan of Qi appointed Sun Bin and Tian Ji, etc., so that the country was strong, and all the vassal states came east to pay homage to Qi.
When the vassal states were working on the offensive strategy of "joining vertical and horizontal lines" and regarded those who were good at attacking as virtuous, Mencius praised the virtues of Tang Yao, Yu Shun, and the Xia, Shang and Zhou dynasties. Therefore, it did not meet the needs of the countries he traveled around.
So he returned to his hometown and worked with Wan Zhang and others to sort out the Classics and Shujing, elucidate Confucius' thoughts and doctrines, and write a book called "Mencius", a total of seven chapters.

He returned home to compile the *Mencius* with Wan Zhang.

中国名曰赤县神州。
赤县神州内自有九州，禹之序九州是也，不得为州数。
中国外如赤县神州者九，乃所谓九州也。
於是有裨海环之，人民禽兽莫能相通者，如一区中者，乃为一州。
如此者九，乃有大瀛海环其外，天地之际焉。
其术皆此类也。
然要其归，必止乎仁义节俭，君臣上下六亲之施，始也滥耳。
王公大人初见其术，惧然顾化，其後不能行之。
是以驺子重於齐。
適梁，惠王郊迎，执宾主之礼。
適赵，平原君侧行撇席。
如燕，昭王拥彗先驱，请列弟子之座而受业，筑碣石宫，身亲往师之。作主运。
其游诸侯见尊礼如此，岂与仲尼菜色陈蔡，孟轲困於齐梁同乎哉！
translation: 
It is called Chixian Shenzhou in China.
There are nine states within Chixian Shenzhou, which are the nine states arranged by Xia Yu in order, but they cannot be counted as the full number of states.
Outside of China, there are nine other places like Chixian Shenzhou. This is the so-called Kyushu.
There are small seas surrounding this place. People and animals cannot communicate with other states. It is like an independent area. This is one state.
There are nine states like this, and the sea surrounds it, which means it reaches the edge of heaven and earth.
Zou Yan's theories are all of this type of statement.
However, to sum up its essence, it must all boil down to benevolence, righteousness, frugality, and be implemented among the king, his ministers, and their relatives. However, the description at the beginning is indeed too rambling.
When princes and princes first saw his teachings, they were surprised and thought about it. They were inspired to learn it, but in the end they could not put it into practice.
Therefore, Zou Yan was respected in Qi State.
When he arrived in Wei State, King Hui of Liang met Gao Ying from afar and performed the courtesy of guest and host with him.
When he arrived at the State of Zhao, Lord Pingyuan accompanied him and personally brushed his seat for him.
When he arrived in Yan State, King Zhao of Yan took a broom to clear the road as a guide for him, and asked to sit on the disciple's seat to learn from him. He also built a Jieshi Palace for him and went to worship him as his teacher in person. He wrote the chapter "Main Movement".
Zou Yan traveled to various countries and received such courtesy and respect. How can this be the same as Confucius and Chen Cai who were starving when they had no food supplies, and Meng Ke was in trouble in Qi and Liang? How can it be the same?

summary:
summary line 1: After Mencius, Zou Yan emerged in Qi, observing princes’ decadence and developing theories on yin-yang and the five virtues (metal, wood, water, fire, earth). His writings, such as *The End and the Beginning*, were vast and speculative, tracing history from the present to before heaven and earth formed. He divided the world into nine continents like Chixian Shenzhou, surrounded by seas, and linked dynastic changes to cosmic cycles. Despite their grandiosity, his ideas emphasized benevolence and frugality, impressing rulers like King Zhao of Yan, who treated him with high respect—unlike Confucius and Mencius, who faced hardship.

故慎到著十二论，环渊著上下篇，而田骈、接子皆有所论焉。
驺奭者，齐诸驺子，亦颇采驺衍之术以纪文。
於是齐王嘉之，自如淳于髡以下，皆命曰列大夫，为开第康庄之衢，高门大屋，尊宠之。
览天下诸侯宾客，言齐能致天下贤士也。
荀卿，赵人。
年五十始来游学於齐。

故齐人颂曰： 谈天衍，雕龙奭，炙毂过髡。

齐尚脩列大夫之缺，而荀卿三为祭酒焉。
齐人或谗荀卿，荀卿乃適楚，而春申君以为兰陵令。
春申君死而荀卿废，因家兰陵。
李斯尝为弟子，已而相秦。

因葬兰陵。
而赵亦有公孙龙为坚白同异之辩，剧子之言；魏有李悝，尽地力之教；楚有尸子、长卢；阿之吁子焉。
自如孟子至于吁子，世多有其书，故不论其传云。
盖墨翟，宋之大夫，善守御，为节用。

translation: 
Therefore, they all wrote. Shen Dao wrote twelve treatises, Huan Yuan wrote the first and second chapters, and Tian Pian and Jie Zi also wrote treatises.
Zou Shi was one of several Zou disciples in Qi State. He used Zou Yan's theories to write articles.
At that time, the King of Qi admired these scholars very much, and appointed everyone from Chunyu Kun to the next to be senior officials. He built residences for them along the thoroughfares with high gates and high gates, to show his respect and preference for them.
In this way, we can attract guests from various vassal states and promote Qi as the best country to attract talented people from all over the world.
Xunqing is from the state of Zhao.
When he was fifty years old, he came to Qi to lobby and give lectures.
Zou Yan's teachings were tortuous, exaggerated and full of empty arguments; Zou Shi's articles were complete and thorough but difficult to implement; Chunyu Kun, if you stayed with him for a long time, you would often learn some penetrating remarks.
Therefore, the people of Qi praised them and said: "Zou Yan is the one who talks eloquently, Zou Shi is the one who is exquisite in craftsmanship, he is wise and eloquent, and Chunyu Kun is the one who talks endlessly."
Tian Pian and others all died during the reign of King Xiang of Qi. At this time, Xunqing was the oldest and most senior master.
At that time, the state of Qi was still filling the vacancy of the senior officials. Xun Qing served as the master of the Jixia bachelor's ceremony three times.
Later, someone in Qi State slandered Xun Qing, so Xun Qing went to Chu State, and Lord Chun Shen appointed him as the Lanling Order.
After the death of Lord Chun Shen, Xun Qing was dismissed from office and settled in Lanling.
Li Si was once his student and later served as prime minister in the Qin Dynasty.
Xun Qing hated the dark politics of the troubled times, and the chaotic monarchs appeared one after another. They did not understand the common sense and right ways, but were deceived by the witches who pretended to be gods.
After his death, he was buried in Lanling.
At that time, the State of Zhao also had Gongsun Long, who argued with Hui Shi's theory of "Li Jian Bai" and "Contracts and Variations", as well as the writings of Juzi; the State of Wei once had Li Kui, who proposed the idea of ​​encouraging farming to maximize the power of the land; the State of Chu once had Xizi and Changlu, and there was a Xuzi in Dong'e of Qi State.
From Mencius to Xuzi, many of their works have been circulated in the world, so I will not go into detail about the contents of these works.
Mo Zhai was a senior official in the Song Dynasty. He was good at guarding and defensive tactics and tried his best to advocate cost-saving.
Some people say that he was a contemporary of Confucius, while others say that he lived after Confucius.

summary:
summary line 2: Other scholars included Chunyu Kun of Qi, known for his wit and ability to read minds; he remained silent before King Hui of Liang until the king focused on governance, later conversing for three days but declining a prime minister post. Shen Dao, Tian Pian, and Jiezi wrote on Huang-Lao doctrines, while Zou Shi expanded on Zou Yan’s work. The King of Qi honored these scholars with high positions and grand residences to attract talent.
