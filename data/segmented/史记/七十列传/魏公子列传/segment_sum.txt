The young master heard that there were two talented and virtuous people in Zhao who were not engaged in politics. One was Mao Gong who was hiding among the gamblers, and the other was Xue Gong who was hiding in the hotel. The young master wanted to see these two people, but the two men hid and refused to see the young master.
When the young master found out where they were hiding, he walked quietly to interact with these two people. They both enjoyed their acquaintance and were very happy.
Lord Pingyuan learned about this situation and said to his wife: At first I heard that my wife's brother Wei Gongzi was an unparalleled sage, but now I heard that he was doing random things and associating with the gang of gamblers and hotel clerks. The young master is just an ignorant and reckless person.
Lord Pingyuan's wife told the young master these words.
After hearing this, the young master said goodbye to his wife and prepared to leave, saying: "I heard that Lord Pingyuan was virtuous, so I betrayed King Pingyuan and rescued Zhao State, which satisfied Lord Pingyuan's request."
Now I know that Mr. Pingyuan's interactions with others are just a bold act to show his wealth. He is not looking for wise men and talents.
Since I was in Daliang, I have often heard that these two people are talented. When I arrived in Zhao State, I was afraid that I would not be able to see them.
I am still afraid that they will not want me when I am dating them. Now Mr. Pingyuan actually regards dating them as a humiliation. Mr. Pingyuan is not worth getting to know.
So I packed my bags and prepared to leave.
The lady told Lord Pingyuan everything the young master said. After hearing this, Lord Pingyuan felt ashamed and took off his hat to the young master to apologize, resolutely retaining the young master.
When the guests of Lord Pingyuan heard about this, half of them left Lord Pingyuan and joined the young master. All the scholars in the world also went to the young master and joined his family.
Mr. Pingyuan's guests were so admired by his conduct that they all came to his door.

summary:
summary line 6: Fearing the king's wrath, the lord remained in Zhao. King Zhao offered five cities, but the lord, humbled by a retainer's rebuke, refused credit. He later befriended recluses Mao Gong and Xue Gong, angering Lord Pingyuan, who deemed them unworthy. The lord defended his actions, causing many of Pingyuan's retainers to defect to him.

Chunk 9: 
original: 
公子率五国之兵破秦军於河外，走蒙骜。
遂乘胜逐秦军至函谷关，抑秦兵，秦兵不敢出。
当是时，公子威振天下，诸侯之客进兵法，公子皆名之，故世俗称魏公子兵法。
秦王患之，乃行金万斤於魏，求晋鄙客，令毁公子於魏王曰： 公子亡在外十年矣，今为魏将，诸侯将皆属，诸侯徒闻魏公子，不闻魏王。
公子亦欲因此时定南面而王，诸侯畏公子之威，方欲共立之。
秦数使反间，伪贺公子得立为魏王未也。
魏王日闻其毁，不能不信，後果使人代公子将。
translation: 
The young master led the armies of the five vassal states and defeated the Qin army south of the Yellow River, causing the Qin general Meng Ao to flee.
Then they took advantage of the victory and pursued it as far as Hangu Pass, trapping the Qin army in Hangu Pass, so that they did not dare to leave the pass again.
At that time, the reputation of the young master shocked the world, and guests from various princely states presented their art of war. The young master put them together and signed his name, so it is commonly known as "Wei Gongzi's Art of War" in the world.
The King of Qin was worried that the young master would further threaten the state of Qin, so he used thousands of kilograms of gold to go to Wei for bribes, find Jin Bi's former retainers, and asked them to slander him in front of the King of Wei: The young master has been in exile for ten years and is now a general of Wei.
The young master will also take this opportunity to decide to become king. The princes are afraid of the young master's power and prestige, and are planning to come together to support him as king.
The Qin State carried out rebellion many times, using the spies of the Wei State in the Qin State, pretending not to know anything and asking them to congratulate the young master and ask if he had been established as the king of Wei.
The king of Wei heard these slanderous words about the young master every day and could not help but believe them. Later, he actually sent someone to replace the young master as general.

summary:
summary line 7: After ten years, Qin attacked Wei. The king begged the lord's return. Mao and Xue convinced him to go back. The lord became general, rallied five allied states, and defeated Qin at the Yellow River, pursuing them to Hangu Pass. His fame spread, and he compiled "Wei Gongzi's Art of War."

Chunk 10: 
original: 
公子自知再以毁废，乃谢病不朝，与宾客为长夜饮，饮醇酒，多近妇女。日夜为乐饮者四岁，竟病酒而卒。其岁，魏安釐王亦薨。
秦闻公子死，使蒙骜攻魏，拔二十城，初置东郡。
其後秦稍蚕食魏，十八岁而虏魏王，屠大梁。
高祖始微少时，数闻公子贤。
及即天子位，每过大梁，常祠公子。
高祖十二年，从击黥布还，为公子置守冢五家，世世岁以四时奉祠公子。
太史公曰：吾过大梁之墟，求问其所谓夷门。
夷门者，城之东门也。
天下诸公子亦有喜士者矣，然信陵君之接岩穴隐者，不耻下交，有以也。
名冠诸侯，不虚耳。
高祖每过之而令民奉祠不绝也。
translation: 
The prince himself knew that he was being deposed for slander again, so he excused himself from going to court because he was ill. He feasted with the guests at home all night long, drank hard liquor, and often hung out with women. He spent four years having fun day and night, and finally died of illness due to excessive drinking. In this year, King Wei Anli also passed away.
When the King of Qin received the news that the young master was dead, he sent Meng Ao to attack Wei, captured twenty cities, and began to establish Dongjun.
From then on, Qin gradually invaded Wei's territory like eating mulberry leaves. After eighteen years, it captured Wei Wangjia, massacred the soldiers and civilians of Daliang, and destroyed the capital.
When Emperor Gaozu of the Han Dynasty was in a low status, he heard many times that Mr. Wei was virtuous and talented.
After he ascended the throne and became emperor, he often went to worship the prince every time he passed by Daliang.
In the twelfth year of Emperor Gaozu of the Han Dynasty, he returned from the front line where he defeated the rebel general Yingbu. When he passed through Daliang, he settled five families for the young master to guard his tomb and let them worship the young master according to the four seasons every year from generation to generation.
Tai Shigong said: When I passed by the ruins of Daliang, I looked for the so-called Yimen.
It turns out that Yimen is the east gate of Daliang City.
There are indeed many gentlemen in the world who are hospitable and like people, but only Lord Xinling can make friends with those people who are hidden in every corner of society. It makes sense that he does not feel ashamed to make friends with the lower class.
His reputation far exceeds that of the princes, and it is indeed true.
Therefore, every time Gaozu passed through Daliang, he ordered the people to offer sacrifices to him without interruption.

summary:
summary line 8: Qin bribed slanderers to claim the lord aimed for kingship. The king believed them and replaced him. The lord feigned illness, indulged in wine and women, and died four years later. King Anli died the same year. Qin then conquered twenty Wei cities and eventually destroyed Wei.
