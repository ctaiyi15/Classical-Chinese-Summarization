Empress Dowager Lu was Gaozu's humble wife and gave birth to Emperor Xiaohui and his daughter, Empress Dowager Lu Yuan.
When Emperor Gaozu became the king of Han, he found Qi Ji in Dingtao. He loved her very much and gave birth to King Ruyi of Zhao Yin.
Emperor Xiaohui was kind and weak, but Gaozu thought he was not like him, so he often wanted to depose the prince and make Qi Ji's son Ruyi, thinking that Ruyi was similar to himself.
Qi Ji was favored and often followed Emperor Gaozu to Guandong. She cried day and night and wanted to make her son the crown prince to replace the original crown prince.
Empress Lu was getting older and often stayed behind. She rarely saw Gaozu, and their relationship became increasingly estranged.
Ruyi was granted the title of Queen Zhao, and almost replaced the prince many times. Fortunately, the ministers' remonstrances and Liu Hou's strategy prevented the prince from being abolished.
Empress Lu was a strong and resolute person. She assisted Emperor Gaozu in pacifying the world and killing ministers, mostly thanks to Empress Lu.
Empress Lu had two older brothers, both of whom were generals.
The eldest brother Zhou Luhou died in the line of duty, and his sons Lu Tai were granted the title of Lihou, Lu Chan was granted the title of Jiaohou; the second brother Lu Shizhi was granted the title of Jiancheng Marquis.
On April 25, the twelfth year of Emperor Gaozu's reign, he died in Changle Palace. The prince inherited the title of Emperor Gaozu and succeeded him as emperor.
At this time, Emperor Gaozu had eight sons: the eldest son Liu Fei, who was the brother of Emperor Xiaohui and had a different mother than Emperor Xiaohui, was named King of Qi; the rest were younger brothers of Emperor Xiaohui. Liu Ruyi, the son of Qi Ji, was named King of Zhao, Mrs. Bo's son Liu Heng was named King of Dai, the other sons of Ji's concubines were Liu Huifeng, King of Liang, Liu Youfeng, King of Huaiyang, Liu Changfeng, King of Huainan, and Liu Jianfeng, King of Yan.
Gaozu's younger brother Liu Jiao was granted the title of King of Chu, and his elder brother's son Liu Bi was granted the title of King of Wu.
Wu Chen, the son of Fan Jun Wu Rui, a hero who was not a descendant of the Liu family, was granted the title of King of Changsha.

summary:
summary line 0: Empress Dowager Lu was the humble wife of Emperor Gaozu and mother of Emperor Xiaohui and Princess Lu Yuan. Gaozu favored Concubine Qi, who bore King Ruyi of Zhao, and often considered deposing the kind and weak Crown Prince Xiaohui in favor of Ruyi. Empress Lu, growing estranged from Gaozu, was resolute and assisted in pacifying the realm and killing ministers. After Gaozu's death in 195 BCE, Xiaohui succeeded him.

When Prime Minister Zhao was summoned to Chang'an, he sent someone to summon King Zhao again.
King Zhao came and had not yet reached the capital.

The Queen Mother wanted to kill King Zhao, but could not find a chance.
In December of the first year of Emperor Xiaohui's reign, Emperor Xiaohui went out to hunt in the morning.
King Zhao is young and cannot get up early.
The queen mother heard that King Zhao was alone at home, so she sent someone to give him poisoned wine to drink.
When Emperor Xiaohui returned at dawn, King Zhao was already dead.
So Liu You, the king of Huaiyang, was moved to the throne of Zhao.
In the summer, an edict was issued to pursue Yi Lihou's father as Lingwuhou.
The Queen Mother cut off Mrs. Qi's hands and feet, gouged out her eyes, burned her ears with fire, gave her dumb potion, made her live in the toilet, and named her "Ren Pig".
After a few days, Emperor Xiaohui was asked to watch the pigs.
When Emperor Xiaohui saw it, he inquired and found out it was Mrs. Qi, so he burst into tears and became ill and couldn't get up for more than a year.

summary:
summary line 1: Empress Lu resented Concubine Qi and King Ruyi. She imprisoned Concubine Qi and summoned Ruyi to the capital. Emperor Xiaohui protected Ruyi, but during a hunt, the Queen Mother poisoned Ruyi. She then mutilated Concubine Qi, calling her a "human pig." Xiaohui, horrified, fell ill and abandoned court affairs, dying in 188 BCE. During his funeral, the Queen Mother cried without tears until ministers, following advice, appointed Lu family members as generals, securing her power.

translation: 
This incident is recorded in "Qi Mourning the Family of King Hui".
The King of Qi wrote to the princes and kings: Emperor Gao pacified the world and made his children kings. King Hui Hui made him the king of Qi.
When King Hui Hui passed away, Emperor Xiaohui sent Zhang Liang, the Marquis of Liu, to make me the King of Qi.
Emperor Xiaohui passed away and Empress Gao came to power. She was old and listened to the opinions of Lu's group and deposed the emperor without authorization. She also killed three Zhao kings one after another, abolished Liang, Zhao and Yan, and used the title of Lu's children as kings. Qi was also divided into four parts.
The loyal ministers gave advice, but the empress was confused and refused to accept it.
Now that Empress Gao has passed away, the emperor is still young and cannot govern the world, so he can only rely on ministers and princes. However, the Lu family prides itself on its position, gathers troops, uses its majesty to intimidate the loyal ministers of the princes, and orders the world under the pretext of imperial edicts. Therefore, the Liu family's ancestral temple is in danger.
I led the army to the court to eliminate those who should not be king.
When the Han court heard the news, Prime Minister Lu Chan and others sent Yingyin Marquis Guanying to lead an army to attack the King of Qi.
When Guan Ying arrived in Xingyang, he discussed with others that the Lu family had taken control of the army in Guanzhong and wanted to eliminate the Liu family and establish themselves as emperor.
If I defeat Qi's army now and go back to resume my life, this will further strengthen the Lu family's power.
So Guan Ying stationed troops in Xingyang and sent envoys to inform the king of Qi and the princes of other countries. We must unite with them, wait for Lu's rebellion, and get rid of Lu together.
After the King of Qi heard the news, he withdrew his army to the western border of Qi State, waited for the news, and acted as agreed.
Lu Lu and Lu Chan wanted to launch a rebellion in Guanzhong, but they were afraid of Suihou, Zhu Xuhou and others internally, and externally they were afraid of the armies of Qi and Chu. They were also worried about Guan Ying's rebellion, so they planned to wait until Guan Ying's army fought with Qi's army before launching a rebellion.
At this time, King Liu Tai of Jichuan, King Liu Wu of Huaiyang, King Liu Chao of Changshan, and King Lu Yuan, grandson of Empress Lu, who were nominally younger brothers of the Young Emperor, all did not return to the country due to their young age and lived in Chang'an.
Zhao Wang Changlu and Liang Wang Lu Chan each commanded troops and lived in the Northern and Southern Army. They were both from the Lu family.
No one among the princes and ministers felt that they would be able to save their lives.
Taiwei Jianghou Zhou Bo could not enter the military camp to take control of the army.
The Marquis of Qu Zhou, Li Shang, was old and sick, and his son Li Ji was friendly with Lu Lu.
Jianghou discussed the strategy with Chen Ping, the prime minister, and sent people to kidnap Li Shang, and asked Li Ji to deceive Lu Lu and say: Emperor Gao and Queen Lu jointly pacified the world. The Liu family was granted nine kings, and the Lu family was granted three kings. They were all agreed upon by the ministers. This matter has been notified to the princes, and they all think it is appropriate.
Now that the Queen Mother is dead and the Emperor is young, and you wear the seal of King Zhao, you do not return home quickly to defend the country, but as a general, you lead the army to stay here, and you are suspected by the ministers and princes.

summary:
summary line 2: The Queen Mother then ruled as regent, intending to make Lu family members kings. She consulted ministers; Wang Ling opposed, citing Gaozu's oath, while Chen Ping and Zhou Bo supported her. She deposed Wang Ling and promoted Chen Ping. She made Lu Tai King of Lu, and after his death, Lu Jia succeeded. She also made several of Xiaohui's sons kings, though they were not his biological children. The Queen Mother deposed and killed a young emperor who threatened rebellion, replacing him with Liu Yi, renamed Hong.

The King of Qi wrote to the princes and kings: Emperor Gao pacified the world and made his children kings. King Hui Hui made him the king of Qi.
When King Hui Hui passed away, Emperor Xiaohui sent Zhang Liang, the Marquis of Liu, to make me the King of Qi.
Emperor Xiaohui passed away and Empress Gao came to power. She was old and listened to the opinions of Lu's group and deposed the emperor without authorization. She also killed three Zhao kings one after another, abolished Liang, Zhao and Yan, and used the title of Lu's children as kings. Qi was also divided into four parts.
The loyal ministers gave advice, but the empress was confused and refused to accept it.
Now that Empress Gao has passed away, the emperor is still young and cannot govern the world, so he can only rely on ministers and princes. However, the Lu family prides itself on its position, gathers troops, uses its majesty to intimidate the loyal ministers of the princes, and orders the world under the pretext of imperial edicts. Therefore, the Liu family's ancestral temple is in danger.
I led the army to the court to eliminate those who should not be king.
When the Han court heard the news, Prime Minister Lu Chan and others sent Yingyin Marquis Guanying to lead an army to attack the King of Qi.
When Guan Ying arrived in Xingyang, he discussed with others that the Lu family had taken control of the army in Guanzhong and wanted to eliminate the Liu family and establish themselves as emperor.
If I defeat Qi's army now and go back to resume my life, this will further strengthen the Lu family's power.

After the King of Qi heard the news, he withdrew his army to the western border of Qi State, waited for the news, and acted as agreed.
