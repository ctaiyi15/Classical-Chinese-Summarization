於是诸侯乃即申侯而共立故幽王太子宜臼，是为平王，以奉周祀。
平王立，东迁于雒邑，辟戎寇。
平王之时，周室衰微，诸侯彊并弱，齐、楚、秦、晋始大，政由方伯。
四十九年，鲁隐公即位。
五十一年，平王崩，太子洩父蚤死，立其子林，是为桓王。
桓王，平王孙也。
桓王三年，郑庄公朝，桓王不礼。
五年，郑怨，与鲁易许田。
许田，天子之用事太山田也。
八年，鲁杀隐公，立桓公。
十三年，伐郑，郑射伤桓王，桓王去归。
二十三年，桓王崩，子庄王佗立。
translation: 
Guo Shi's father was a eloquent man who liked flattery and was greedy for wealth, but King You put him in high regard.
In addition, he deposed Empress Shen and removed the prince.
Marquis Shen was very angry and joined forces with Zhen and Quan Rong, who belonged to Xiyi, to attack King You.
King You raised beacon fire to recruit the armies of the princes, but none of them came.
Marquis Shen killed King You at the foot of Mount Li, captured Bao Si, and took away all Zhou's treasures before leaving.
At that time, all the princes came to Shenhou to jointly establish Yijiu, the prince of the former Youwang, as Pingwang, in order to maintain the Zhou Dynasty's sacrificial tradition.
King Ping ascended the throne and moved the capital east to Luoyi to avoid the invaders.
During the reign of King Ping, the Zhou royal family declined, and powerful states annexed weak ones. Qi, Chu, Qin, and Jin began to become powerful, and political orders often came from the hegemonic monarch.
In the forty-ninth year, Duke Yin of Lu came to the throne.
In the fifty-first year, King Ping passed away, and the prince's father died early, so he made his son Lin the king, who was King Huan.
King Huan is the grandson of King Ping.
In the third year of King Huan's reign, Duke Zhuang of Zheng came to court, but King Huan did not give him any courtesy.
In the fifth year, Zheng sent Wan to exchange Xu Tian with Lu.
Xutian is the land used by the emperor to worship Mount Tai.
In the eighth year, the state of Lu killed Duke Yin and established Duke Huan.
In the thirteenth year, when Zheng was attacked, Zheng shot and wounded King Huan, and King Huan fled back.
In the twenty-third year, King Huan died and his son, King Zhuang Tuo, ascended the throne.
