Emperor Xiaowen was the eldest son of Emperor Gaozu.
In the spring of the eleventh year of Emperor Gaozu's reign, Chen Xi's army was defeated and Dai was pacified. Emperor Xiaowen was made King of Dai and his capital was Zhongdu.
He is the son of Queen Mother Bo.
In the seventeenth year of his reign as King of Dai, it was the eighth year of the reign of Emperor Gao. In July of this year, Queen Gao passed away.
In September, Lu Chan and others from Empress Lu's family wanted to rebel and overthrow the Liu family's regime. The ministers annihilated the Lu family together and discussed summoning the king to come to the capital and establish him as emperor. This incident is recorded in "The Chronicles of Empress Dowager Lu".
Prime Minister Chen Ping, Taiwei Zhou Bo and others sent people to greet the king.
The king asked his ministers, Lang Zhongling, Zhang Wu and others for their opinions.
Zhang Wu and others suggested: The ministers in the Han Dynasty were all generals from the time of Emperor Gao. They were familiar with military affairs and were scheming and deceitful. Their real intention was not just that, they were just afraid of the power of Emperor Gao and Empress Dowager Lu.
Now that the Lu family has been annihilated and the capital has been bloodbathed, they are here to greet the king in name, but in fact they cannot be trusted.
I hope that the king will pretend to be ill, not go to the capital, and sit back and watch the situation change.
Lieutenant Song Chang said: The opinions of the ministers are all wrong.
When the corruption of the Qin Dynasty collapsed, the princes and heroes rose up at the same time, thinking that they could conquer the world. There were tens of thousands of people. However, it was the Liu family who finally ascended to the position of emperor. Those who competed in the world cut off the hope of becoming emperor. This is the first point.
Emperor Gao made his children kings, and the territories of the kingdoms were intertwined. This is what people call a clan that is as solid as a rock. Everyone in the world succumbed to the strength of the Liu family. This is the second point.
The rise of the Han Dynasty abolished the harsh decrees of the Qin Dynasty, simplified the laws, and benefited the people. Everyone lived peacefully and unshakably. This is the third point.
With the majesty of the Empress Dowager Lu, she established three of Lu's children as kings, who excelled in power and autocratic power. However, the Taiwei entered the Northern Army with integrity. When he called out, the soldiers all bared their left arms to support the Liu family and betrayed the Lu family. As a result, the Lu family was wiped out.

summary:
summary line 0: Emperor Xiaowen, the eldest son of Emperor Gaozu, was made King of Dai after Chen Xi's defeat in the spring of Gaozu's eleventh year, with his capital at Zhongdu. In the eighth year of Emperor Gao's reign, Empress Gao died, and the Lu family plotted rebellion. The ministers annihilated them and summoned the king to become emperor. The king consulted his ministers; Zhang Wu advised caution, suspecting the ministers' intentions, while Song Chang argued that the Liu family's rule was divinely ordained, the people were loyal, and the king, as the eldest surviving son of Emperor Gaozu, was the rightful choice. After divination showed a favorable omen, the king sent his uncle Bo Zhao to verify the ministers' sincerity. Confirmed, he traveled to Chang'an, where he was greeted by officials. He initially refused the throne three times but eventually accepted, becoming Emperor Xiaowen.

六年，有司言淮南王长废先帝法，不听天子诏，居处毋度，出入拟於天子，擅为法令，与棘蒲侯太子奇谋反，遣人使闽越及匈奴，发其兵，欲以危宗庙社稷。
群臣议，皆曰 长当弃市 帝不忍致法於王，赦其罪，废勿王。

长未到处所，行病死，上怜之。
後十六年，追尊淮南王长谥为厉王，立其子三人为淮南王、衡山王、庐江王。
十三年夏，上曰： 盖闻天道祸自怨起而福繇德兴。
百官之非，宜由朕躬。
今祕祝之官移过于下，以彰吾之不德，朕甚不取。
其除之。
五月，齐太仓令淳于公有罪当刑，诏狱逮徙系长安。
太仓公无男，有女五人。
太仓公将行会逮，骂其女曰： 生子不生男，有缓急非有益也！
其少女缇萦自伤泣，乃随其父至长安，上书曰： 妾父为吏，齐中皆称其廉平，今坐法当刑。
妾伤夫死者不可复生，刑者不可复属，虽复欲改过自新，其道无由也。
妾原没入为官婢，赎父刑罪，使得自新。
书奏天子，天子怜悲其意，乃下诏曰： 盖闻有虞氏之时，画衣冠异章服以为僇，而民不犯。
translation: 
In the sixth year, officials reported that Huainan King Liu Chang had abandoned the decrees of the late emperor and did not obey the emperor's edicts. The palace he lived in was not in compliance with the law.
The ministers discussed it and all said that Chang Liu should be executed on the street and his body exposed to the public. The emperor could not bear to deal with King Huainan according to the law, so he pardoned his sins and deposed him from the throne.
The ministers requested that King Huainan be exiled to the Qiongdu area of ​​Yandao, Shu County, and the emperor agreed.
Liu Chang died of illness on the road before he reached the place where he was exiled. The emperor took pity on him.
Later, in the sixteenth year, he honored Liu Chang, King of Huainan, with the posthumous title of King Li, and named his three sons King of Huainan, King of Hengshan, and King of Lujiang.
In the summer of the thirteenth year, the emperor said: I heard that disasters arise from resentment, and happiness arises from virtue. This is the law of God.
I should be personally responsible for the mistakes of all officials.
Now the officials praying secretly blame the ministers below, which makes my immorality even more obvious. I strongly disapprove of this approach.
It should be abolished.
In May, Taicang of Qi State ordered Chunyu Gong to commit a crime and should be punished. He was arrested and detained in Chang'an.
The Duke of Taicang had no boys, but five daughters.
When the Duke of Taicang was arrested and leaving, he scolded his daughter and said: "Whoever gives birth to a child but does not give birth to a boy has an urgent matter and it is of no use at all!"
His youngest daughter Tiying cried sadly alone and followed her father to Chang'an. She wrote a letter saying: My father is an official, and people in Qi state praise him for being honest and fair. Now he should be punished for breaking the law.
I am saddened that the dead cannot be resurrected, and those who are under punishment cannot join their limbs together again. Although I want to change my ways, there is no way out.
I am willing to be taken into the government as a slave to atone for my father's punishment so that he can change his ways.
Tiying's petition was sent to the emperor. The emperor felt pity for her and issued an edict: I heard that during the Yu family, criminals were painted on their clothes and hats to make them different from ordinary people, in order to humiliate the criminals and the people did not violate the law.

summary:
summary line 2: The emperor faced natural disasters, interpreting solar eclipses as divine warnings. He urged officials to correct his faults, reduced expenses, and promoted agriculture. He abolished laws against defamation to encourage free speech. He dealt with rebellions, such as that of the King of Jibei, and conflicts with the Xiongnu, often opting for defense over aggression. He showed leniency to the King of Huainan, exiling him instead of executing him, and later honored his sons. He abolished corporal punishment after Tiying's plea for her father, emphasizing education over harsh penalties.
