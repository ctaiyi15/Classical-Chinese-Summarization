项籍者，下相人也，字羽。
初起时，年二十四。
其季父项梁，梁父即楚将项燕，为秦将王翦所戮者也。
项氏世世为楚将，封於项，故姓项氏。
项籍少时，学书不成，去。
学剑，又不成。
项梁怒之。
籍曰： 书足以记名姓而已。
剑一人敌，不足学，学万人敌。
於是项梁乃教籍兵法，籍大喜，略知其意，又不肯竟学。
项梁尝有栎阳逮，乃请蕲狱掾曹咎书抵栎阳狱掾司马欣，以故事得已。
项梁杀人，与籍避仇於吴中。
吴中贤士大夫皆出项梁下。
每吴中有大繇役及丧，项梁常为主办，阴以兵法部勒宾客及子弟，以是知其能。
秦始皇帝游会稽，渡浙江，梁与籍俱观。
籍曰： 彼可取而代也。
梁掩其口，曰： 毋妄言，族矣！
梁以此奇籍。
籍长八尺馀，力能扛鼎，才气过人，虽吴中子弟皆已惮籍矣。

Xiang Ji, Xia Xiangren, named Yu.

Xiang Liang was once implicated in the Yueyang crime case, so he asked Cao Jiu, the warden of Qixian County, to write to Sima Xin, the warden of Yueyang, and the matter was settled.
Xiang Liang killed someone, and he and Xiang Ji went to Wuzhong to avoid their enemies.
None of the talented scholar-bureaucrats in Wuzhong could compare with Xiang Liang.
Whenever there was a large-scale military service or funeral in Wuzhong, Xiang Liang often took charge of the arrangements and secretly used military arrangements to arrange guests and children, so he understood everyone's abilities.
When the First Emperor of Qin visited Kuaiji and crossed Zhejiang, Xiang Liang and Xiang Ji went to watch it together.
Xiang Ji said: I can replace that emperor.
Xiang Liang covered his mouth and said, "Don't talk nonsense, or the whole clan will be beheaded!"
Xiang Liang therefore felt that Xiang Ji was different from ordinary people.
Xiang Ji is more than eight feet tall, can lift a cauldron, and is so talented that the children of Wuzhong already fear him.

秦二世元年七月，陈胜等起大泽中。

众乃皆伏。
於是梁为会稽守，籍为裨将，徇下县。

项梁乃以八千人渡江而西。
闻陈婴已下东阳，使使欲与连和俱西。

summary:
summary line 1: In July of Qin II's first year, Chen She rebelled in Daze Township. In September, Yin Tong, the governor of Kuaiji, proposed raising troops with Xiang Liang and Huan Chu. Xiang Liang, knowing Huan Chu's whereabouts, had Xiang Ji wait with a sword. After entering the governor's chambers, Xiang Liang called Xiang Ji in, who beheaded the governor. Xiang Yu killed over a hundred guards, and the rest submitted. Xiang Liang raised an army of 8,000 elite soldiers from Wuzhong, appointing local heroes as officers. He became governor of Kuaiji, with Xiang Ji as general. Zhao Ping of Guangling urged Xiang Liang to lead troops west to attack Qin. Xiang Liang crossed the river with 8,000 men, joined forces with Chen Ying of Dongyang, and gained 60,000-70,000 troops at Xiapi. He defeated Qin Jia, who had betrayed King Chen, and merged his army. After defeating Qin forces at Dong'a, Xiang Liang grew arrogant, ignoring Song Yi's warnings. Qin reinforced Zhang Han, who defeated and killed Xiang Liang at Dingtao.
