Taigong looked at Lu Shang, who was from the East China Sea.
His ancestors once served as officials in the Four Mountains, assisting Xia Yu in water management and made great contributions.
Shun and Yu were granted the title of Lu, and some were granted the title of Shen, with the surname Jiang.
During the Xia and Shang dynasties, some places in Shen and Lu were granted to descendants of other branches, and some descendants became commoners. Lu Shang is their descendant.
Lu Shang's original surname was Jiang. Because he took the name of his fiefdom, he was called Lu Shang.
Lu Shang was once very famous. When he was old, he took the opportunity of fishing to ask to see Zhou Xibo.
Before going out hunting, Xibo made a divination, and the hexagram said: The prey obtained is neither a dragon nor a chi, nor a tiger nor a bear; what he obtains is the assistant minister who will become the overlord.
So Xibohou went out hunting, and sure enough he met Taigong on the north bank of the Wei River. After talking with Taigong, Xibo was overjoyed and said: I have long heard the Taigong, the ancestor of our country, say: A saint will definitely come to Zhou, and Zhou will prosper.
That's you, right?
Our grandpa has been waiting for you for a long time.
So he called Lu Shang "Tai Gong Wang", and the two of them rode back together and respected him as military advisor.
Some people say that Taigong was very knowledgeable and once assisted King Zhou of Shang Dynasty.
Because King Zhou was unruly, Taigong left.
He went around lobbying the princes of various countries, but failed to find a king, so he finally went west to join Zhou Xibo.
Some people say that Lu Shang was a scholar who lived in seclusion by the sea.

summary:
summary line 0: Taigong Wang, originally named Lu Shang and surnamed Jiang, was a descendant of officials who assisted Yu in flood control during ancient times. In his old age, he met Zhou Xibo (later King Wen of Zhou) while fishing on the Wei River. Xibo, having received a divination that he would find an assistant to become overlord, recognized Lu Shang as the sage his ancestors had predicted would come to Zhou. He called him "Taigong Wang" and respected him as military advisor. Some accounts say Lu Shang had previously served King Zhou of Shang but left due to his unruliness, while others claim he was a recluse recruited by Xibo's ministers to help rescue Xibo from imprisonment. Regardless of the legends, all agree he became teacher and assistant to both King Wen and King Wu.
