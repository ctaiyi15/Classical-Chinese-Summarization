Qin besieged Handan, but Zhao was saved by reinforcements from Chu and Wei. Later, King Xiaocheng's son, King Daoxiang, and his grandson, King Youmiao, ruled as Qin's power grew. Li Mu, a capable general, won victories against Qin, but was killed due to slander. Finally, in 228 BCE, Qin captured Handan, ending the Zhao state. Zhao Jia, a brother of the last king, briefly ruled in Dai before being defeated, and Zhao was made into a Qin commandery.
