Zhao Gongxi has the same surname as the Zhou royal family, Ji.
After King Wu of Zhou destroyed King Zhou of Shang, he granted Duke Zhao a title in Northern Yan.
When King Cheng of Zhou was on the throne, Duke Zhao was in charge of the three Dukes: from the west of Shaanxi, Duke Zhao was in charge; from the east of Shaanxi, Duke Zhou was in charge.
At that time, King Cheng was still young, and Duke Zhou presided over the affairs of state on his behalf and held the power of the country, just like the emperor. Duke Zhao was suspicious of Duke Zhou's actions, so Duke Zhou wrote an article "Jun Shi" to express his confession.
Duke Zhao was still very dissatisfied with Duke Zhou.
Duke Zhou then praised the relevant historical facts of the Yin and Shang Dynasties and said: During the reign of Shang Tang, there was Yi Yin, whose merits reached heaven; during Taiwu's reign, there were people like Yi Zhi and Chen Hu, whose merits reached heaven, and Wu Xian managed the government; during Zu Yi's reign, there were people like Wu Xian; during Wu Ding's era, there were people like Gan Ban: these ministers all had the merit of assisting the king and presiding over governance, and the Yin Dynasty was governed and stabilized.
After hearing these words, Duke Zhao became happy.
Duke Zhao governed the western region and was deeply loved by the people.
When Zhao Gong went to inspect villages and towns, he saw a Tangli tree, and he judged lawsuits and handled political affairs under the tree. From marquises, counts to civilians, everyone has been properly accommodated, and there has been no neglect of duty.
After the death of Duke Zhao, the people missed his political achievements and the time when Duke Zhao handled political affairs under the Tangli tree. They were reluctant to cut down the trees and praised Duke Zhao, writing a poem called "Gantang".
