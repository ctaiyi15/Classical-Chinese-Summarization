summary line 0: Wei Shu, courtesy name Yangyuan, was born in Renchengfan. Orphaned young, he was raised by his maternal grandfather’s Ning family. A feng shui master predicted a noble nephew would be born, and Wei Shu’s intelligence led his grandmother to believe he fulfilled this prophecy. Tall and majestic, he was slow and simple, not valued by villagers. His uncle Wei Heng underestimated him, but Wei Shu remained unbothered. He enjoyed riding, archery, and hunting. Wang Yi of Taiyuan predicted he would become prime minister and supported him. Wei Shu once witnessed a prophecy about a boy who died at fifteen from an ax wound, which later came true. At forty, he studied for a hundred days, passed exams, and became Chief of Mianchi. He later served as Shangshu Lang, where he voluntarily resigned when incompetent officials were dismissed. He excelled in archery, surprising his superior Zhong Yu. As Prime Minister’s aide, he planned wisely on major issues. He appointed Zhou Zhen, who had been avoided due to a superstitious nickname, without disaster. He repeatedly resigned due to illness, eventually being allowed to retire as Juyangzi with honors. He died at eighty-two, posthumously named Kang. His son Wei Hun died young, and his great-nephew Wei Huang succeeded him.
