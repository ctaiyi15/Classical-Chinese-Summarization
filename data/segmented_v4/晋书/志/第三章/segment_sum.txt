translation:
In the third month of Guimao, the fourth year of Emperor Huangchu of Wei Dynasty, the moon violated the great star Antares.
Divination said: The heart is the location of the king of heaven, and the emperor hates this kind of astrology.
In June Jiashen, Venus appears during the day.
Liu Xiang's "Five Chronicles" said: Venus belongs to Shaoyin and is weak and cannot act independently, so it uses self-deficiency as a boundary and cannot move through the sky.
Day will appear after the movement of the sky. This astrological phenomenon heralds war, death, rebellion, and the change of monarchs; strong countries will become weak, and small countries will become strong.
When he was sent, Sun Quan accepted the title conferred by Emperor Wei, but he raised troops to resist and defend.
On December Bing of this year, the moon clashed with the great star Antares.
Ditto for divination.
In the tenth month of the fifth year of Huangchu, Venus appeared during the day, and the divination was the same as above.
Jupiter also entered the Taiwei Yuan and went retrograde, taking a total of 149 days to come out.
Divination says: If five stars enter Taiwei Yuan, and if they enter from the right for more than thirty days, the monarch will be in great trouble.
Another way to say it is: There will be forgiveness.
In May of the seventh year, Emperor Wen died, and Emperor Ming ascended the throne and granted amnesty to the world.
In the fifth month of the sixth year of Renxu, Mars entered Taiweiyuan and reached Renshen. It was close to Jupiter and violated the right law enforcement star. It was not until Guiyou that it left Taiweiyuan.
Divination says that if the person enters from the right for more than thirty days, the ruler will be in great trouble.
He also said: If the moon and five stars clash with the law enforcement stars on the left and right, the minister will be in trouble.
Another saying is: Those who enforce the law will be killed. This situation is particularly severe when Venus and Mars appear.
In November, the prince Cao Jian, king of Dongwuyang, died.
In the first month of the seventh year, Cao Hong, the hussar general, was exempted from civilian status.
In April, Xia Houshang, the general who conquered the south, died.
In May, Emperor Ming died.
"Shu Ji" records that Emperor Ming asked Huang Quan: The world is divided, who is the orthodox?
The answer is: It should be verified by celestial phenomena.
In the past, when Mars was in the heart, Emperor Wen passed away, but Wu and Shu were safe and sound, and creation was a test.
There is no record of Mars residing in the heart in the Three Kingdoms history books. It may be that Mars entered Taiweiyuan.
In August, Wu State besieged Jiangxia and invaded Xiangyang. General Xuan Emperor rescued Xiangyang and killed Wu general Zhang Ba and others.
In May of the fifth year of Emperor Taihe's reign, Mars invaded the house.
Divination says: There are four stars in the house, which are the positions of ministers and generals. If the moon and five stars oppose or reside here, the generals will be in trouble.
In July of this year, the chariot general Zhang He pursued Zhuge Liang and was killed by Zhuge Liang.
In December, Taiwei Hua Xin died.
In November of this year, the moon clashed with the star Regulus.
Divination said: The heroine is in trouble.
In the third month of the sixth year of the lunar calendar (Yihai), the moon clashed with the star Regulus again.
On Bingyin in November, Venus appeared in the Southern Dipper during the day, and it always appeared after more than eighty days.
The divination said: There will be a war in the state of Wu.
The next year, Sun Quan sent Zhang Mi and others to lead an army of tens of thousands. Emperor Ming granted Gongsun Wenyi the title of King of Yan. Wen Yi killed Zhang Mi and others and captured his army.

summary:
In the third month of the fourth year of Emperor Huangchu of Wei, the moon violated the great star Antares, a portent the emperor hated. In June, Venus appeared during the day, heralding war, death, rebellion, and changes of monarchs. This was fulfilled when Sun Quan accepted Wei's title but later resisted. In December, the moon again clashed with Antares. In the fifth year, Venus appeared during the day again, and Jupiter entered Taiwei Yuan retrograde for 149 days, foretelling great trouble for the monarch or forgiveness. In the seventh year, Emperor Wen died, and Emperor Ming ascended, granting amnesty.
