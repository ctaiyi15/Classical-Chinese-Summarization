Wang Zhang, from Taishan, was a blunt admonisher who slandered Zhongshu Ling Shi Xian, leading to his dismissal. Recalled by Emperor Cheng, he became Sili Xiaowei and later Jing Zhaoyin. He accused General Wang Feng of monopolizing power, leading to his execution for treason despite his wife's warnings. His family was exiled but later returned. He served for two years and was mourned by the people.
