胜使赐田臧楚令尹印，使为上将。

胜诛邓说。
胜初立时，氵夌人秦嘉、铚人董緤、符离人朱鸡石、取虑人郑布、徐人丁疾等皆特起，将兵围东海守于郯。
胜闻，乃使武平君畔为将军，监郯下军。

因矫以王命杀武平君畔。
章邯已破五逢，击陈，柱国房君死。
章邯又进击陈西张贺军。
胜出临战，军破，张贺死。
腊月，胜之汝阴，还至下城父，其御庄贾杀胜以降秦。
葬砀，谥曰隐王。
胜故涓人将军吕臣为苍头军，起新阳，攻陈，下之，杀庄贾，复以陈为楚。
初，胜令铚人宋留将兵定南阳，入武关。
留已徇南阳，闻胜死，南阳复为秦。
宋留不能入武关，乃东至新蔡，遇秦军，宋留以军降秦。
秦传留至咸阳，车裂留以徇。
秦嘉等闻胜军败，乃立景驹为楚王，引兵之方舆，欲击秦军济阴下。
使公孙庆使齐王，欲与并力俱进。
齐王曰： 陈王战败，未知其死生，楚安得不请而立王？
公孙庆曰： 齐不请楚而立王，楚何故请齐而立王！
且楚首事，当令于天下。
田儋杀公孙庆。
秦左右校复攻陈，下之。
吕将军走，徼兵复聚，与番盗英布相遇，攻击秦左右校，破之青波，复以陈为楚。
会项梁立怀王孙心为楚王。
陈胜王凡六月。
初为王，其故人尝与佣耕者闻之，乃之陈，叩宫门曰： 吾欲见涉。
宫门令欲缚之。
自辩数，乃置，不肯为通。
胜出，遮道而呼涉。
乃召见，载与归。
入宫，见殿屋帷帐，客曰： 夥，涉之为王沈沈者！
楚人谓多为夥，故天下传之 夥涉为王 ，由陈涉始。
客出入愈益发舒，言胜故情。
或言 客愚无知，专妄言，轻威 。
胜斩之。
诸故人皆自引去，由是无亲胜者。
以朱防为中正，故武为司过，主司群臣。
诸将徇地，至，令之不是者，系而罪之。以苛察为忠。
其所不善者，不下吏，辄自治。
胜信用之，诸将以故不亲附。
此其所以败也。
胜虽已死，其所置遣侯王将相竟亡秦。
高祖时为胜置守冢于砀，至今血食。
王莽败，乃绝。
项籍字羽，下相人也。
初起，年二十四。
其季父梁，梁父即楚名将项燕者也。
家世楚将，封于项，故姓项氏。
籍少时，学书不成，去；学剑又不成，去。
梁怒之。
籍曰： 书足记姓名而已。
剑一人敌，不足学，学万人敌耳。
于是梁奇其意，乃教以兵法。籍大喜，略知其意，又不肯竟。
梁尝有栎阳逮，请蕲狱掾曹咎书抵栎阳狱史司马欣，以故事皆已。
梁尝杀人，与籍避仇吴中。

Chen Sheng, courtesy name She, was from Yangcheng, and Wu Guang, courtesy name Shu, was from Yangxia. When Chen She was young, he worked as a hired farmhand. Once, while resting, he expressed frustration and told his fellow workers that if they became rich, they should not forget each other. They laughed at him, but Chen Sheng sighed, "How can a sparrow know the ambition of a swan?" In the autumn of the first year of Qin II, the government sent 900 people, including Chen Sheng and Wu Guang as village chiefs, to guard Yuyang. They encountered heavy rain in Qidaze Township, blocking the road and causing them to miss the deadline, which was punishable by beheading. Chen Sheng and Wu Guang discussed that escaping or rising up both led to death, so they decided to die for the state. Chen Sheng noted that the people had long suffered under Qin's harsh rule. He proposed pretending to be Fusu, the eldest son of the First Emperor, and Xiang Yan, a beloved Chu general, to gain support. Wu Guang agreed. They consulted a fortune teller who hinted at using ghosts and gods to gain prestige. They wrote "King Chen Sheng" on silk and hid it in a fish, which soldiers found. Chen Sheng also had Wu Guang imitate a fox's cry at night, shouting, "Great Chu is rising, King Chen Sheng." The soldiers were frightened and talked about it the next day. Chen Sheng and Wu Guang, known for their kindness, killed the two escorting generals after Wu Guang provoked them. Chen Sheng addressed the soldiers, saying that missing the deadline meant death, and that strong men should leave a great name. The soldiers agreed, and they staged an uprising, pretending to be Fusu and Xiang Yan. They attacked Daze Township, Qixian, and other counties, recruiting troops along the way. By the time they reached Chen County, they had six to seven hundred chariots, over a thousand cavalry, and tens of thousands of infantry. They captured Chen County, and the local elders declared Chen Sheng king, known as Zhang Chu. Many counties killed their Qin officials in response. Chen Sheng appointed Wu Guang as acting king to attack Rongyang, and sent others to capture Zhao, Jiujiang, and Wei.
