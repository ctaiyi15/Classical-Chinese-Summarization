The Chanyu accepted the surrendered people, placed them in Zuoguli, and sent an envoy to the Han Dynasty to report the situation and said: I have accepted them all.
The imperial edict sent the lieutenant generals Han Long and Wang Chang, the deputy captain Zhen Fu, the minister's visitor Bo Chang, and the Changshui captain Wang She as envoys to the Xiongnu, telling the Shanyu: The Western Region is under the jurisdiction of the Han Dynasty, and the Shanyu should not accept surrendered people. I want you to release them.
Shanyu said: Emperor Xiaoxuan and Emperor Xiaoyuan took pity on us and made an agreement with us that the area south of the Great Wall would belong to the Emperor of the Han Dynasty, and the area north of the Great Wall would belong to Shanyu.
If someone invades the frontier fortress, they must inform each other of the situation; if someone comes to surrender, they must not accept it.
My father, Huhanxie Shanyu, received immeasurable kindness from the Han Dynasty. Before he died, he left a message: If there are people who surrender from the Central Plains, they are not allowed to accept it. They must be sent back to the frontier fortress and returned to the Han Dynasty to repay the kindness of Emperor Mo. But this time the people who surrendered are from countries other than the Great Wall, I can accept it.
The envoy of the Han Dynasty said: At the beginning, the Xiongnu's relatives attacked each other, and the country was almost completely reduced. Thanks to China's great kindness, it supported the Chanyu, so that the Xiongnu who were in danger could continue to survive, and their families, wives and children could be kept safe. From generation to generation, the Xiongnu should repay the great kindness to the Han Dynasty.
After hearing this, the Shan Yu kowtowed to the envoy to apologize, and captured the two princes who had surrendered and handed them over to the Han Dynasty envoy.
The Han Dynasty issued an edict to send Zhonglang General Wang Meng to the border of Badunu in the Western Regions to meet and accept the returned people.

summary:
After Wang Mang's death, the Han Dynasty restored relations with the Xiongnu, but the chanyu remained arrogant. The historian concludes that peace treaties and military campaigns both had limitations, and the Xiongnu's nature made lasting peace difficult.
