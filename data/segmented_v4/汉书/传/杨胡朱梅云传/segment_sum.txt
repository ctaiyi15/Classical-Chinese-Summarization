Mei Fu studied in Chang'an, mastering the Book of Documents and Guliang's Spring and Autumn Annals. His advice was largely ignored. During Wang Mang's reign, Mei Fu abandoned his family and disappeared, later seen working as a market servant in Wu County under a false name.
