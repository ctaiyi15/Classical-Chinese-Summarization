translation:
Sima Xiangru, courtesy name Changqing, was born in Chengdu, Shu County.
When he was young, he loved studying and practiced fencing, so he was named Quanzi.
After Xiangru completed his studies, he admired Lin Xiangru's character and changed his name to Xiangru.
He used family funds to buy a Langguan, serve Emperor Jing of the Han Dynasty, and served as a regular attendant on martial arts, but this was not his hobby.
It happened that Emperor Jing did not like to write poems. At this time, King Xiao of Liang came to Beijing to see the emperor. Zou Yang from Qijun, Meicheng from Huaiyin, Yan Ji from Wuxian and other lobbyists came to Beijing with him.
When King Xiao of Liang died, Xiangru returned home. He was poor and had nothing to do.
He had always been on good terms with Wang Ji, the magistrate of Linyin County. Wang Ji said: "I have been looking for an official position abroad for many years and have not been very satisfied. You can come to me."
So Xiangru went to Linyin.
Live in a hotel outside the city. The county magistrate of Linyin showed his courtesy and visited Xiangru every day. Xiangru initially received him, but later claimed to be ill and asked his entourage to say goodbye to Wang Ji. Wang Ji became more cautious and respectful.
There were many rich people in Linyin City. Prince Zhuo and his wife had 800 slaves, and Cheng Yin also had several hundred slaves. The two of them called each other, "The county magistrate has a distinguished guest. We must hold a banquet to entertain him."
Also invite the county magistrate.
The county magistrate has arrived, and Zhuo's guests are counted in hundreds. At noon, Sima Changqing was invited. The Changqing said he was ill and could not go. The Linyin County magistrate did not dare to taste the food and went to greet Xiangru in person.
Xiangru has no choice but to do so.
He reluctantly went there, and everyone in the house admired his style. When the wine was in full swing, the Linyin County Magistrate came forward with the piano in his hands and said: "I heard privately that Chang Qing likes this, and hopes to play it to make himself happy."
Xiangru declined and played a tune or two.
At this time, Zhuo Wangsun had a daughter named Wenjun, whose husband had just died. She loved music, so Xiangru pretended to respect the county magistrate and teased her with the sound of the piano.
When Xiangru came to Lingongkou, he was accompanied by carriages and horses, and his behavior was generous and handsome. When he was drinking and playing piano at Huangdi's house, Sai Jun secretly peeked at him from the crack in the door. He was happy and admired him, and was worried that he would not be worthy of him.
After the piano playing was over, Xiangru asked Wenjun's waiter to reward him generously to express his personal feelings to her.
Wenjun escaped from home at night and eloped with Xiangru, so Xiangru and Wenjun drove back to Chengdu in a hurry.
The home is empty except for the four upright walls.
Zhuo Wangsun lost his temper and said: My daughter is extremely incompetent. I can't bear to kill her, but I will never give her a cent!
Some people tried to persuade Wang Sun, but Wang Sun still refused to listen.
After a long time, Wenjun felt unhappy and said: "Changqing just went to Linyin with me, and borrowing money from the brothers was enough to survive. Why did you put yourself in such hardship?"
Xiangru and Wenjun came to Linyin together, sold all the carriages and horses, and bought a hotel to start a wine business. He asked Wenjun to sit in front of the stove and sell wine.
When Zhuo Wangsun heard about it, he thought it was a great shame and humiliation, so he stayed behind closed doors.

summary:
Sima Xiangru, courtesy name Changqing, was born in Chengdu, Shu County. As a youth, he studied literature and practiced fencing. After completing his studies, he admired Lin Xiangru's character and changed his name accordingly. He purchased a government position serving Emperor Jing of Han, but found the role unsatisfying. Emperor Jing did not appreciate poetry, so when King Xiao of Liang visited the capital with scholars including Zou Yang and Meicheng, Xiangru associated with them. How does your king of Chu's hunting in the wild compare with me?
I jumped out of the car and said to King Qi: "I am the most despicable person in the Chu Kingdom. I have very little knowledge. I have been fortunate enough to be a guard in the palace for more than ten years. I often go hunting with the King of Chu. Most of the hunting grounds are in the back garden. I have been to some places in the garden, but I have never seen some places."

summary:
Later, Yang Deyi, a native of Shu serving as a dog keeper, praised "Zixu Fu" to Emperor Wu. When the emperor learned Xiangru wrote it, he summoned him. Xiangru confirmed this but offered to compose a hunting rhapsody for the emperor. He created three fictional characters—Zixu, Mr. Wuyou, and a deceased man—to discuss hunting. Zixu boasted of Chu's Yunmeng Marsh, describing its vast mountains, minerals, plants, animals, and the King of Chu's grand hunting expeditions. Mr. Wuyou criticized Zixu for boasting rather than praising virtue. The deceased man then described the emperor's Shanglin Garden, far surpassing anything in Qi or Chu, with its eight rivers, diverse terrain, abundant flora and fauna, and imperial hunts. He concluded by advocating frugality and virtue, warning against excessive luxury. The emperor was pleased and appointed Xiangru as a court gentleman.
