Li Guang's grandson Li Ling led a campaign against the Xiongnu with 5,000 infantry, fighting bravely but ultimately surrendering after running out of arrows and reinforcements. The emperor, misled by reports that Li Ling was training the Xiongnu, executed his family. Li Ling remained with the Xiongnu, marrying a Chanyu's daughter and dying there years later.
