Jiang Chong had grudges against the prince and the Wei family, and was worried that the prince would execute him after Emperor Wu of the Han Dynasty. Prime Minister Gongsun He and his son, the princesses of Yangshi and Zhuyi, and the Queen's brother's son Changping Houwei and his wife were all implicated and killed.
All these are recorded in "The Biography of Gongsun He" and "The Biography of Jiang Chong".
Jiang Chong was responsible for investigating the witchcraft case. He already knew the emperor's mood, so he told the emperor that there was a poisonous atmosphere in the palace. He entered the palace and went to the emperor's residence, demolished the throne and dug up the ground.
Emperor Wu of the Han Dynasty sent Hou Hanshuo, Yushi Zhanggan, Huangmen Suwen and others to assist Jiang Chong.
Jiang Chong went to the prince's palace to dig for poisonous things and found a little figure carved from paulownia wood.
At that time, Emperor Wu of the Han Dynasty was ill and went to Ganquan Palace to escape the summer heat. Only the queen and the prince were in the palace.
The prince summoned his young master Shi De and asked him what he should do about this matter. Shi De was afraid that he would be executed together because he was the master, so he said to the prince: Before this, the prime minister and his son, the two princesses, and the Wei family were all implicated and punished. Now the witch and the officials who came to investigate the matter dug up the ground and found the evidence. We don't know if the witch put it there intentionally, or if it was really there. We can't explain it ourselves. We can pretend to be an imperial edict and use talismans to arrest Jiang Chong and others in jail, and trace out their treacherous conspiracy.
Besides, the emperor is recuperating in Ganquan Palace, and the greetings from the queen and the prince's staff have not been answered. The emperor's life or death is unknown. And now that a traitor has done such a thing, prince, don't you remember the murder of Prince Fusu of the Qin Dynasty?
The prince was very anxious and accepted Shi De's opinion.
On Renwu day in the seventh month of the second year of Zhenghe, the prince sent a retainer to pretend to be a messenger and arrest Jiang Chong and others.
