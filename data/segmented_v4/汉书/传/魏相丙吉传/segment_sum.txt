original:
魏相字弱翁，济阴定陶人也，徙平陵。
少学《易》，为郡卒史，举贤良，以对策高第，为茂陵令。
顷之，御史大夫桑弘羊客诈称御史止传，丞不以时谒，客怒缚丞。
相疑其有奸，收捕，案致其罪，论弃客市，茂陵大治。
后迁河南太守，禁止奸邪，豪强畏服。
会丞相车千秋死，先是千秋子为雒阳武库令，自见失父，而相治郡严，恐久获罪，乃自免去。
相使掾追呼之，遂不肯还。
相独恨曰： 大将军闻此令去官，必以为我用丞相死不能遇其子。
使当世贵人非我，殆矣！
武库令西至长安，大将军霍光果以责过相曰： 幼主新立，以为函谷京师之固，武库精兵所聚，故以丞相弟为关都尉，子为武库令。
今河南太守不深惟国家大策，苟见丞相不在而斥逐其子，何浅薄也！
后人有告相贼杀不辜，事下有司。
河南卒戍中都官者二三千人，遮大将军，自言愿复留作一年以赎太守罪。
河南老弱万余人守关欲入上书，关吏以闻。
大将军用武库令事，遂下相廷尉狱。
久系逾冬，会赦出。
复有诏守茂陵令，迁杨州刺史。
考案郡国守相，多所贬退。
相与丙吉相善，时吉为光禄大夫，与相书曰： 朝廷已深知弱翁治行，方且大用矣。
愿少慎事自重，臧器于身。
相心善其言，为霁威严。
居部二岁，征为谏大夫，复为河南太守。
数年，宣帝即位，征相入为大司农，迁御史大夫。
四岁，大将军霍光薨，上思其功德，以其子禹为右将军，兄子乐平侯山复领尚书事。
相因平恩侯许伯奏封事，言： 《春秋》讥世卿，恶宋三世为大夫，及鲁季孙之专权，皆危乱国家。
自后元以来，禄去王室，政繇冢宰。
今光死，子复为大将军，兄子秉枢机，昆弟诸婿据权势，在兵官。
光夫人显及诸女皆通籍长信宫，或夜诏门出入，骄奢放纵，恐浸不制。
宜有以损夺其权，破散阴谋，以固万世之基，全功臣之世。
又故事诸上书者皆为二封，署其一曰副，领尚书者先发副封，所言不善，屏去不奏。
相复因许伯白，去副封以防雍蔽。
宣帝善之，诏相给事中，皆从其议。
霍氏杀许后之谋始得上闻。
乃罢其三侯，令就第，亲属皆出补吏。
于是韦贤以老病免，相遂代为丞相，封高平侯，食邑八百户。
及霍氏怨相，又惮之，谋矫太后诏，先召斩丞相，然后废天子。
事发觉，伏诛。
宜帝始亲万机，厉精为治，练群臣，核名实，而相总领众职，甚称上意。
元康中，匈奴遣兵击汉屯田车师者，不能下。
上与后将军赵充国等议，欲因匈奴衰弱，出兵击其右地，使不敢复扰西域。

translation:
The Prime Minister of Wei, whose courtesy name is Wenweng, was born in Dingtao, Jiyin, and later moved to Pingling.
When Wei Xiang was young, he studied the Book of Changes and became the governor of the county. He was praised as a virtuous person. Because he ranked first in answering policy questions, he became the magistrate of Maoling County.
Soon, the guests of Censor Da Sang Hongyang came to Maoling and falsely claimed that Sang Hongyang was coming to the guest house.
The county magistrate did not go to see the guest on time, so he tied up the county magistrate. Prime Minister Wei suspected that the guest was causing trouble, arrested him, tortured him and learned about his crime, and sentenced the guest to be executed in the street. Therefore, Maoling is managed very well.
Later, the prime minister of Wei was promoted to the governor of Henan, where he prohibited bad people from doing evil things, and the local powerful were very afraid of him.
It happened that the prime minister Che Qianqiu died at the time. Che Qianqiu's son had been the commander of the Luoyang Armory. He saw that his father was dead, and the Wei Prime Minister was very strict in managing the affairs of the county. He was afraid that he would be blamed for it in the long run, so he resigned from the official position.
Prime Minister Wei sent Sasuke's own deputies to chase him and call him back, but he finally did not come back.
Prime Minister Wei himself sighed regretfully: When the general resigned after hearing this arsenal order, he would definitely think that I was disrespectful to his son because the prime minister was dead.
It will also make those powerful people in the world blame me. It’s dangerous!
When Che Qianqiu, the son of an arsenal commander, arrived in Chang'an, General Huo Guang scolded Prime Minister Wei for this: The young new emperor had just ascended the throne and believed that Hangu Pass was the strongest place to defend the capital and the weapons depot was a place where sophisticated weapons were stored, so he made the prime minister's brother the captain of Hangu Pass and the prime minister's son the commander of the weapons depot.
Now the governor of Henan, Wei Xiang, did not think deeply about the country's plans, but just dismissed the prime minister's son when he saw his death. How shallow is this!
Later, someone accused Prime Minister Wei of killing an innocent person, and the matter was brought to the competent government office.
There were two to three thousand officers from the Henan garrison who stopped the general Huo Guang and said they were willing to stay for another year to atone for the sin of the prefect Wei Xiang.
More than 10,000 old and weak people in Henan guarded Hangu Pass and asked to enter the pass to write to the emperor. The officials guarding the pass reported the matter.
General Huo Guang handed Wei Prime Minister to Tingwei for punishment because of the arsenal order.
Prime Minister Wei was imprisoned for a long time. After the winter, it happened that the imperial court pardoned the prisoners.
There was also an edict ordering Prime Minister Wei to serve as the magistrate of Maoling County again and promote him to the governor of Yangzhou.
Later, the imperial court inspected the ministers of the county and state, and many of them were demoted.
Prime Minister Wei and Bing Ji were close. At that time, Bing Ji's official position was Doctor Guanglu. He wrote to Prime Minister Wei and said: The imperial court has a deep understanding of your achievements and behavior and will appoint you.
I hope you will be cautious and self-respecting in everything you do and cultivate your talents.
Prime Minister Wei thought Bingji's words were right, so he restrained his dignity.
After serving as governor for two years, he was recruited as an admonishing official and then became the governor of Henan.
A few years later, Emperor Xuan ascended the throne and recruited Wei Xiang to join the imperial court as chief minister of agriculture, and was later promoted to imperial censor.
Four years later, the general Huo Guang died. The emperor missed his merits and virtues and made his son Huo Yu the right general. Huo Guang's nephew Huoshan, the Hou of Leping, was in charge of the affairs of the Shangshu Province.
Prime Minister Wei wrote to the emperor through Ping'en Hou Xu Bo, saying: "Spring and Autumn" ridicules people who have been ministers for generations, hates that all the three generations of Song Dynasty were officials, and even Lu Jisun's autocratic rule has put the country in danger and chaos.
Since the first year of Emperor Wu's reign, the royal family's children could receive salaries, but the country's political affairs had to be decided by Tsukasa.
Now that Huo Guang is dead, his son has become a general again, his brother's son has become a minister and holds political positions, and his brothers and sons-in-law have military power and are very powerful.
Huo Guang's wife Xian and their female relatives are all registered in Changxin Palace and can come and go freely. Sometimes they go in and out through the forbidden gate at night. They are arrogant, extravagant, and uninhibited. I am afraid that they will gradually lose control in the future.
We should try to weaken their power and dispel their conspiracy, so as to strengthen the foundation of the Han Dynasty for generations and preserve the reputation of the hero Huo Guang.
In addition, according to past regulations, anyone who wrote a letter to the emperor must make two copies of the memorial, one of which was a copy. The official in charge of the affairs of the Shangshu Province would read the copy first. If the content of the memorial was inappropriate, it would be shelved and not reported to the emperor.
Prime Minister Wei used Uncle Xu to remove the copy to avoid being blocked by Huo's people.
Emperor Xuan read the memorial of Prime Minister Ding and Wei and thought it was very good. He issued an edict to confer Prime Minister Wei on the throne and fully adopted Prime Minister Wei's memorial.
Only then did the emperor find out about Huo's murder of Queen Mother Xu.
So Huo Yu, Huo Yun, and Huo Shan were deposed from their titles and ordered to return to their mansions. Their relatives were moved out to serve as officials.
At this time, Wei Xian was dismissed from the post of prime minister because of old age and illness. Wei Xian then became prime minister in Wei Xian's place, and was named Gaopinghou and granted food to 800 households in the city.
Later, the people of the Huo family resented Prime Minister Wei and were afraid of him. They planned to pretend that the Queen Mother had an edict, summon Prime Minister Wei to kill him, and then depose the emperor.
The matter was discovered and Huo's people were killed.
Only then did Emperor Xuan begin to personally handle the political affairs of the court, working hard to govern, selecting good ministers, and assessing their reputation. As prime minister, Wei Xiang was in charge of the affairs of various government offices, which was very popular with the emperor.
During the Yuankang period of Emperor Xuan, the Xiongnu sent troops to attack the Han army stationed in Cheshi, but they failed to capture it.
Emperor Xuan discussed with General Tang Chongguo and others, planning to take advantage of the Xiongnu's weakness to send troops to attack the area on their right, so that the Xiongnu would not dare to harass the western regions again.

summary:
Wei Xiang, courtesy name Wenweng, was a native of Dingtao, Jiyin, later moving to Pingling. He studied the Book of Changes in his youth, became a county governor, and was praised for his virtue. After ranking first in policy questions, he became magistrate of Maoling County. When a guest of Censor Sang Hongyang falsely claimed Sang was arriving and tied up the magistrate, Wei Xiang suspected foul play, arrested the guest, tortured him to reveal his crime, and executed him in the street, leading to excellent governance in Maoling. Promoted to governor of Henan, he prohibited evil deeds, instilling fear in local powers. When Prime Minister Che Qianqiu died, his son, commander of the Luoyang Armory, resigned fearing Wei Xiang’s strictness. Wei Xiang regretted this, fearing it would cause resentment. General Huo Guang later scolded Wei Xiang for dismissing the prime minister’s son after his death, calling it shallow. Accused of killing an innocent person, Wei Xiang faced investigation, but over 10,000 Henan residents petitioned for him. Huo Guang handed him to Tingwei for punishment, but he was pardoned and reappointed as magistrate of Maoling, then governor of Yangzhou. After demotions in an imperial inspection, Wei Xiang, close to Bingji, heeded his advice to restrain his dignity. He served as governor for two years, then became an admonishing official and later governor of Henan again. After Emperor Xuan ascended, Wei Xiang became chief minister of agriculture, then imperial censor. Four years later, Huo Guang died. Wei Xiang wrote to the emperor, warning against the Huo family’s power, citing historical precedents of autocratic rule causing chaos. He suggested weakening their influence to strengthen the Han dynasty. Emperor Xuan adopted his advice, deposing Huo Yu, Huo Yun, and Huo Shan. Wei Xiang became prime minister, replacing Wei Xian, and was named Gaopinghou. The Huo family later plotted to kill him but were discovered and executed. Emperor Xuan then personally governed, selecting good ministers, and Wei Xiang was popular as prime minister. During the Yuankang period, when the Xiongnu attacked Han forces, Wei Xiang advised against military retaliation, citing border poverty and internal social issues like 222 cases of familial killings. He urged focus on domestic stability. Emperor Xuan followed his advice. Wei Xiang, well-versed in the Book of Changes, studied Han history and advocated following past systems. He presented 23 items from the late emperor’s edicts, emphasizing agriculture, grain storage, and frugality to prepare for disasters. He warned of current grain price hikes and border conflicts, urging the emperor to care for the people. Emperor Xuan implemented his suggestions. Wei Xiang also presented chapters on Yin-Yang and seasonal governance, arguing that proper seasonal management prevents disasters and ensures prosperity. He recommended selecting ministers knowledgeable in Yin-Yang to oversee seasonal affairs. The emperor adopted his proposals. Wei Xiang’s commissioners reported irregularities from counties, often revealing unreported rebellions or disasters. He was severe and resolute, unlike the tolerant Bingji. He served as prime minister for nine years, dying in the third year of the Shenjue era, and was posthumously named Xianhou. His son Wei Hong inherited the title but was demoted to Marquis of Guannei during the Ganlu era due to a crime.
