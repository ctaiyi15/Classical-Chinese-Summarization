Emperor Gaozu personally went to attack him and named his son Liu Chang the King of Huainan.
In October of the twelfth year, Emperor Gaozu defeated Yingbu's army in Huizhen, and Yingbu fled. Gaozu ordered other generals to pursue him.
Emperor Gaozu led his army back, passed by Peixian County, and stopped.
A banquet was held in Peigong, and all the friends, fathers and children from the past were called together to have a feast. Selecting Peizhong children, one hundred and twenty were obtained and taught them to sing.
After drinking to his heart's content, Emperor Gaozu was building a building, composed a poem by himself, and sang: The strong wind is blowing, the clouds are flying, the sea is full of power, we are returning to our hometown, and the warriors are safe and guarding the four directions!
Let the children learn to sing along.
Emperor Gaozu danced again, feeling sad and tearful, and said to his father and brothers in Peixian County: People who travel far away miss their hometown.
Although I have established my capital in Guanzhong, my soul will still miss Sipei County for thousands of years to come.
I started as Pei Gong, punished violence and rebelled, and finally conquered the world. I regard Peixian County as my Tangmuyi, exempting the people of Peixian County from corvee service, and they will not have to do corvee service for generations to come.
The fathers, brothers, elders, women, and old friends in Peixian County drank and drank happily every day, talking about old ways and making fun of themselves.
After more than ten days, Gaozu wanted to leave, but the elders and brothers in Peixian County insisted on keeping Gaozu.
Emperor Gaozu said: I have so many followers that my father and brothers cannot afford to support them.
So Gaozu set out.
The people of Peixian County came out in large numbers and went to the west of the city to contribute cattle and wine.
Emperor Gaozu stayed again, pitched a tent, and feasted for three days.
The fathers and brothers of Pei County all kowtowed and asked, "Pei County is fortunate enough to be exempted from the corvee, but Fengyi has not yet been exempted from the corvee. Please show mercy to Fengyi."
Emperor Gaozu said: Fengyi is the place where I grew up, and I will never forget it. I only went to help the Wei state because Fengyi rebelled against me because of Yong Chi. The fathers and brothers of Peixian County insisted on their request, and then they exempted Fengyi from the corvee. Like Peixian County, they made Peihou Liu Bi the king of Wu.
The generals of the Han army pursued Yingbu's army on both the north and south sides of the Taoshui River. They defeated Yingbu's army and captured and killed Yingbu in Poyang.
Fan Kuai led another army to pacify Daidi and killed Chen Xi in the city.
In November, Emperor Gaozu returned to Chang'an from the army that conquered Yingbu.
In December, Emperor Gaozu said: The First Emperor of Qin, King Chen She of Chu Yin, King Anli of Wei, King Qi Min, and King Zhao Daoxiang all died without heirs, and were given to ten families to guard their tombs, twenty families of Qin Shihuang, and five families of Wei Gongzi Wuji.
All local officials and people who were coerced by Chen Xi and Zhao Li were pardoned.
When Chen Xi's surrendered generals said that Chen Xi was rebelling, King Lu Wan of Yan sent people to Chen Xi to participate in the conspiracy.
Emperor Gaozu sent Marquis Piyang to pick up Lu Wan, but Lu Wan refused to come because he was ill.
Marquis Piyang came back and explained in detail that there were signs of Lu Wan's rebellion.
In February, Fan Kuai and Zhou Bo were sent to lead an army to attack Lu Wan, King of Yan. Pardon the officials and people of Yan who participated in the rebellion.
The prince Liu Jian was granted the title of King of Yan.
When Emperor Gaozu attacked Yingbu, he was shot by a stray arrow and fell ill during the march.

summary:
After establishing the Han Dynasty, Gaozu faced rebellions from various kings including Zang Tu, Han Xin, Chen Xi, and Yingbu. He personally led campaigns against them, executing or defeating the rebels. During a visit to his hometown Peixian County, Gaozu composed the famous Song of the Great Wind and exempted the county from corvée service. He died from an arrow wound sustained during the campaign against Yingbu, refusing medical treatment by declaring his fate determined by heaven. Before death, he designated successors for key positions, including Cao Shen, Wang Ling, Chen Ping, and Zhou Bo. Gaozu was buried in Changling with the posthumous title Emperor Gao, having established the Han Dynasty from humble beginnings.
