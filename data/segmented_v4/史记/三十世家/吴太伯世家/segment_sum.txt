summary:
King Fuchai avenged his father by defeating Yue at Fujiao. King Gou Jian of Yue was forced to hide in Kuaiji Mountain and sought peace through the Grand Prime Minister Bo Pei. Wu Zixu advised against this, comparing Gou Jian to Shaokang, who restored the Xia dynasty from a small territory. Fuchai then focused on northern campaigns against Qi, ignoring Zixu's warnings about the greater threat from Yue. While Fuchai was at the Huangchi meeting vying for supremacy with Jin, Yue attacked Wu, capturing the crown prince. After the meeting, Fuchai sought peace with Yue. Eventually, Yue grew stronger, defeated Wu in battle, and besieged its capital. King Fuchai, regretting not listening to Zixu, committed suicide. The King of Yue destroyed Wu and killed Dazai Bo Pei for his disloyalty.
