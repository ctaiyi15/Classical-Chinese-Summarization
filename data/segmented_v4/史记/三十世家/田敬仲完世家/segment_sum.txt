厉公既立，娶蔡女。
蔡女淫于蔡人，数归，厉公亦数如蔡。
桓公之少子林怨厉公杀其父与兄，乃令蔡人诱厉公而杀之。
林自立，是为庄公。
故陈完不得立，为陈大夫。
厉公之杀，以淫出国，故《春秋》曰 蔡人杀陈他 ，罪之也。
庄公卒，立弟杵臼，是为宣公。
宣公二十一年，杀其太子御寇。
御寇与完相爱，恐祸及己，完故奔齐。
齐桓公欲使为卿，辞曰： 羁旅之臣幸得免负檐，君之惠也，不敢当高位。
桓公使为工正。
齐懿仲欲妻完，卜之。占曰： 是谓凤皇于蜚，和鸣锵锵。
有妫ī之后，将育于姜。
五世其昌，并于正卿。
八世之后，莫之与京。
卒妻完。
完之奔齐，齐桓公立十四年矣。
完卒，谥为敬仲。
仲生稚孟夷。
敬仲之如齐，以陈字为田氏。
田稚孟夷生愍孟庄，田愍孟庄生文子须无。
田文子事齐庄公。
晋之大夫栾逞作乱于晋，来奔齐，齐庄公厚客之。
晏婴与田文子谏，庄公弗听。
文子卒，生桓子无宇。

translation:
Chen Wan is Chen Ligong's son.
When Wan was born, Taishi of Zhou happened to pass by the state of Chen. Chen Ligong asked him to divine divination for Chen Wan. The hexagram obtained was "Guan Gua" changed to "No Gua". Taishi said: The meaning of the hexagram is: observing the customs and sentiments of the country is helpful for being the king's guest.
Does this mean that he will obtain the throne of Chen and own the country?
Maybe it's not in Chen country but in another country?
Or it may not be fulfilled on others, but be fulfilled on his descendants.
If it is in another country, it must be the country with the surname Jiang.
The surname Jiang is a descendant of the Four Mountains during the reign of Emperor Yao.
It is impossible for two things to be powerful at the same time. After the decline of Chen State, his branch will prosper!
Li Gong is the youngest son of Chen Wengong, and his mother is a woman from Cai State.
After Duke Wen died, Duke Li's brother Chen Bao came to the throne, and this was Duke Huan.
Duke Huan and his younger brother Chen were not born from the same mother.
When Duke Huan was ill, the people of Cai killed Duke Huan Chen Bao and Prince Chen Min on behalf of Chen Ta, and made Chen Ta the king. This is Duke Li.
After Li Gong came to the throne, he married a woman from Cai State.
This Cai woman committed adultery with a Cai man and returned to Cai many times. Duke Li also frequently went to Cai.
Chen Lin, the youngest son of Duke Huan, resented Duke Li for killing his father and brother, so he asked the people of Cai to trick Duke Li and kill him.
Chen Lin established himself as king, this is Duke Zhuang.
Therefore, Chen Wan could not be established as the king, but only as the official of the state of Chen.
Duke Li was killed because he went abroad for promiscuity, so the Spring and Autumn Annals said that "Cai people killed Chen Ta", which was an accusation against him.
After Zhuang Gong died, his younger brother Chujiu came to the throne. This was Chen Xuangong.
In the 21st year of Xuangong's reign, Chujiu killed the prince Yu Kou.
Yu Kou and Chen Wan were close, and he was afraid that the disaster would involve him, so Chen Wan fled to Qi State.
Duke Huan of Qi wanted to appoint him as minister, but he declined and said: "As a humble minister living abroad, I am fortunate enough to be exempted from all kinds of burdens. It is already a favor given to me by you. I dare not hold such a high position again."
Duke Huan of Qi appointed him as Gongzheng in charge of hundreds of workers.
Qi Yizhong wanted to marry his daughter to Chen Wan, so he conducted a divination for this matter. The result of the divination said: This is called the flying phoenix, with a harmonious sound.
The descendants of the Gui family will grow up under the Jiang family.
After the Five Dynasties, he will be prosperous and have the same status as Zhengqing.
After eight generations, no one can be higher than him.
He finally married his daughter to Chen Wan.
When Chen Wan fled to Qi, Duke Huan of Qi had been in power for fourteen years.
After Chen Wan's death, his posthumous title was Jingzhong.
Jingzhong gave birth to Yu Mengyi.
After Jingzhong arrived in Qi State, he changed the Chen family name to the Tian family name.
Tian Zhen Meng Yi gave birth to Min Meng Zhuang, and Tian Min Meng Zhuang gave birth to Wen Zi Xu Wu.
Tian Wenzi served Duke Zhuang of Qi.
Luan Cheng, a senior official of the Jin State, caused trouble in the Jin State and fled to the Qi State. Duke Zhuang of Qi gave him generous treatment.
Yan Ying and Tian Wenzi advised him, but Duke Zhuang did not listen.
Tian Wenzi died, and his son was Huanzi Wuyu.

summary:
The historian concludes that the Tian family’s rise, from Chen Wan’s flight to Qi to their seizure of power, may have been guided by the divinations of the Zhou priest and Yizhong, fulfilling prophecies over generations.
