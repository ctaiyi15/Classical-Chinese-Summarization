Less than seven feet tall, he is funny and eloquent. He has been on many missions to the princes and has never been humiliated.
When King Wei of Qi was in power, he liked to talk in argot, feast all night long, indulge in excessive drinking, ignored state affairs, and entrusted political affairs to the officials.
The civil and military officials are dissolute and indulgent, and the princes and countries invade and invade. The country is in danger and is in danger in a matter of seconds. None of the ministers around King Qi dared to give advice.

So he ordered all the chiefs of the seventy-two counties in the country to come to the court to report. They rewarded one person and killed another. They also sent troops to defend against the enemy. The princes and states were shocked and returned the occupied land to Qi.
Qi's prestige lasted for thirty-six years.
All these things are recorded in "Tian Wan Family".
In the eighth year of King Wei of Qi's reign, the State of Chu sent a large army to invade its borders.
The King of Qi sent Chunyu Kun as an envoy to the State of Zhao to request reinforcements, and asked him to carry a gift of a hundred catties of gold and ten four-horse carriages.
Chunyu Kun looked up to the sky and laughed so hard that the string holding his hat broke.
King Wei said: Sir, do you think the gift is too little?
Chunyu Kun said: How dare you think too little!
King Wei said: Then you laugh, do you have any excuse?
Chunyu Kun said: Today I came from the east and saw a man worshiping the God of the Fields by the roadside. He held a pig's trotter and a glass of wine and prayed: "The grain harvested in the highlands fills the bonnet, and the crops harvested in the low fields fill the vehicles; the grains are lush and ripe, and the rice grains are piled up in the warehouse."
I saw that he brought few sacrifices and prayed for many things, so I laughed at him.
So King Wei of Qi increased the gifts to a thousand pieces of gold, ten pairs of white jade stones, and a hundred four-horse carriages.
Chunyu Kun said goodbye and set off for the Kingdom of Zhao.
King Zhao allocated him 100,000 elite soldiers and 1,000 leather-wrapped chariots.
When the Chu State heard the news, they withdrew their troops overnight.
King Qi Wei was very happy. He set up wine and food in the harem, summoned Chunyu Kun and gave him wine to drink.
Ask him: How much wine can you drink before you get drunk?
Chunyu Kun replied: I can get drunk even if I drink a dou of wine, and I can get drunk even if I drink a stone of wine.
