Chen Xi was a native of Liang. When he was young, he always praised and admired Wei Gongzi Xinlingjun. Later, he led the army to guard the border, gathered guests, and treated virtuous corporals, and his reputation exceeded his actual status.
Zhou Chang doubted him, and many mistakes occurred from here. Fearing that disaster would come, the treacherous villain took the opportunity to speak again, and finally fell into a treasonous situation.
Oh, how sad!
It can be seen from this that the maturity and success or failure of the plan have a profound impact on a person!
