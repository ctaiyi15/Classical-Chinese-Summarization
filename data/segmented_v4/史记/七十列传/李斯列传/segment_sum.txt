When he was young, he worked as a minor official in the county. He saw rats eating dirty things in the toilet near his office. Whenever someone or a dog came near, he would be frightened and run away.

So Li Si sighed with emotion: A person's talent or incompetence is determined by his environment, just like a mouse.
So Li Si learned from Xunzi how emperors governed the world.

Being in a humble position and not thinking about seeking fame and wealth is like an animal. It only wants to eat meat when it sees it. It only has a human face but can barely walk.
Therefore, the greatest shame is humble status, and the greatest sorrow is poverty.
Being in a humble position and difficult situation for a long time, yet still criticizing society, hating fame and wealth, and slandering oneself as uncontested is not the original wish of a scholar.
So I am going west to lobby the King of Qin.
After Li Si arrived in Qin, just as King Zhuangxiang of Qin passed away, Li Si asked to be a retainer of Lu Buwei, the prime minister of Qin. Lu Buwei appreciated him very much and appointed him Lang Guan.
As a result, Li Si got the opportunity to lobby. He said to the King of Qin: Mediocre people often lose opportunities, but those who can achieve great achievements are those who can take advantage of opportunities and be ruthless.
In the past, although Duke Mu of Qin dominated the world, he did not move eastward and annex the six kingdoms of Shandong. What was the reason?
The reason was that the number of princes was still large and the prestige of the Zhou Dynasty had not completely declined. Therefore, the five hegemons emerged alternately and successively promoted the Eastern Zhou royal family.
Since Qin Xiaogong, the royal family of the Eastern Zhou Dynasty has been weak and weak, and the princes have merged with each other. The area east of Hangu Pass has been transformed into six kingdoms. Qin State took advantage of the victory to gradually conquer the six kingdoms. It has been about six generations now.
Nowadays, the princes obey Qin just like the counties obey the imperial court.
With the power of Qin and the wisdom of the king, just like sweeping away the dust on the stove, it is enough to wipe out the princes, achieve the emperor's cause, and realize the unification of the world. This is the only opportunity that comes once in a lifetime.
If we are slack now and do not take action on this matter, when the princes become stronger again and enter into alliance alliances, even if they are as capable as Huang Di, they will not be able to annex them.
Qin Shihuang appointed Li Si as the chief historian, followed his plan, and secretly sent counselors to various countries with gold and jade treasures to lobby.
If famous figures from various countries can be bribed, they will be bribed with gifts; if they cannot be bribed, they will be killed with sharp swords.
