Ding Jinlin said: In the past, Sun Shuao told his son that when he was granted a title, he must seek a barren land. Now that I can do a small job, it will be great to get a rural pavilion.
The emperor obeyed and named him Marquis of Xin'an Township, Dingling, with a settlement of 5,000 households. Later, he was renamed Marquis of Lingyang.
When Ding Hongnian was 13 years old, he studied "Ouyang Shangshu" from Huan Rong. In three years, he understood the chapters and sentences, could debate and speak for everyone, so he determined to further his studies, put on civilian clothes, carried his luggage, and traveled thousands of miles to study.
At first, Ding Jinlin followed Shizu in the expedition, and Ding Hong lived with his younger brother Ding Sheng. They loved Ding Sheng when he was young and lived a poor life together.
After Ding Jinlin's death, Ding Hong was granted the hereditary title and wrote a letter to transfer the country to Ding Sheng, but received no reply.
His father had been buried, so he hung mourning clothes in the tomb and slipped away, leaving a message to Ding Sheng: "Hong was greedy for scriptures. He ignored his kindness and righteousness. He studied with his teacher when he was young. He did not support his parents during his life. He could not fulfill his filial piety until his death. The ancestors of the emperor and heaven did not bless and help him. He was seriously ill and could not bear the soil."
The last time I wrote a letter stating my illness, I was willing to resign my title to my younger brother, but the memorial was shelved without a reply, and the time was approaching for me to be granted a title.
I would like to give up the title and seek good doctors abroad.
If the disease cannot be cured, forget about dying in a ravine.
Ding Hong initially became a student with Huan Rong, a colleague of Bao Jun from Jiujiang, and the two had a close friendship. Later, Ding Hong escaped from the throne and met Bao Jun in the East China Sea. Ding Hong pretended not to know Bao Jun.
Bao Jun took Ding Hong's arm and scolded him: "In the past, Boyi and Wu Jizha were in troubled times, so they had to express their ambition to surrender the country."
The great righteousness of "Spring and Autumn" is not to waste the king's affairs because of family affairs. Now you are using your brother's affair to cut off your father's immortal inheritance. Can you say it is smart?
Ding Hong was very moved and sighed in tears, so he returned to his country and taught apprentices.
Bao Jun also wrote a letter saying that Ding Hong was a good scholar and practiced the classics, and Xianzong appreciated him very much.
In the 10th year of Yongping, the emperor issued an edict to recruit talents. Ding Hong was summoned when he arrived and told the story of "The Order of Marquis Wen". The emperor bestowed royal robes and ribbons, food and carriage, and received the same courtesy as a doctor.
Soon, he became a servant.
In the thirteenth year, he also served as the captain of Shesheng.
In the fourth year of Jianchu period, he was granted the title of Marquis of Luyang Township.
Suzong ordered Ding Hong, Wang Xian of Guangping, and other Confucian scholars such as Lou Wang, Cheng Feng, Huan Yu, Jia Kui, etc. to discuss the similarities and differences of the Five Classics at the White Tiger Temple in the Beigong. Wei Ying, the minister of five sense organs, took over the task of handling the problem, and Chunyu, the servant, went to Gongzou, and the emperor personally announced the decision.
Ding Hong was highly talented and the most eloquent in discussing difficult issues. He was praised by many scholars and even praised by the emperor many times.
People at that time sighed: "There is no one like Ding Xiaogong in the palace."
Ding Hong was rewarded many times and promoted to school secretary, so he replaced Cheng as Shaofu.
From then on, the sect became more prosperous, with thousands of people coming from far away.
Liu Kai from Pengcheng, Bamao from Beihai, and Zhu Xian from Jiujiang all became public ministers.
In the third year of Yuanhe, he was renamed Marquis of Mating Township.
Emperor He ascended the throne and was promoted to Taichang. In the fourth year of Yongyuan, Yuan An became Situ.
At this time, Empress Dowager Dou came to power, and her brothers Dou Xian were each good at authority.

summary:
Ding Hong, courtesy name Xiaogong, was a native of Dingling, Yingchuan. His father, Ding Lin, surrendered Yingyang to Shizu and was rewarded, later becoming Governor of Henan. Ding Lin chose to be enfeoffed in his hometown, becoming Marquis of Xin'an Township. Ding Hong studied "Ouyang Shangshu" under Huan Rong at thirteen, mastering it in three years. After his father's death, he attempted to cede his title to his younger brother Ding Sheng but fled when his request was ignored. His friend Bao Jun persuaded him to return, citing the importance of upholding his father's legacy. Ding Hong returned, taught disciples, and was later summoned by Emperor Xianzong, becoming a servant and then Captain of Shesheng. He participated in the White Tiger Temple discussions on the Five Classics, where his eloquence was praised. He was promoted to Shaofu and later Taichang. During Empress Dowager Dou's regency, Ding Hong submitted a memorial warning against the Dou family's power, citing solar eclipses as divine warnings. His advice led to the confiscation of Dou Xian's seal and the suicides of Dou Xian and his brothers. Ding Hong also reformed the system for recommending filial and incorrupt candidates. He died in the sixth year of Yongyuan.
