Later, as Situ Zuo Changshi, he criticized the emperor for overworking himself and proposed consolidating official positions, simplifying affairs, and promoting agriculture. He also petitioned against extravagance, citing ancient frugality. During Emperor Hui's reign, he advised Yang Jun on regency matters and later criticized Sima Liang for excessive rewards. Fu Xian was known for his fearless impeachments, including against Wang Rong, and his advocacy for long-term official tenures. He died in office at age fifty-six, posthumously titled Zhen. He had three sons: Fu Fu, Fu Rixi, and Fu Zuan.
