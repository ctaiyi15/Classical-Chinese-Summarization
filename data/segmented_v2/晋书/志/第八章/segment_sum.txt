魏尚书郎杨伟表曰： 臣览载籍，断考历数，时以纪农，月以纪事，其所由来，遐而尚矣。
乃自少昊，则玄鸟司分，颛顼、帝喾，则重黎司天；唐帝、虞舜，则羲和掌日，三代因之，则世有日官。
日官司历，则颁之诸侯，诸侯受之，则颁于境内。
夏后之世，羲和湎淫，废时乱日，则《书》载《胤徵》。
由此观之，审农时而重人事，历代然之也。
逮至周室既衰，战国横鹜，告朔之羊，废而不绍，登台之礼，灭而不遵，闰分乖次而不识，孟陬失纪而莫悟，大火犹西流，而怪蛰虫之不藏也。
是时也，天子不协时，司历不书日。诸侯不受职，日御不分朔，人事不恤，废弃农时。
仲尼之拨乱于《春秋》，托褒贬纠正，司历失闰，则讥而书之，登台颁朔，则谓之有礼。
自此以降，暨于秦汉，乃复以孟冬为岁首，闰为后九月，中节乖错，时月纰缪。加时后天，蚀不在朔，累载相袭，久而不革也。
至武帝元封七年，始乃悟其缪焉，于是改正朔，更历数，使大才通人，更造《太初历》，校中朔所差，以正闰分，课中星得度，以考疏密，以建寅之月为正朔，以黄钟之月为历初。
其历斗分太多，后遂疏阔。
至元和二年，复用《四分历》，施而行之，至于今日，考察日蚀，率常在晦，是则斗分太多，故先密后疏而不可用也。
是以臣前以制典余日，推考天路，稽之前典，验之以蚀朔，详而精之，更建密历，则不先不后，古今中天。
以昔在唐帝，协日正时，允厘百工，咸熙庶绩也。
欲使当今国之典礼，凡百制度，皆韬合往古，郁然备足，乃改正朔，更历数，以大吕之月为岁首，以建子之月为历初。
臣以为昔在帝代，则法曰《颛顼》，曩自轩辕，则历曰《黄帝》，暨至汉之孝武，革正朔，更历数，改元曰太初，因名《太初历》。
今改元为景初，宜曰《景初历》。
臣之所建《景初历》，法数则约要，施用则近密，治之则省功，学之则易知。
虽复使研桑心算，隶首运筹，重黎司晷，羲和察景，以考天路，步验日月，究极精微，尽术数之极者，皆未能并臣如此之妙也。
是以累代历数，皆疏而不密，自黄帝以来，常改革不已。
壬辰以来，至景初元年丁已岁，积四千四十六，算上。
此元以天正建子黄钟之月为历初，元首之岁，夜半甲子朔旦冬至。

Wei Shangshulang Yang Wei said in the table: I read ancient books and investigated the role of calendar numbers in judgment. Seasons are used to organize agricultural affairs, and months are used to organize personnel affairs. They have a long history.
During the reign of Shaohao, the days were divided based on phenological judgments such as the return of swallows; during the reigns of Zhuanxu and Emperor Xue, Chongli was in charge of heaven;
The Japanese official is in charge of the calendar and promulgates it to the princes. After the princes obtain it, they will promulgate it within the territory.
During the Xia Dynasty, Xihe was addicted to wine, which caused the calendar to be disordered. The "Book" wrote this incident in the "Yin Zheng" wrapper.
From this point of view, it can be seen that people are aware of the farming season and pay attention to personnel matters, which has been the case in all ages.
Until the decline of the Zhou Dynasty and the outbreak of the Warring States Period, the sheep used for the lunar calendar were discarded and not inherited, the rituals of offering sacrifices to heaven and earth were extinct but not followed, the setting of the leap month was wrong but not aware of it, the arrangement of the first month was not in line with the season but not aware of it, the big Mars was still in the west, but it was strange that the stinging insects were not hidden.
At that time, the emperor did not coordinate the seasons, the officials in charge of the calendar did not write down the good and bad taboos of the next day, the princes did not perform their duties, the Japanese officials did not even calculate the new moon, and they were not considerate of personnel and abandoned the farming season.
Zhongni tried to correct chaos in the book "Spring and Autumn" and relied on praise and criticism to correct mistakes. The official in charge of the calendar set the leap month improperly, so he recorded it in the book and ridiculed it.
From then on, until the Qin and Han Dynasties, Mengdong was still used as the beginning of the year, and the leap month was the ninth month. The solar terms were disordered, the seasons and months were wrong, the correction values ​​lag behind the actual celestial phenomena, and the solar eclipse did not occur at the new moon. It has been followed for many years and has not changed for a long time.
It was not until the seventh year of Emperor Wu's Yuanfeng reign that he realized the above-mentioned error, so he changed the position of the first month, changed the basic data of the calendar, and ordered talented and knowledgeable people to create the Taichu Calendar. The month of Jianyin is used as the first month, and the month of Huangzhong is used as the beginning of the calendar.
The differences in this calendar were too great, and the errors gradually increased.
In the second year of Chenghe, the "Four Points Calendar" was used again and has been used until now. When observing solar eclipses, they often occur on dark days. This is because there are too many points, so it was first precise and then rough. It is no longer available.
I previously used my spare time in compiling codes to calculate and study the movement of celestial bodies, and to examine previous classics. I verified them with solar eclipses and new moons, and established a more precise calendar.
In the past, during the reign of Emperor Tang, by coordinating time and governing the people with integrity, all kinds of undertakings were prosperous.
In order to make the ceremonies and various systems of today's country consistent with the past and very complete and sufficient, the location of the first month was changed, and the basic data of the calendar was changed. The month of Daru was used as the beginning of the year, and the month of Jianzi was used as the initial month of the calendar.
I think that in the Zhuanxu era, the calendar was called "Zhuanxu", and in the Xuanyuan era, the calendar was called "Huangdi". When Emperor Xiaoyuan of the Han Dynasty reformed Zhengshuo and changed the calendar, the changed Yuan was called Taichu, so the calendar was named "Taichu Calendar".
Now that the Yuan Dynasty has been changed to Jingchu, the calendar should be called "Jingchu Calendar".
The method and data of the "Jingchu Calendar" I formulated are concise and concise, the application is close to precision, the study of it is efficient, and the study of it is easy to understand.
Even if I were to do mental calculations, make plans, control the sundial, observe the sun's shadow and celestial phenomena, calculate and verify the movement of the sun and the moon, and explore the subtleties and subtleties, and reach the limit of my skills, it would not be as exquisite as my calendar.
Therefore, the calendars of the past dynasties are rough and not precise. Since the Yellow Emperor, they have been constantly changing.
From Renchen Yuan Dynasty to Ding Si, the first year of Jingchu Dynasty, the accumulation was 4046 years, counting.
This epoch begins with the Huangzhong month of Tianzheng Jianzi as the beginning of the calendar. In the first year of each epoch, the winter solstice occurs at midnight on Jiazishuo day.

乃自少昊，则玄鸟司分，颛顼、帝喾，则重黎司天；唐帝、虞舜，则羲和掌日，三代因之，则世有日官。
日官司历，则颁之诸侯，诸侯受之，则颁于境内。
夏后之世，羲和湎淫，废时乱日，则《书》载《胤徵》。
由此观之，审农时而重人事，历代然之也。
逮至周室既衰，战国横鹜，告朔之羊，废而不绍，登台之礼，灭而不遵，闰分乖次而不识，孟陬失纪而莫悟，大火犹西流，而怪蛰虫之不藏也。
是时也，天子不协时，司历不书日。诸侯不受职，日御不分朔，人事不恤，废弃农时。
仲尼之拨乱于《春秋》，托褒贬纠正，司历失闰，则讥而书之，登台颁朔，则谓之有礼。
自此以降，暨于秦汉，乃复以孟冬为岁首，闰为后九月，中节乖错，时月纰缪。加时后天，蚀不在朔，累载相袭，久而不革也。
至武帝元封七年，始乃悟其缪焉，于是改正朔，更历数，使大才通人，更造《太初历》，校中朔所差，以正闰分，课中星得度，以考疏密，以建寅之月为正朔，以黄钟之月为历初。
其历斗分太多，后遂疏阔。
至元和二年，复用《四分历》，施而行之，至于今日，考察日蚀，率常在晦，是则斗分太多，故先密后疏而不可用也。
是以臣前以制典余日，推考天路，稽之前典，验之以蚀朔，详而精之，更建密历，则不先不后，古今中天。
以昔在唐帝，协日正时，允厘百工，咸熙庶绩也。
欲使当今国之典礼，凡百制度，皆韬合往古，郁然备足，乃改正朔，更历数，以大吕之月为岁首，以建子之月为历初。
臣以为昔在帝代，则法曰《颛顼》，曩自轩辕，则历曰《黄帝》，暨至汉之孝武，革正朔，更历数，改元曰太初，因名《太初历》。
今改元为景初，宜曰《景初历》。
臣之所建《景初历》，法数则约要，施用则近密，治之则省功，学之则易知。
虽复使研桑心算，隶首运筹，重黎司晷，羲和察景，以考天路，步验日月，究极精微，尽术数之极者，皆未能并臣如此之妙也。
是以累代历数，皆疏而不密，自黄帝以来，常改革不已。
壬辰以来，至景初元年丁已岁，积四千四十六，算上。
此元以天正建子黄钟之月为历初，元首之岁，夜半甲子朔旦冬至。

In the past, during the reign of Emperor Tang, by coordinating time and governing the people with integrity, all kinds of undertakings were prosperous.
