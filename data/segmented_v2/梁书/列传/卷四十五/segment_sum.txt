以天监中随父来奔。
起家为湘东王国左常侍。
王为丹阳尹，转府行参军。
王出守会稽，兼中兵参军事。
王为荆州，仍除中兵，在限内。
时武宁郡反，王命僧辩讨平之。
迁贞威将军、武宁太守。
寻迁振远将军、广平太守。
秩满，还为王府中录事，参军如故。
王被征为护军，僧辩兼府司马。
王为江州，仍除云骑将军司马，守湓城。
俄监安陆郡，无几而还。
寻为新蔡太守，犹带司马，将军如故。
王除荆州，为贞毅将军府谘议参军事，赐食千人，代柳仲礼为竟陵太守，改号雄信将军。
属侯景反，王命僧辩假节，总督舟师一万，兼粮馈赴援。
才至京都，宫城陷没，天子蒙尘。
僧辩与柳仲礼兄弟及赵伯超等，先屈膝于景，然后入朝。景悉收其军实，而厚加绥抚。
未几，遣僧辩归于竟陵，于是倍道兼行，西就世祖。
世祖承制，以僧辩为领军将军。
及荆、湘疑贰，军师失律，世祖又命僧辩及鲍泉统军讨之，分给兵粮，克日就道。
时僧辨以竟陵部下犹未尽来，意欲待集，然后上顿。
谓鲍泉曰： 我与君俱受命南讨，而军容若此，计将安之？
泉曰： 既禀庙算，驱率骁勇，事等沃雪，何所多虑。
僧辩曰： 不然。
君之所言故是，文士之常谈耳。
河东少有武干，兵刃又强，新破军师，养锐待敌，自非精兵一万，不足以制之。
我竟陵甲士，数经行阵，已遣召之，不久当及。

Wang Sengbian, also known as Juncai, was the son of Youwei General Wang Shennian.
During the Tianjian period, Wang Sengbian came to join his father.
He was recruited from his family and served as the Zuo Changshi of the Xiangdong Kingdom.
King Xiangshu was appointed Danyang Yin, and Wang Sengbian was transferred to Fu Xing to join the army.
King Shu of Hunan was appointed as the prefect of Kuaiji, and Wang Sengbian also served as a soldier in the military.
King Xiangshu was appointed governor of Jingzhou, and Wang Sengbian was still appointed as a soldier in the army and held a confidential position.
At that time, Wuning County was in rebellion, and King Xiangdong ordered Wang Sengbian to attack and quell the rebellion.
Wang Sengbian was moved to the post of General Zhenwei and Prefect of Wuning.
Soon he was moved to the post of General Zhenyuan and Grand Administrator of Guangping.
After his term of office expired, Wang Sengbian returned to the Prince of Xiangshu and served as a recorder of affairs in the palace, and his position in the army remained unchanged.
The king of eastern Hunan was conscripted into the capital to serve as a military guard, and Wang Sengbian also served as the Sima of the palace.
The king of eastern Hunan was appointed governor of Jiangzhou, and Wang Sengbian was subsequently appointed as General Yunqi, Sima, and governor of Huancheng.
Soon he moved to oversee Anlu County, and returned to the palace not long after.
Soon he was appointed governor of Xincai, still holding the post of Sima, and his title of general remained unchanged.
King Shu of Xiang was appointed as the prefect of Jingzhou. Wang Sengbian consulted for General Zhenyi's office and joined the military. He was given food for a thousand people. He replaced Liu Zhongli as the prefect of Jingling and changed his title to General Xiongxin.
When Hou Jing rebelled, the king of eastern Hunan ordered Wang Sengbian to go on holiday. The governor sent 10,000 naval troops to aid the capital with food supplies.
When Monk Wang arrived at the capital, the palace was captured and the emperor was humiliated.
Wang Sengbian, Liu Zhongli brothers, Tang Bochao and others first surrendered to Hou Jing, and then entered the palace to see the emperor. Hou Jing confiscated all their weapons and food, and deeply comforted them.
Not long after, Hou Jing sent Wang Sengbian back to Jingling. Wang Sengbian then doubled his travels, traveling day and night, and went west to join Shizu.
Shizu followed the emperor's will and appointed Wang Sengbian as the leading general.
There was a rift between the governor of Jingzhou, King Xiangdong, and the governor of Xiangzhou, King Hedong. They were suspicious of each other, and the army could not be commanded in a unified manner. The emperor ordered Wang Sengbian and Bao Quan to lead the army to attack them, allocate enough military rations, and set a date for departure.
At that time, Wang Sengbian planned to wait for all of Jingling's men to gather before moving forward together because not all of Jingling's men had arrived.
Wang Sengbian said to Bao Quan: You and I accepted the order to march southward, but the army formation is so uneven. What are your plans?
Bao Quan said: Since we have accepted the imperial court's war plan and led our brave soldiers to kill the enemy, the situation is like pouring hot water on snow, so why should we worry more?
Monk Wang argued: What you said is wrong.
What you are saying is nothing more than a truism from scribes.
The King of Hedong had military talent when he was young, and his weapons and equipment were very strong. He had just defeated our army and was now recharging his strength, waiting for us to attack. Without ten thousand elite soldiers, it would not be enough to defeat them.
The Jingling soldiers under my leadership have experienced many battles. I have sent people to summon them and will arrive soon.

summary:
summary line 0: Wang Sengbian, son of General Wang Shennian, began his military career during the Tianjian period, serving under various princes in positions such as Zuo Changshi and military advisor. He quelled a rebellion in Wuning County and held several governorships, including Wuning, Guangping, and Anlu. When Hou Jing rebelled, Wang Sengbian led 10,000 naval troops to aid the capital, but the palace was captured. He briefly surrendered to Hou Jing before rejoining Shizu (Emperor Yuan of Liang). Ordered to attack the governor of Xiangzhou, Wang Sengbian delayed departure due to insufficient troops, angering Shizu, who struck him with a sword and imprisoned him. He was later pardoned to defend against King Yueyang's attack, successfully defeating him and replacing Bao Quan to pacify Xiangzhou.
