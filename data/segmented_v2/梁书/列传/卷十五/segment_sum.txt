Xie Bei, courtesy name Jingchong, was born in Yangxia, Chen County.
His grandfather Hongwei was Taichang Qing in the Song Dynasty, and his father was Xie Zhuang, a doctor named Youguanglu. Both of them were famous in previous generations.
Xie Sui was smart and smart when he was a child. Xie Zhuang valued him very much and often took him with him.
He could write articles at the age of ten.
Xie Zhuang was playing in Tushan and composing poems. He asked Xie Li to write a poem. Xie Bei picked up his pen and wrote it.
Wang Jingwen of Langye said to Xiezhuang: Your son is enough to be called a child prodigy, and he will become a particularly promising person among future generations.
Xie Zhuang smiled, patted Xie Li's back and said, "You are really the treasure of our family."
Emperor Xiaowu was visiting Gushu, and ordered Xie Zhuang to take Xie Kui to follow the driver. He also ordered Xie Chu to compose "Praise to the Dongjing" and played the "Praise to the Dongjing" while he was sitting.
Emperor Xiaowu said: Although he is young, he is a child prodigy.
Xie Li initially served as Fujun Fa Cao Xing and joined the army. He was promoted to Prince Sheren but resigned due to his father's death.
After serving the funeral, he became a layman again, and successively served as Zhongshulang and General Yuan Can of the Wei.
Yuan Can was simple and strict by nature and rarely interacted with guests. People at the time compared him to Li Ying.
When Xie Li saw that he had retired, Yuan Can said, "This is Xie Ling's immortality."
Soon he was transferred to serve as the minister of Huangmen.
He served as the internal history of Linchuan and was impeached for bribery. The case passed through Yuan Can, who shelved the matter.
Emperor Qi Gao served as a hussar general to assist the government, elected Xie Bei to be the chief historian, and ordered him to enter the court to serve the Song emperor together with Chu Xuan of Henan, Jiang Jiao of Jiyang, and Liu Yu of Pengcheng. They were known as the four friends of the emperor at that time.
Then he was appointed as Shizhong, and was in charge of the Shaoguan documents of Zhongshu and Sanqi provinces.
Emperor Gao was promoted to the rank of Taiwei, and appointed Xie Zhu as the chief official and concurrently as the Grand Administrator of Nanshutu.
The childbirth was about to plot to succeed the emperor. The ministers who were considering assisting him in starting a business thought that Xie Bei had great reputation and respected him deeply.
Discussing the story of Wei and Zhi with Xie Nao, he said: The changes in destiny and destiny of Zhi Dai reform have been foreshadowed for a long time. Hu Bao did not encourage Emperor Wen of Jin to ascend the throne earlier, and only cried for him after his death. Compared with Feng Yi, he did not know the timing.
Xie Chu replied: It turns out that there were ministers in the Wei State who encouraged Emperor Wu of Wei to ascend the throne. Emperor Wu of Wei said to them: If destiny is useful to me, I should probably become King Wen of Zhou!
Emperor Wen of Jin served the Wei family all his life, and he would definitely be a minister to the north for the rest of his life. If Wei had followed the story of Tang Yu's surrender earlier, then Emperor Wen of Jin should have resigned many times to show his noble character.
Emperor Gao was very unhappy after hearing what he said.
Then he recommended Wang Jian to be the chief historian of Zuo, and asked Xie Zhu to write poems and serve as secretary and supervisor.
When Qi arrived, he accepted the abdication. Xie Zhu was on duty that day, and all the officials were sitting with him. The waiter was supposed to take off the jade seal. Xie Li pretended not to understand and asked: "What's the official business?"
The person who passed it on to Shao said: Take off the jade seal and give it to the King of Qi.
Xie Chu said: Qi himself should have an attendant.
