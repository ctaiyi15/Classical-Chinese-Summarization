萧子恪，字景冲，兰陵人，齐豫章文献王嶷第二子也。
永明中，以王子封南康县侯。
年十二，和从兄司徒竟陵王《高松赋》，卫军王俭见而奇之。
初为宁朔将军、淮陵太守，建武中，迁辅国将军、吴郡太守。
大司马王敬则于会稽举兵反，以奉子恪为名，明帝悉召子恪兄弟亲从七十余人入西省，至夜当害之。
会子恪弃郡奔归，是日亦至，明帝乃止，以子恪为太子中庶子。
东昏即位，迁秘书监，领右军将军，俄为侍中。
中兴二年，迁辅国谘议参军。
天监元年，降爵为子，除散骑常侍，领步兵校尉，以疾不拜，徙为光禄大夫，俄为司徒左长史。
子恪与弟子范等，尝因事入谢，高祖在文德殿引见之，从容谓曰： 我欲与卿兄弟有言。
夫天下之宝，本是公器，非可力得。
苟无期运，虽有项籍之力，终亦败亡。
所以班彪《王命论》云： 所求不过一金，然终转死沟壑 。
卿不应不读此书。
宋孝武为性猜忌，兄弟粗有令名者，无不因事鸩毒，所遗唯有景和。
至于朝臣之中，或疑有天命而致害者，枉滥相继，然而或疑有天命而不能害者，或不知有天命而不疑者，于时虽疑卿祖，而无如之何。
此是疑而不得。
又有不疑者，如宋明帝本为庸常被免，岂疑而得全？
又复我于时已年二岁，彼岂知我应有今日？
当知有天命者，非人所害，害亦不能得。
我初平建康城，朝廷内外皆劝我云： 时代革异，物心须一，宜行处分。
我于时依此而行，谁谓不可！
我政言江左以来，代谢必相诛戮，此是伤于和气，所以国祚例不灵长。
所谓 殷鉴不远，在夏后之世。
此是一义。

When he was twelve years old, he wrote "Gaosong Fu" with his cousin King Situ Jingling. The guard Gongjian saw him and admired him very much.
He first served as General Ningshuo and Grand Administrator Jinduo. During the Jiangan period, he moved to General Fu Guo and Grand Administrator Wujun.
Grand Sima Wang Jingze raised troops in Kuaiji to rebel. In the name of supporting Zijian, Dandantong summoned more than 70 Wang Tan brothers to the Western Province and planned to kill them at night.
Shizi abandoned Wu County and fled back. He rushed to the capital that day, so Emperor Ming did not harm them. Appointed Wang Yan as the crown prince's concubine.
Donghunhou ascended the throne, moved to the post of secretary supervisor, led the right army general, and soon became the minister.
In the second year of Zhongxing Dynasty, he moved to the post of auxiliary to consult the country and join the army.
In the first year of Tianjian, he was demoted to viscount, appointed as a regular attendant of the cavalry, and a captain of the infantry. However, he was not appointed because of illness. He was moved to the post of Guanglu doctor, and soon he was appointed as the chief historian of Situ Zuo.
Fan and others, disciples of Xiao Zike, once came to court to express gratitude for their affairs. Emperor Gaozu received them in the Hall of Wende and calmly said to them: I want to say a few words to you brothers.
The most precious thing in the world is fame and fortune, but it cannot be obtained by force.
If there is no luck, even with Xiang Ji's strength, he will eventually fail.
Therefore, Mo Ai's "The Theory of King's Destiny" says: What he sought was just a gold, but he ended up tossing and dying in a ravine.

As for the ministers of the imperial court, they may be killed on suspicion of destiny, which is a mistake, and innocent victims follow one after another. However, there are also those who doubt a person's destiny but cannot kill him, or who do not know that a person has a destiny but do not doubt him. At that time, although he doubted your grandfather, he did not know how to treat him.
This is when people are suspected but not killed.
There are also people who are not suspected. For example, Emperor Ming of the Song Dynasty was exempted because of his mediocrity and inaction, but he was saved because he was suspected.
Then again, I was already two years old at that time, how could he know that I should be where I am today.
You should know that people with destiny cannot be harmed by humans, and even if you want to harm them, you cannot succeed.
When I first pacified Jiankang City, people inside and outside the court advised me: Times have changed, people must be united, and punishments must be implemented.
I acted according to this theory at that time, who would say it’s not possible!
I was saying that since the founding of Jiangzuo, if we want to change dynasties, we must kill each other. This is damaging to harmony, so the national destiny cannot be broad and long-lasting as usual.
The so-called lessons of Xia's demise are right in front of us, and the descendants of Yin should take them as a warning.

summary:
summary line 0: Xiao Zike, courtesy name Jingchong, was the second son of Xiao Yi, King Wen of Yuzhang in the Qi Dynasty. During the Yongming era, he was named Marquis of Nankang County. At age twelve, he co-wrote "Gaosong Fu" with his cousin, King Jingling of Situ, earning admiration from the guard Gongjian. He began his career as General Ningshuo and Grand Administrator of Jinduo, later moving to General Fuguo and Grand Administrator of Wujun during the Jiangan period. When Grand Sima Wang Jingze rebelled in Kuaiji, Zike fled to the capital, avoiding harm from Emperor Ming, who appointed him as the crown prince's concubine. Under Donghunhou, he became Secretary Supervisor, Right Army General, and Minister. In the second year of Zhongxing, he served as Auxiliary to Consult the Country. In the first year of Tianjian, he was demoted to Viscount, appointed as Regular Attendant of the Cavalry and Infantry Captain, but declined due to illness. He later held posts including Guanglu Doctor and Chief Historian of Situ Zuo.
