中大通元年，转车骑将军。
四年，进太尉，领金紫光禄。

translation: 
Yuan Faseng is a relative of the Wei family.
His ancestor was Emperor Daowu of the Northern Wei Dynasty.
His father, Zhong Kui, was named King of Jiangyang.
Fa Seng served as an official in the Northern Wei Dynasty, serving successively as Guanglu doctor, and later as envoy Chijie, governor of Xuzhou military affairs, Xuzhou governor, and guarding Pengcheng.
In the fifth year of ordinary times, the Wei family was in chaos. Fa Seng occupied Pengcheng and proclaimed himself emperor. He punished dissidents, established various princes as kings, and arranged generals, hoping to revive the Northern Wei Dynasty.
Soon after the civil strife in the Northern Wei Dynasty was calmed down, the monks were about to attack the monks. The monks were afraid, so they sent envoys to express their submission to the Liang Dynasty and asked him to become a vassal of the Liang Dynasty. Emperor Gaozu agreed and appointed him Shizhong and Sikong.
When the Northern Wei army approached him, Fa Seng asked to return to the Liang Dynasty, and Emperor Gaozu sent Zhu Yi, a member of Zhongshushe, to greet him.
When he came to Liang Dynasty, he was extremely favored.
At that time, he was attracting people who had returned, and he was friendly to those who surrendered. He also gave the Dharma monk a house, female music, and gold and silk.
When Fa Seng was in the Northern Wei Dynasty, he had been a border guard official for a long time. Every time he invaded and plundered, he killed many people. He asked for troops to be sent for self-defense. Shao ordered him to have hundreds of guards wearing armor and weapons, and they were prohibited from entering and leaving the palace.
In the second year of Datong, he was appointed as the champion general.
In the first year of Zhongdatong, he was transferred to the general of chariots and cavalry.
In four years, he was promoted to Taiwei and received gold and purple light.
At that time, he was established as the lord of the Eastern Wei Dynasty, but he did not return to the Wei Dynasty. He was appointed as envoy Chijie, regular attendant of Sanqi, general of Hussar, Kaifu Tongsansi Zhiyi, and governor of Yingzhou.
In the second year of Datong, he was recruited as Shizhong, Taiwei, and general of the army division. He died at the age of eighty-three.
The two sons, named Jinglong and Jingzhong, followed Fa Seng into the Liang Dynasty during the ordinary years.
Jinglong was granted the title of Duke of Chuanyang County, with a settlement of thousands of households, and served as Chijie, Commander-in-Chief of Guangyue, Jiaogui and other thirteen states' military affairs, General Pingnan, General Lang of Huyue Zhonglang, and Governor of Guangzhou.
In the third year of Zhongdatong, he was recruited as Shizhong and General Anyou.
In the fourth year, he was appointed as the General of Zhengbei and the Governor of Xuzhou, and was granted the title of King of Pengcheng.
In the early years of the Taiqing Dynasty, he also served as envoy Chijie, governor of Guangyue Jiaogui and other military forces in thirteen states, general of the southern expedition, general of Pingyue Zhonglang, and governor of Guangzhou. He traveled to Leishou and died of illness at the age of fifty-eight.
Jing Zhong was granted the title of Duke of Zhijiang County, with thousands of households living in the city, and was appointed as Shizhong and Right Guard General.
In the third year of Datong, the number of new towns was increased to 2,000 households, and a female musical instrument was also given.
