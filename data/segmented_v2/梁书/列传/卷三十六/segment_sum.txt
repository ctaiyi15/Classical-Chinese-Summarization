The eighth grandson of Kong Chong, the prefect of Danyang in Jin Dynasty.
His great-grandfather Kong Yaozhi served as Minister of the Ministry of Water of the Song Dynasty.

Xiuyuan lost his father when he was eleven years old. The mourning period was completely in accordance with etiquette. Every time he saw his father's handwriting, he would burst into tears and couldn't help himself. Everyone who saw it would not shed tears for him.
Later, he went to Wuxing to study under Xuan Diezao, and he was familiar with the principles of Confucian classics.
In the fourth year of Jianwu's reign, the prefecture held a ceremony for scholars. Taiwei Tu Cunqi read his policies and articles and admired them very much. He said to the people sitting with him: "Why are Dong Zhongshu and Hua Lingsi better than him? They can be called models for future generations."
Looking at his policy article, it can be said that he has the talent of a king.
Wang Rong, a native of Longxie, was very friendly with him, so he recommended Kong Shunuo to King Situ Jingling as a bachelor of Xili.

Xiuyuan came to Beijing for the first time and stayed in the house of Kong Deng, the minister of the clan's Shaofu. He once went to the temple for sacrifices. Fan Yun, the attendant, met him for the first time and praised him very much. He said: "I met you unexpectedly, and my stingy heart disappeared. Soon he was granted the title of Prince of Linchuan to join the army.

Xu Mian replied: Kong Xiuyuan has clear knowledge and is familiar with the past. He is familiar with the "Daily Notes" of the Jin Dynasty and Song Dynasty.
Emperor Gaozu had always heard about it, so he was awarded the title of Doctor Cao, who was also the Minister of Book Yi.
At that time, there were many changes in the court rituals. Whenever he was asked about the past, Xiuyuan immediately made random decisions based on his memory without hesitation.
Renfang, the official of the official department, often called him "Kong Dusong".

Later, some people were selected to serve as prison chiefs, and Emperor Gaozu encouraged them by citing Xiu Yuan's deeds.
He was granted the title of Zhongshu Sheren, and Situ joined the army in the palace's record office. He moved to Zuocheng, Shangshu, and rectified the Shangshu Province, living up to the expectations of the court.
At that time, Prince Zhan Shizhou She compiled "The Book of Rites", which included relevant historical materials from Han and Wei to Qi and Liang, and all Xiuyuan's memorials were selected for inclusion.
