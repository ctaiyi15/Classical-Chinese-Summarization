The King of Eastern Hunan came to listen to the lecture in person and discussed it with Yu Chengxian for a whole day. He was deeply impressed by him.
King Xiangshu also invited Yu Chengxian to the palace and stayed there for more than a month before Yu Chengxian returned to the mountains.
When he returned to the mountain, King Xiangshu personally held a farewell banquet for him and wrote poems as gifts. People who lived in seclusion felt that this was an honor.
This year, Yu Chengxian died at the age of sixty.
