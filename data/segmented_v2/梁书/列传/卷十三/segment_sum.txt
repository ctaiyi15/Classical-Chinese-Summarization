年八岁，遇宋豫州刺史殷琰于涂，琰异之，要就席，云风姿应对，傍若无人。
琰令赋诗，操笔便就，坐者叹焉。
尝就亲人袁照学，昼夜不怠。
照抚其背曰： 卿精神秀朗而勤于学，卿相才也。
少机警有识，且善属文，便尺牍，下笔辄成，未尝定藁，时人每疑其宿构。
父抗，为郢府参军，云随父在府，时吴兴沈约、新野庾杲之与抗同府，见而友之。
起家郢州西曹书佐，转法曹行参军。
俄而沈攸之举兵围郢城，抗时为府长流，入城固守，留家属居外。
云为军人所得，攸之召与语，声色甚厉，云容貌不变，徐自陈说。
攸之乃笑曰： 卿定可儿，且出就舍。
明旦，又召令送书入城。
城内或欲诛之，云曰： 老母弱弟，悬命沈氏，若违其命，祸必及亲，今日就戮，甘心如荠。
长史柳世隆素与云善，乃免之。
齐建元初，竟陵王子良为会稽太守，云始随王，王未之知也。
会游秦望，使人视刻石文，时莫能识，云独诵之，王悦，自是宠冠府朝。
王为丹阳尹，召为主簿，深相亲任。
时进见齐高帝，值有献白乌者，帝问此为何瑞？云位卑，最后答曰： 臣闻王者敬宗庙，则白乌至。
时谒庙始毕。
帝曰： 卿言是也。
感应之理，一至此乎！
转补征北南郡王刑狱参军事，领主簿如故，迁尚书殿中郎。
translation: 
Fan Yun, courtesy name Yanlong, was born in Wuyin, Nanxiang, and was the sixth-generation descendant of Jiewang, the Pingbei general of the Jin Dynasty.
When he was eight years old, he met Yin Yan, the governor of Yuzhou in the Song Dynasty, on the road. Yin Yan saw that his appearance was very unusual, so he invited him to sit with him.
Yin Yan asked him to compose a poem, and he wrote it immediately.
Everyone present was impressed. Fan Yun once studied with his relative Yuan Zhao and worked tirelessly day and night.
Yuan Zhao patted his shoulder and said, "You are bright and cheerful, and you are diligent in studying." He will definitely be a talented person in the future.
Fan Yun has been smart and flexible since he was a child, and he is insightful when dealing with things. He is good at writing articles and is familiar with various literary styles such as letters.
His father, Fan Kang, served in the Ying Mansion and joined the army. Fan Yun lived with his father in Fubao. At that time, Shen Yue from Wuxing, Yu Daizhi from Xinye, and Fan Kangtong were working in Ying Mansion. Fan Yun immediately became good friends after meeting them.
Fan Yun first served as Shuzuo of Xicao in Yingzhou, and then joined the army as Cao Xing of Facao.
Soon Shen Youzhi rebelled and raised troops to besiege Ying City. Fan Kang was appointed Changliu of Ying Mansion at that time and entered the city to take charge of defense, leaving his family members outside the city.
Fan Yun was captured by enemy soldiers, and Youzhi interrogated him personally. His tone was very stern, but Fan Yun's appearance remained unchanged and he answered calmly.
After hearing this, Youzhi smiled and said: "You are such a pleasant child. Go home for now."
The next morning, Youzhi sent for Fan Yun again and ordered him to deliver a message to Cheng Bao.
Some of the defenders in the city advocated killing Fan Yun. Fan Yun said: I have an old mother and a young brother at home outside the city. Their lives are all in Shen's hands. If I disobey orders, they will be harmed. Today I am killed for them, and I am willing to die without any regrets.
Longshi Ryu Shiryong Pingsuo was friendly with Fan Yun and managed to save him from death.
In the early years of Jianyuan of Qi Dynasty, Prince Liang of Jingling became the prefect of Kuaiji, and Fan Yun just started to follow Prince Jingling at that time.
The king didn't know him well. It happened that King Jingling was visiting Qinwang Mountain. The king asked the people around him to identify the ancient characters on the stone inscriptions, but no one could understand them. Only Fan Yun could read the inscriptions. King Jingling was very happy. From then on, Jiang Zhou was favored more than anyone else in the same house.
When King Jingling was appointed Danyang Yin, Fan Yun was summoned to be the chief administrator, and he was highly valued by his close associates.
When he came to see Emperor Gao of Qi, someone happened to offer a white crow. Emperor Gao asked what this meant was a sign. Because Fan Yun was in a low position, he finally replied: I heard that when the emperor worships the ancestral temple, white crows will come.
At that time, the visit to the ancestral temple had just ended.
Emperor Yigao said: Just as you said.
The principle of induction between heaven and man can be so miraculously consistent.
Fan Yun later transferred to the prison of the North and South County Prince to join the military. He also served as the chief secretary and was promoted to the position of Zhonglang in the Shangshu Palace.

出为零陵内史，在任洁己，省烦苛，去游费，百姓安之。
明帝召还都，及至，拜散骑侍郎。
复出为始兴内史。
郡多豪猾大姓，二千石有不善者，谋共杀害，不则逐去之。

初，云与尚书仆射江祏善，祏姨弟徐艺为曲江令，深以托云。
有谭俨者，县之豪族，艺鞭之，俨以为耻，诣京诉云，云坐征还下狱，会赦免。
永元二年，起为国子博士。
初，云与高祖遇于齐竟陵王子良邸，又尝接里闬，高祖深器之。
及义兵至京邑，云时在城内。
东昏既诛，侍中张稷使云衔命出城，高祖因留之，便参帷幄，仍拜黄门侍郎，与沈约同心翊赞。
俄迁大司马谘议参军、领录事。
梁台建，迁侍中。
时高祖纳齐东昏余妃，颇妨政事，云尝以为言，未之纳也。
后与王茂同入卧内，云又谏曰： 昔汉祖居山东，贪财好色，及入关定秦，财帛无所取，妇女无所幸，范增以为其志大故也。
今明公始定天下，海内想望风声，奈何袭昏乱之踪，以女德为累。
王茂因起拜曰： 范云言是，公必以天下为念，无宜留惜。
高祖默然。
云便疏令以余氏赉茂，高祖贤其意而许之。
明日，赐云、茂钱各百万。
天监元年，高祖受禅，柴燎于南郊，云以侍中参乘。
translation: 
When Xiao Ziliang was a Situ, Jianyun joined the military affairs department and was soon awarded the title of Minister of Tongzhi Sanqi, and also the chief of the Honshu Dazhongzheng.
Later, he served as the internal history of Lingling. During his tenure, he was able to keep himself clean and tidy, eliminating many cumbersome government orders and unnecessary expenses, so that the people could live and work in peace and contentment.
Emperor Qi Ming summoned Fan Yun back to the capital. After arriving, he was granted the title of Minister of Sanqi.
He also served as the internal history of Shixing.
There are many powerful and cunning families in Shixing County. If the county governor does not treat them well, they will conspire to kill him together. If they fail, they will drive the governor away.
The border counties of Shixing were inhabited by barbarian tribes, and there were particularly many thieves in these areas. In the past, all the officials in the imperial office wore weapons for self-defense.
After Fan Yun arrived in Shixing, he used his kindness to appease them and removed the surveillance post.
The merchants who came and went could sleep in peace, and the people in the county praised him as a god, so he was promoted to Jiajie, General Jianwu, General Pingyue Zhonglang, and Governor of Guangzhou.
At the beginning, Ji Yun was friendly with Shangshu Pu Shesi Yu, and his younger brother served as the magistrate of Diliao County, entrusting Xian Yi to Lian Nuo for care.
Zhanli is a wealthy family in the county. He was punished by Xu Yi and was flogged. Tan Bao felt humiliated and went to the capital to accuse Fan Yun. Fan Yun was recalled to the capital and convicted and imprisoned. It happened to be an amnesty and he was exempted from punishment.
In the second year of Qi Yongyuan, he was re-appointed as Dr. Guozi.
At first, Ji Yun had met Luan Chandan at the residence of King Tuzao of Zhajing. Yi had lived in a nearby lane, and Liang Gaozu thought highly of him.
When the army opposing Shuhunhou arrived in the capital, Fan Yun was in the city.
After Saihunhou was executed, Zhang Ji, the servant, sent Fan Yun out of the city with a mission. Luan Gaozu kept him and made him serve as a staff officer in the army and strategize. He was also awarded the title of Huangmen Shilang, and worked together with Shen Yue to assist Gaozu.
Soon he was promoted to Grand Sima, joined the army and recorded affairs.
After the establishment of the Liang Dynasty, Fan Yun was promoted to the rank of Shizhong.
At that time, Liang Gaozuna married Qi Shuhunhou's concubine Yu, which seriously affected the government affairs of the court. Fan Yun once made a remonstrance about this, but it was not accepted.
Later, Fan Yun and Wang Mao entered Gaozu's bedroom and remonstrated again: When Mo Xuanzu was in Shandong in the past, he was greedy for wealth and loved beautiful women; but after entering the customs and pacifying the Qin Dynasty, he did not collect property or be intimate with women. Fan Zeng believed that this was because of his lofty ambitions.
Now, Your Majesty, you have just stabilized the world, and everyone in the world admires your reputation. How can you follow in the footsteps of the confused king and be dragged down by women and delay the great event?
Wang Mao also took the opportunity to stand up and bow down and said: "What Fan Yun said is absolutely true. My lord, you must put the world's major events first and you should not regret the future."
Emperor Gaozu was silent after hearing this.
Fan Yun then went to Shu and suggested that Wang Mao should be rewarded with the Yu family. Emperor Gaozu thought that what he said was loyal and upright, so he approved it.
The next day, Emperor Gaozu rewarded Fan Yun and Wang Mao with one million coins each.
In the first year of Tianjian, Emperor Gaozu of Liang received Zen and ascended the throne. He held a ceremony of burning firewood to offer sacrifices to heaven in the southern suburbs. Fan Yun participated in the ceremony as an attendant.
