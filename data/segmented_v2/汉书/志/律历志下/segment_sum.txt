纪术推五星见复，置太极上元以来，尽所求年，乘大终见复数，盈岁数得一，则定见复数也。
不盈者名曰见复余。
见复余盈其见复数，一以上见在往年，倍一以上，又在前往年，不盈者在今年也。
推星所见中次，以见中分乘定见复数，盈见中法得一则积中也。
不盈者名曰中余。
以元中除积中，余则中元余也。
以章中除之，余则入章中数也。
以十二除之，余则星见中次也。
中数从冬至起，次数从星纪起，算外，则星所见中次也。
推星见月，以闰分乘定见复数，以章岁乘中余从之，盈见月法得一，并积中，则积月也。
不盈者名曰月余。
以元月除积月余，名曰月元余。
以章月除月元余，则入章月数也。
以十二除之，至有闰之岁，除十三入章。
三岁一闰，六岁二闰，九岁三闰，十一岁四闰，十四岁五闰，十七岁六闰，十九岁七闰。
不盈者数起于天正，算外，则星所见月也。
推至日，以中法乘中元余，盈元法得一，名曰积日，不盈者名曰小余。
小余盈二千五百九十七以上，中大。
数除积日如法，算外，则冬至也。
推朔日，以月法乘月元余，盈日法得一，名曰积日，余名曰小余。
小余三十八以上，月大。
数除积日如法，算外，则星见月朔日也。
推入中次日度数，以中法乘中余，以见中法乘其小余并之。盈见中日法得一，则入中日入次度数也。
中以至日数，次以次初数，算外，则星所见及日所在度数也。
求夕，在日后十五度。
推入月日数，以月法乘月余，以见月法乘其小余并之，盈见月日法得一，则入月日数也。
并之大余，数除如法，则见日也。
推后见中，加积中于中元余，加后中余于中余，盈其法得一，从中元余，除数如法，则后见中也。
推后见月，加积月于月元余，加后月余于月余，盈其法得一，从月元余，除数如法，则后见月也。
推至日及人中次度数，如上法。
推朔日及入月数，如上法。
推晨见加夕，夕见加晨，皆如上法。
推五步，置始见以来日数，至所求日，各以其行度数乘之。
其星若日有分者，分子乘全为实，分母为法。
其两有分者，分母分度数乘全，分子从之，令相乘为实，分母相乘为法，实如法得一，名曰积度。
数起星初见所在宿度，算外，则星所在宿度也。
岁术推岁所在，置上元以来，外所求年，盈岁数，除去之，不盈者以百四十五乘之，以百四十四为法，如法得一，名曰积次，不盈者名曰次余。
积次盈十二，除去之，不盈者名曰定次。
数从星纪起，算尽之外，则所在次也。
欲知太岁，以六十除积次，余不盈者，数从丙子起，算尽之外，则太岁日也。
translation: 
Calculate the five-star manifestation complex number: Multiply the Datong manifestation complex number from the original Shangyuan and all the required years. The full number of years is one, which is the definite manifestation complex number.
Those who are less than one year old are called Xianfuyu.
The present plural is full of its present plural. If it is more than one, it will appear in the previous year. If it is more than double, it will appear in the year before that. If it is less than one year old, it will appear in this year.
Calculate the middle number of the star's appearance: multiply the present complex number by the divided part of the present middle number. If the full present middle number is obtained, one is obtained, which is the product middle number.
Those who are dissatisfied with the present Zhongfa are called Zhongyu.
Divide product Zhong by Yuan Zhong, and the remainder is Zhong Yuan remainder.
Use the zhongzhong to divide the middle yuan, and the remainder will be included in the zhongzhong.
Divide the number of chapters by twelve, and the remainder is the number of star appearances.
The middle number starts from the winter solstice, and the number starts from the star era. If not counted, it is the middle number of the star.
Calculate the star and the moon: Multiply the definite present complex number by the leap factor, multiply the remainder by the chapter year, add the two products, the full moon method is one, add the product, it is the accumulated month.
Those who are dissatisfied with the appearance of the moon are called Yue Yu.
Dividing the accumulated monthly surplus by the first month is called Yueyuanyu.
If the remainder of the month is divided by the zhang month, it is incorporated into the number of the zhang month.
Divide the month number of the chapter by twelve, and in a leap year, divide it by thirteen and merge it into the chapter.
There is one leap in every three years, two leaps in six years, three leaps in nine years, four leaps in eleven years, five leaps in fourteen years, six leaps in seventeen years, and seven leaps in nineteen years.
Those who are dissatisfied with the method of manifesting the moon will start counting from Tianzheng. If not calculated, the moon will be manifested by the stars.
The calculation is as follows: Multiply the middle Yuan by the Zhong method. If the Yuan method is full, you will get one, which is called accumulation. If it is less than the Yuan method, it will be called Xiaoyu.
Xiaoyu is over two thousand five hundred and ninety-seven, and is a middle school student.
According to the rules, divide the product and calculate, it is the winter solstice.
Calculate the new moon day: Use the moon method to multiply the moon Yuan Yu, and the full Yue method is used to get one, which is called Ji Yue, and the rest is called Xiao Yu.
Xiaoyu is over thirty-eight and one month old.
According to the rules, if you divide the accumulated days and count them, it means that the stars will appear on the first day of the new moon.
Calculate the degree of the next day after entering the center: Multiply the middle remainder by the center method, multiply the small remainder by the present center method, add the two products, and when the present center method is full, we get one, which is the degree of the enter day.
The middle number is the number of the day, the second number is the initial number, and the outer number is the degree where the star appears and is.
Asking for the evening, it will be fifteen degrees later.
To calculate the number of days in the moon: Multiply the remainder of the month by the moon method, multiply its small remainder by the method of the apparent moon, and add their products. When the full moon is full, the number of days in the moon is one, which is the number of the days of the entered moon.
Add the remainder and divide according to the rules, which is the present day.
Calculate the posterior and present middle: add the middle remainder of the product to the middle remainder, add the posterior middle remainder to the middle remainder, and get one when it is full, add the middle remainder, and divide according to the rules, which is the posterior manifestation middle.
Calculate the post manifest month: Add the accumulated month to the month's remainder, add the post month's remainder to the month's remainder, until it is full, you get one, add the month's remainder, and divide according to the rules to get the post manifest month.
Calculate the solstice and the middle degree: the same method as before.
Calculate the new day and the beginning of the month: the same as the previous method.
Calculating morning appearance plus evening time: adding evening time to morning time is the same as the previous method.
Five steps of calculation: use the number of days since it first appeared, and multiply them by the number of degrees it travels to the desired day.
If the star has a divisor, multiply the numerator by the whole number as the divisor and the denominator as the dividend.
Among them, if two stars have points, multiply the whole by the denominator and the numerator, so that the multiplication becomes the divisor, and the denominator multiplication becomes the dividend. If the dividend is the same as the divisor, we get one, which is called the integral.
The calculation starts from the nakshatra degree where the star first appears. If not calculated, it is the nakshatra degree where the star is.
Calculating the age: Since the Yuan Dynasty, regardless of the year required, if the number of years is found, just remove it. If it is less than the number of years, multiply it by one hundred and forty-five, and use one hundred and forty-four as the divisor. If it is equal to the divisor, it will be one, which is called the accumulation times. If it is less than the number of years, it is called the remainder.
If the accumulated number reaches twelve, it is removed. If it is less than twelve, it is called fixed number.
Start counting from the star era, and anything beyond the end of the calculation is the current time.
If you want to know Tai Sui, divide the accumulated times by sixty, and the remainder is less than twelve. Count from Bing Zi. After counting, the rest is Tai Sui.

The "method number" is derived from multiplying the original Huang Zhong Chujiu by itself. The "leap method" of nineteen is used as the number of years in a chapter. The "Tongfa" (1,539) is obtained by multiplying the leap number by the daily number, and the "Yuanfa" (4,617) is three times the Tongfa. Other key numbers include the "Zhang month" (235), "Moon Dharma" (2,392), and "Weekly days" (562,120). The text details the calculation of various astronomical cycles, including the synodic meeting, meeting months, and the cycles of the year star, Taibai Star, Zhenxing, Yinghuo, and the stars (Mercury). For each planet, the text provides specific numbers for its appearance, motion (direct, retrograde, and stationary), and the duration of its visibility and concealment, along with the degrees it travels.
