楚元王交字游，高祖同父少弟也。
好书，多材艺。
少时尝与鲁穆生、白生、申公俱受《诗》于浮丘伯。
伯者，孙卿门人也。
及秦焚书，各别去。
高祖兄弟四人，长兄伯，次仲，伯蚤卒。
高祖既为沛公，景驹自立为楚王。
高祖使仲与审食其留侍太上皇，交与萧、曹等俱从高祖见景驹，遇项梁，共立楚怀王。
因西攻南阳，入武关，与秦战于蓝田。
至霸上，封交为文信君，从入蜀汉，还定三秦，诛项籍。
即帝位，交与卢绾常侍上，出入卧内，传言语诸内事隐谋。
而上从父兄刘贾数别将。
汉六年，既废楚王信，分其地为二国，立贾为荆王，交为楚王，王薛郡、东海、彭城三十六县，先有功也。
后封次兄仲为代王，长子肥为齐王。
初，高祖微时，常避事，时时与宾客过其丘嫂食。
嫂厌叔与客来，阳为羹尽，轑釜，客以故去。
已而视鉴中有羹，繇是怨嫂。
及立齐、代王，而伯子独不得侯。
太上皇以为言，高祖曰： 某非敢忘封之也，为其母不长者。
七年十月，封其子信为羹颉侯。
元王既至楚，以穆生、白生、申公为中大夫。
高后时，浮丘伯在长安，元王遣于郢客与申公俱卒业。
文帝时，闻申公为《诗》最精，以为博士。
元王好《诗》，诸子皆读《诗》，申公始为《诗》传，号《鲁诗》。
元王亦次之《诗》传，号曰《元王诗》，世或有之。
高后时，以元王子郢客为宗正，封上邳侯。
元王立二十三年薨，太子辟非先卒，文帝乃以宗正上邳侯郢客嗣，是为夷王。
申公为博士，失官，随郢客归，复以为中大夫。
立四年薨，子戊嗣。
文帝尊宠元王，子生，爵比皇子。
景帝即位，以亲亲封元王宠子五人：子礼为平陆侯，富为休侯，岁为沈犹侯，执为宛朐侯，调为棘乐侯。
初，元王敬礼申公等，穆生不耆酒，元王每置酒，常为穆生设醴。
及王戊即位，常设，后忘设焉。

The five kings of Chu were friends with each other, and they were the younger brothers of the same father.
Likes to read and is versatile.
When I was a child, I studied "Poetry" with Ni Qiuyuan together with Lu Musheng, Bai Sheng, Shen Gong and Fu Qiubo.
Fu Qiu Bo is a disciple of Se Qing.
When they arrived in Taiwan to burn books, they dispersed.
There were four Gaozu brothers, the eldest brother Liu Bo and the second brother Liu Zhong. Uncle Liu died early.
Emperor Gaozu became Duke of Pei, and Jing Ju established himself as King of Chu.
Gaozu asked Liu Zhong and Shen Shiqi to stay and serve the emperor. Liu Jiao, Xiao He, Cao Shen and others went with Gaozu to see Jing Ju. They met Xiang Liang and together they supported King Huai of Chu.
So he attacked Nanyang to the west, entered Wuguan, and started a battle with Qin in Lantian.
When he reached Ba Shang, he made Liu Jiao the king of Wenxin, followed him into the Shu Han Dynasty, returned to the Three Qin Dynasty, and killed Xiang Ji.
After ascending to the throne, Liu Jiao and Lu Wan often served the emperor, coming in and out of the bedroom, and conveying various internal affairs secrets to the outside world.
The emperor's cousin Liu Jia led troops to cooperate with the main force in many battles.
In the sixth year of the Han Dynasty, King Xin of Chu was deposed and his territory was divided into two kingdoms. Liu Jia was made King of Jing and Liu Jiao was King of Chu. They ruled thirty-six counties of Xuejun, Donghai and Pengcheng because of their previous meritorious service.
Later, his second brother Liu Zhong was made acting king, and his eldest son Liu Fei was made king of Qi.
At first, when Emperor Gaozu was humble, he often took refuge and ate at his sister-in-law's house with his guests.
My sister-in-law hated her brother-in-law and guests coming, so she pretended that she had finished the soup and scraped the pot with a spoon, so the guests all left.
Emperor Gaozu then saw that there was soup in the pot, so he resented his sister-in-law.
After the kings of Qi and Dai were established, only Liu Bo's son did not get the title of marquis.
The Supreme Emperor spoke for him, and Gaozu said: I dare not forget to confer him a title because his mother is not kind.
In October of the seventh year, Liu Xin, the son of Uncle Liu, was granted the title of Genjiehou.
When King Yuan arrived in Chu, he appointed Mu Sheng, Bai Sheng, and Shen Gong as middle-level officials.
When he was in high school, Uncle Fuqiu was in Chang'an, and King Yuan sent his son Ying Ke to complete his studies with Duke Shen.
During the reign of Emperor Wen, he heard that Shen Gong was the most proficient in studying "Poetry" and he was appointed a doctor.
King Yuan liked "Poems" and all his sons read "Poems". Duke Shen began to write a biography of "Poems", which was called "Lu Shi".
King Yuan also compiled a biography of "Poems", which is called "Poems of King Yuan". There may still be poems by him in the world.
When he was a queen, he ordered Ying Ke, the son of King Yuan, to be Zongzheng and granted the title of Pihou.
King Yuan died in the twenty-third year of his reign, and Prince Pifei died first. Emperor Wen ordered Zongzheng to ascend the throne to Pihou Yingke, who was the king of Yi.
Duke Shen became a doctor and was relieved of his official duties. He went back with Ying Ke and was appointed as a middle doctor.
After reigning for four years, his son Liu Wu succeeded him.
Emperor Wen favored King Yuan, gave birth to a son, and was given the title of Prince Bi.
Emperor Jing ascended the throne and named five of King Yuan's favored sons in the name of relatives: his son Liu Li was the Marquis of Pinglu, Liu Fu was the Marquis of Xiu, Liu Sui was the Marquis of Shen You, Liu Zhi was the Marquis of Wanqu, and Liu Tiao was the Marquis of Jile.
At first, King Yuan respectfully reiterated his dignity, but Musheng did not like drinking. King Yuan often prepared sweet wine for Musheng every time he served wine.
When Wang Liuwu came to the throne, he often prepared it, but later he forgot to put sweet wine.
