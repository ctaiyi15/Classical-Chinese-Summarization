Every time he goes to court, when the emperor is free, he always makes remarks without praising the wise people in the world.
When recommending scholars, prime ministers, historians and other subordinate officials, he praised them with great enthusiasm and often mentioned that he thought they were more talented than himself.
He never calls his subordinates by their first names, and talks to them as if he is afraid of hurting them.
I heard the kind words spoken by others and recommended them to the emperor, but I was afraid that I would be too slow.

Zheng Zhuang was sent to inspect the breach of the Yellow River, and he applied for a five-day leave to repair his luggage.
The emperor said: I heard that Zheng Zhuang traveled thousands of miles without food. Now I am applying for leave to pack my luggage. Why?
However, when Zheng Zhuang was discussing matters in the court, he often echoed and catered to the emperor's wishes and did not dare to clearly state whether his position was right or wrong.
The Han Dynasty conquered the Xiongnu and recruited the Yi tribes from all directions. The country spent a lot of money and became increasingly depleted of financial and material resources.
When Zheng Zhuang was appointed as the chief minister, some of the people he recommended and the guests who were familiar with him were engaged in transportation for the chief minister, and they owed a lot of money.
