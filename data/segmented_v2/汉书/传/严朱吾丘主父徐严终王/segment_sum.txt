He argued that the territory was not worth the cost, citing historical precedents like Yao, Shun, and the Zhou Dynasty, which did not forcibly conquer distant lands. He contrasted Emperor Wen's frugality and peace with Emperor Wu's costly expansions, which led to widespread suffering and rebellion. Emperor Yuan accepted his advice and abandoned Zhuli County. Later, Jia Ganzhi and Yang Xing, a favored official, conspired to recommend each other for high positions, but Shi Xian reported their scheme.
