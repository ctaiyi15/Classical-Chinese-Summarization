韦贤字长孺。鲁国邹人也。
其先韦孟，家本彭城，为楚元王傅，傅子夷王及孙王戊。
戊荒淫不遵道，孟作诗风谏。
后遂去位，徒家于邹，又作一篇。
其谏诗曰：
肃肃我祖，国自豕韦，黼衣朱绂，四牡龙旂。
彤弓斯征，抚宁遐荒，总齐群邦，以翼大商，迭披大彭，勋绩惟光。
至于有周，历世会同。
王赧听谮，实绝我邦。
我邦既绝，厥政斯逸，赏罚之行，非由王室。
庶尹群后，靡扶靡卫，五服崩离，宗周以队。
我祖斯微，迁于彭城，在予小子，勤诶厥生，厄此嫚秦，耒耜以耕。
悠悠嫚秦，上天不宁，乃眷南顾，授汉于京。
于赫有汉，四方是征，靡适不怀，万国逌平。
乃命厥弟，建侯于楚，俾我小臣，惟傅是辅。
兢兢元王，恭俭净一，惠此黎民，纳彼辅弼。
飨国渐世，垂烈于后，乃及夷王，克奉厥绪。
咨命不永，唯王统祀，左右陪臣，此惟皇士。
如何我王，不思守保，不惟履冰，以继祖考！
邦事是废，逸游是娱，犬马繇繇，是放是驱。
务彼鸟兽，忽此稼苗，烝民以匮，我王以愉。
所弘非德，所亲非悛，唯囿是恢，唯谀是信。
睮々谄夫，咢咢黄发，如何我王，曾不是察！

Wei Xian, whose courtesy name is Changru, was born in Zou, State of Lu.
His ancestor was Wei Meng, who originally lived in Pengcheng and was the Fu of King Yuan of Chu. He was also the Fu of King Yuan's son, King Yi, and his grandson, Liu Wu.
Liu Wu was dissolute and disobedient, so Wei Meng wrote poems to ridicule and admonish him.
Later, Wei Meng resigned from his official position, moved his family to Zoudi, and wrote another remonstrative poem.

Our solemn and great ancestors founded the country from the time of Weishi, wearing fur clothes decorated with ax-shaped patterns and Zhu Fu decorated with "Ya" patterns, driving a chariot drawn by four male horses, with dragon pins on them.
He was given the Tong Gong and was responsible for conquests; he pacified the people in remote areas, took charge of many states, and assisted the Shang Dynasty. Later, there was Dapeng, who also made outstanding achievements.
By the time of the Zhou Dynasty, our Chu state had been among the princes for several generations and participated in alliances.
Later, King Nan of Zhou believed the slander and deprived us of the title of Chu State.
Our Chu State has severed relations with the Zhou Dynasty, and the laws of the Zhou Dynasty are no longer implemented in the Chu State. The matter of rewards and punishments was no longer decided by the Zhou Dynasty.
The ministers of the imperial court and the princes and princes no longer assisted and protected the Zhou Dynasty; the five areas around the capital fell apart, and Zong Zhou collapsed like this.
Our ancestors also declined and migrated to Pengcheng. In our generation, life is really hard-working.
Facing the disaster of being bullied by the powerful Qin, the late king had to go to the fields to cultivate the land himself.
The arrogant and arrogant Qin State, God will not bless them. God favored and blessed the south, and granted the capital of the Qin Dynasty to the Han Dynasty. The establishment of the Han Dynasty was truly great, with conquests in all directions. Everywhere they went surrendered, and thousands of states were at peace.
He appointed his younger brother to establish a marquis and a state in the Chu land; let us, the little ministers, assist the King of Chu carefully.
He is the king of the Yuan Dynasty who is diligent and conscientious. He is respectful, diligent and frugal and lasts for a long time. He blesses all the people with happiness and health, and appoints assistants and ministers to recruit virtuous people.
After reigning for nearly thirty years, his legacy was passed down to his descendants; later he was succeeded as King Yi, who was able to carry on the legacy.
It is a pity that King Yi did not live long. King Wu ascended the throne and was succeeded by King Wu. All the ministers around him, gentlemen and gentlemen, are all pillars.
Why does our king not consider preserving the ancestral heritage? Why does he not think about the difficulties of walking on thin ice, so as to inherit and carry forward the ancestral heritage!
The country's major affairs are neglected, and they wander around for endless entertainment all day long; the hounds and horses are beautiful and majestic, running here and there in front and behind.
He loves animals and birds wholeheartedly, but neglects farm work and forgets about crops. The people were in want because of this, and the king took pleasure in it.
What is promoted is not virtue, and what is close to is not talent. Just expanding the garden, just getting close to villains.
A flattering person, an upright gentleman; why doesn't the king clearly understand this?

summary line 0: Wei Xian, courtesy name Changru, was from Zou in the State of Lu. His ancestor, Wei Meng, served as a tutor to King Yuan of Chu and his descendants. Because Liu Wu was dissolute, Wei Meng wrote poems to admonish him, then resigned and moved his family to Zoudi, where he wrote another remonstrative poem. This poem recounts the history of their lineage from the Shang Dynasty through the Zhou, the decline of Chu, and the rise of the Han Dynasty. It criticizes the current king for neglecting state affairs, indulging in pleasure, and surrounding himself with flatterers, warning him to learn from past examples. Wei Meng later died in Zoudi. It was passed down for five generations to Wei Xian.

或曰其子孙好事，述先人之志而作是诗也。
自孟至贤五世。
贤为人质朴少欲，笃志于学，兼能《礼》、《尚书》，以《诗》教授，号称邹鲁大儒。
征为博士，给事中，进授昭帝《诗》，稍迁光禄大夫、詹事，至大鸿胪。
昭帝崩，无嗣，大将军霍光与公卿共尊立孝宣帝。
帝初即位，贤以与谋议，安宗庙，赐爵关内侯，食邑。
徙为长信少府，以先帝师，甚见尊重。
本始三年，代蔡义为丞相，封扶阳侯，食邑七百户。
时，贤七十余，为相五岁，地节三年以老病乞骸骨，赐黄金百斤，罢归，加赐第一区。
丞相致仕自贤始。
年八十二薨，谥曰节侯。
贤四子：长子方山为高寝令，早终；次子弘，至东海太守；次子舜，留鲁守坟墓；少子玄成，复以明经历位至丞相。
故邹鲁谚曰： 遗子黄金满籝，不如一经。
玄成字少翁，以父任为郎，常侍骑。
少好学，修父业，尤谦逊下士。
出遇知识步行，辄下从者，与载送之，以为常。
其接人，贫贱者益加敬，繇是名誉日广。
以明经擢为谏大夫，迁大河都尉。
初，玄成兄弘为太常丞，职奉宗庙，典诸陵邑，烦剧多罪过。
父贤以弘当为嗣，故敕令自免。弘怀谦，不去官。
及贤病笃，弘竟坐宗庙事系狱，罪未决。
室家问贤当为后者，贤恚恨不肯言。
于是贤门下生博士义倩等与宗家计议，共矫贤令，使家丞上书言大行，以大河都尉玄成为后。
贤薨，玄成在官闻丧，又言当为嗣，玄成深知其非贤雅意，即阳为病狂，卧便利，妄笑语昏乱。
征至长安，既葬，当袭爵，以病狂不应召。
大鸿胪奏状，章下丞相、御史案验。
玄成素有名声，士大夫多疑其欲让爵辟兄者。
案事丞相史乃与玄成书曰： 古之辞让，必有文义可观，故能垂荣于后。
translation: 
Some people say that his descendants wrote this poem because they did good deeds and recalled the ambitions of their ancestors.
It has been passed down for five generations from Wei Meng to Wei Xian.
Wei Xian was a simple man with a pure heart and few desires. He devoted himself to learning. He was proficient in "Li" and "Shangshu" and taught "Poetry" to others. He was called the great Confucian of Zou Lu.
Later, the imperial court recruited him as a doctor and appointed him as an official. He entered the palace to teach Emperor Zhao to study "Poetry", and was slowly promoted to Guanglu doctor Zhan Shi, and later to Dahonglu.
Emperor Zhao died without an heir, so the general Huo Guang and the ministers honored him as Emperor Xiaoxuan.
Emperor Xuan had just ascended the throne. Wei Xian was given the title of Marquis of Guannei and had a household to worship him because he participated in the plan to support Emperor Xuan, stabilize the country and stabilize the country.
Moved to the Shaofu of Changxin Palace. Because Wei Xian was once the teacher of Emperor Zhao, he was very respected.
In the third year of Benshi, Wei Xian replaced Cai Yi as prime minister and was granted the title of Marquis of Fuyang, with a settlement of 700 households.
At that time, Wei Xian was over 70 years old. He had been prime minister for five years and had been in Jiji for three years. He asked the court to resign because of his old age and illness. The court rewarded him with one hundred catties of gold, dismissed him and returned to his hometown, and also gave him a mansion.
The resignation of the prime minister began with Wei Xian.
Wei Xian died at the age of eighty-two, and the imperial court gave him the title of Jiehou.
Wei Xian had four sons: the eldest son, Wei Fangshan, served as the magistrate of Gaodi County and died very early; the second son, Wei Hong, was promoted to the prefect of Shuhai; the third son, Wei Shun, stayed in Lu to guard the cemetery of Wei's ancestral hall; the youngest son, Wei Xuancheng, was promoted to the position of prime minister many times because of his proficiency in Confucian classics.
Therefore, there is a proverb in Zoulu: It is better to leave a box full of gold to your son than to leave him a scripture.
Wei Xuancheng, a young man, was appointed as a Langguan and a regular attendant on Sanqi due to his father's favor.
He was eager to learn when he was young and inherited his father's Confucianism. He was extremely humble and courteous to virtuous people.
Sometimes when he went out and met people he knew on foot, he would always ask his attendants to get off the car and take them back as a normal thing.
He treated people, including the poor and humble, with even greater respect, and his reputation spread far and wide.
Because he was proficient in Confucian classics, he was promoted to the position of admonishing doctor and then moved to the rank of captain of Dahe County.
At the beginning, Xuancheng's elder brother Wei Hong was serving as Taichang Cheng, and his duties were to guard the ancestral temple and be in charge of the imperial tombs and gardens. Because of the complicated affairs, he committed many crimes.
His father Wei Xian, because Wei Hong should be the heir of the Wei family in the future, was afraid that he would be deposed for committing crimes, so he ordered Wei Hong to resign from the official position of Taichang Cheng due to illness. However, Wei Hong was humble and refused to resign.
When Wei Xian became seriously ill, Wei Hong was finally imprisoned for not being considerate in guarding the ancestral temple, and had not yet been convicted.
The family members asked Wei Xian who should be the heir to the Wei family, but Wei Xian showed an expression of anger and hatred and refused to speak.
So Wei Xian's disciple, Dr. Yi Qian, and others discussed with Wei Xian's clan members, pretending that it was Wei Xian's order, and asked the general manager of the Wei family to write a general order to establish Wei Xuan, the captain of Dahe County, as the successor of the Wei family.
After Wei Xian's death, Xuancheng heard the bad news in his official position and heard that he should be the heir. Xuancheng knew that dismissal was not what his father Wei Xian had always meant, so he pretended to be insane, urinated and defecated in bed, talked nonsense, and laughed wildly.
The imperial court summoned him to Chang'an. After the funeral, Wei Xuancheng was supposed to inherit his father's title, but Xuancheng pretended to be insane and ignored the imperial edict.
Da Honglu wrote to the court, and the court issued documents to the prime minister and censor, asking them to investigate and handle the matter.
Xuancheng always had a good reputation. Many scholar-bureaucrats believed that Xuancheng wanted to give up his title to his brother Wei Hong, so he pretended to be insane.
Prime Minister Shi, who was in charge of the investigation, then wrote to Xuancheng, saying: The ancients must have a decent name when they resigned, so that their reputation can be passed down to future generations.
