He criticized Prime Minister Zhai Fangjin for bias and corruption, and later defended Dong Hong's proposal to honor the emperor's mother. Du Ye was demoted to captain of Shangdang and eventually dismissed from office. When Wang Mang came to power, Du Ye, fearing punishment, fell ill and died. The Du family, starting with Du Zhou and Du Yannian, were known as harsh officials but produced virtuous sons. Their lineage continued until the end of the Han Dynasty, with no one surpassing them after the great heroes and scholars.
