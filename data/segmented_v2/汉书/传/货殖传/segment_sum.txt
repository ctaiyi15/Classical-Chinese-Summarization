昔先王之制，自天子、公、侯、卿、大夫、士至于皂隶、抱关、击者，其爵禄、奉养、宫室、车服、棺椁、祭祀、死生之制各有差品，小不得僣大，贱不得逾贵。
夫然，故上下序而民志定。
于是辩其土地、川泽、丘陵、衍沃、原隰之宜，教民种树畜养；五谷六畜及至鱼鳖、鸟兽、雚蒲、材干、器械之资，所以养生送终之具，靡不皆育。
然后四民因其土宜，各任智力，夙兴夜寐，以治其业，相与通功易事，交利而俱赡，非有征发期会，而远近咸足。
故《易》曰 后以财成辅相天地之宜，以左右民 ， 备物致用，立成器以为天下利，莫大乎圣人 。
此之谓也《管子》云古之四民不得杂处。
士相与言仁谊于闲宴，工相与议技巧于官府，商相与语财利于市井，农相与谋稼穑于田野，朝夕从事，不见异物而迁焉。
故其父兄之教不肃而成，子弟之学不劳而能，各安其居而乐其业，甘其食而美其服，虽见奇丽纷华，非其所习，辟犹戎翟之与于越，不相入矣。
是以欲寡而事节，财足而不争。
于是在民上者，道之以德，齐之以礼，故民有耻而且敬，贵谊而贱利。
此三代之所以直道而行，不严而治之大略也。
translation: 
In the past, the system of the kings, from the emperor, princes to slaves, their official titles, salary, life and death system had different levels. The small ones could not surpass the big ones, and the lowly ones could not surpass the noble ones. Therefore, the top and bottom were orderly and the people's hearts were stable. Therefore, different geographical conditions such as land, rivers, lakes, hills, fertile land, plains, and depressions were identified, and the people were taught planting and livestock raising techniques. In this way, the people's supplies for daily life and funerals, including grains, livestock, fish, turtles, birds, beasts, firewood, wood, instruments, and other materials were produced. Production must follow certain seasons, and consumption must be controlled.
Before the first month of the lunar calendar, one cannot go into the mountains and forests to cut down trees. Before the first month of the lunar calendar, one cannot go to the rivers and lakes to fish.
In addition to complying with seasonal production, they are also not allowed to cut down small trees in the mountains, cut young grass by the lake, catch young insects, fish, or beasts, or collect bird eggs. This is to adapt to the seasonal climate and allow various organisms to reproduce and thrive. In this way, you can give full play to the effects of nature and make all kinds of property reserves abundant.
Then, according to the natural conditions of the area, the scholars, farmers, industry and merchants gave full play to their intelligence and physical strength, got up early and went to bed late, managed their own industries, and exchanged with each other: the results of their work met their respective needs. In this way, there is no additional requisition of civilian manpower and material resources, and there is sufficient property in areas near and far.
Therefore, the "Book of Changes" says: The monarch uses decrees to determine the changes in nature, assists the heaven and earth to suit the needs of the world, teaches the people to engage in production, produces various kinds of property, uses it in various aspects, and makes various utensils so that the world can benefit. This is the greatness of the sage, and this is what it means.
"Guanzi" said: In ancient times, scholars, farmers, workers, and merchants were not allowed to live together.
Scholars discuss benevolence and righteousness with each other in the place where they study, craftsmen discuss skills with each other in the government office, businessmen discuss wealth and profit with each other in the market, and farmers discuss farming affairs with each other in the fields. They are engaged in one occupation from morning to night and do not change when things change.
Therefore, even if their father and brother's teachings are not strict, they can teach their children well; their children can learn skills even if they do not work hard. They are content with where they live and are happy to pursue their own professions. Even if they see strange and gorgeous items, they will not accept them because they are not suitable for their customs. This is just like the customs of the Rong people and Di people in the northwest and the Wuyue people in the southeast cannot be integrated.
In this case, people's desires are few and things are simple, and property is abundant without competition.
So the rulers use morality to guide them and use rituals to unify them. Therefore, the common people have integrity and courtesy, value benevolence and righteousness, and despise wealth.
This is the summary of why the Xia, Shang and Zhou dynasties were able to develop along the right path and govern the country without resorting to harsh politics.

If you understand the relationship between time changes and the supply and demand of goods, you can understand the market conditions of various goods.

This is in line with the laws of development and change of things. The State of Yue pursued the policy of Ji Ran, and after ten years of rule, the country became greatly wealthy. It rewarded the soldiers with large sums of money, and finally avenged the State of Wu and washed away the shame of being besieged in Kuaiji.
