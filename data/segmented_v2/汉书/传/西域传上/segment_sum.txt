西域以孝武时始通，本三十六国，其后稍分至五十余，皆在匈奴之西，乌孙之南。
南北有大山，中央有河，东西六千余里，南北千余里。
东则接汉，厄以玉门、阳关，西则限以葱岭。
其南山，东出金城，与汉南山属焉。
其河有两原：一出葱岭出，一出于阗。
于阗在南山下，其河北流，与葱岭河合，东注蒲昌海。
蒲昌海，一名盐泽者也，去玉门、阳关三百余里，广袤三四百里。
其水亭居，冬夏不增减，皆以为潜行地下，南出于积石，为中国河云。
自玉门、阳关出西域有两道：从鄯善傍南山北，波河西行至莎车，为南道，南道西逾葱岭则出大月氏、安息。
自车师前王廷随北山，波河西行至疏勒，为北道，北道西逾葱岭则出大宛、康居、奄蔡焉。
西域诸国大率土著，有城郭田畜，与匈奴、乌孙异俗，故皆役属匈奴。
匈奴西边日逐王置僮仆都尉，使领西域，常居焉耆、危须、尉黎间，赋税诸国，取富给焉。
自周衰，戎狄错居泾渭之北。
及秦始皇攘却戎狄，筑长城，界中国，然西不过临洮。
汉兴至于孝武，事征四夷，广威德，而张骞始开西域之迹。
其后骠骑将军击破匈奴右地，降浑邪、休屠王，遂空其地，始筑令居以西，初置酒泉郡，后稍发徙民充实之，分置武威、张掖、敦煌，列四郡，据两关焉。
自贰师将军伐大宛之后，西域震惧，多遣使来贡献。汉使西域者益得职。
于是自敦煌西至盐泽，往往起亭，而轮台、渠犁皆有田卒数百人，置使者校尉领护，以给使外国者。
至宣帝时，遣卫司马使护鄯善以西数国。
及破姑师，未尽殄，分以为车师前后王及山北六国。
时汉独护南道，未能尽并北道也。然匈奴不自安矣。
其后日逐王畔单于，将众来降，护鄯善以西使者郑吉迎之。
既至汉，封日逐王为归德侯，吉为安远侯。
是岁，神爵二年也。
乃因使吉并护北道，故号曰都护。
都护之起，自吉置矣。
僮仆都尉由此罢，匈奴益弱，不得近西域。
于是徙屯田，田于北胥鞬，披莎车之地，屯田校尉始属都护。
都护督察乌孙、康居诸外国，动静有变以闻。
translation: 
The Western Regions began to communicate with the Central Plains from the time of Emperor Wu of the Han Dynasty. There were originally thirty-six countries in that area. Later, it was gradually divided into more than fifty kingdoms, all of which were located to the west of the Xiongnu and south of the Wusun.
The Western Region has large mountains in the north and south, and a river in the center. It is more than 6,000 miles wide from east to west and more than 1,000 miles long from north to south.
It is connected to the Han Dynasty in the east, with Yumen Pass and Yang Pass as dangerous fortresses, and Congling as the boundary in the west.
Its Nanshan Mountain starts from Jincheng County and is connected to the Nanshan Mountain of the Han Dynasty.
Its river has two sources: one originates from Congling Mountain and the other originates from Khotan.
Khotan is at the foot of Nanshan Mountain. The river flows north, merges with the Congling River, and then flows eastward into the Puchang Sea.
Puchanghai, also known as Yanze, is more than 300 miles away from Yumenguan and Yangguan. The length and width of the lake are about 300 miles.
The lake water is stable and does not increase or decrease in winter and summer. The lake water flows underground and emerges from Jishi Mountain to the south, which is the Huanghui in the Central Plains.
There are two roads from Yumenguan and Yangguan to the Western Regions. From Shanshan, follow the north side of Nanshan and go west along the Tarim River to Yarkand, which is the south road; the south road goes west across Congling to reach Dayuezhi and Anxi.
From Cheshiqian Wangting, follow the south side of Beishan and go west along the Tarim River to Shule, which is the north road: the north road goes west across Congling to reach Dawan, Kangju, and Yancai.
Most of the countries in the Western Regions lived a settled life, with cities, fields, and livestock. They were different from the customs of the Xiongnu and Wusun. They were all enslaved and subordinate to the Xiongnu in the past.
King Yue Zhu in the western Xiongnu set up a boy servant captain to manage the western regions. He was often stationed in Yanqi, Weixu, Weili and other places, collecting taxes from various countries, and he was very wealthy.
Since the decline of the Zhou Dynasty, the Rong, Di and other ethnic groups have lived together in the north of the Jingshui and Weishui rivers.
When Qin Shihuang arrived, he drove away the Rong and Di and built the Great Wall as the border of the Central Plains countries, but the west of Qin did not exceed Lintao County.
From the establishment of the Western Han Dynasty to the time of Emperor Wu, he managed the surrounding ethnic areas and promoted mighty virtue, so Zhang Qian began to open the road to the Western Regions.
Later, Husband General Huo Qubing defeated the Xiongnu in the right area, and King Hunxie and Xiutu surrendered. There were no Xiongnu in the right area. The Han Dynasty began to build beacons to the west of Lingju and began to establish Jiuquan County. Later, the people were recruited to live here, and Wuwei, Zhangye, and Dunhuang were established in four counties, and they guarded Yumen and Yangguan Pass.
Since Li Guangli, the general of the Second Division, attacked Dayuan, all countries in the Western Regions were frightened. Most countries sent envoys to Chang'an to pay tribute. The Han Dynasty's envoys to the Western Regions were increasingly rewarded and promoted.
So from the west of Dunhuang to Yanze, pavilions and barriers were built everywhere. Hundreds of field soldiers were stationed in Luntai and Quli. Envoys and captains were set up to supervise the field affairs and provide livelihood for the Han Dynasty's envoys to foreign countries.
During the reign of Emperor Xuan, Wei Sima was assigned to oversee several countries west of Shanshan.
When it came time to defeat Gu Shi, he did not eliminate them all, but divided them into the former king of Cheshi, the queen of Cheshi, and the six kingdoms of Shanbei.
At that time, the Han Dynasty only supervised the southern route and did not annex all the northern routes, but the Huns were already feeling very uneasy.
Later, King Rizhu betrayed Chanyu and led his troops to surrender to the Han Dynasty. Zheng Ji, the envoy of the Han Dynasty who protected Shanshan from the west, met King Rizhu.
In the Han Dynasty, the Han Dynasty named Zhu Wang as the Marquis of Guide and Zheng Ji as the Marquis of Anyuan.
This year is the third year of Shenjue.
The Han Dynasty sent Zheng Ji to protect the North Road, so it was called "Duhu".
The establishment of the Protectorate began with Zheng Ji.
The Xiongnu's child servant captain originally stationed in the Western Regions has since been dismissed. The Xiongnu has become even weaker and cannot approach the Western Regions.
So the Han Dynasty moved the people to farm in Beixu Tar, dividing the land of Shache. From then on, the military commander of the farm began to belong to the military guard.
The Protectorate monitors the situation in Wusun, Kangju and other foreign countries, and reports to the emperor immediately if there is any movement.

summary:
summary line 0: The Western Regions began communication with the Central Plains during Emperor Wu of Han, originally comprising thirty-six countries that later divided into over fifty kingdoms, all located west of the Xiongnu and south of the Wusun. The region spans more than 6,000 miles east to west and over 1,000 miles north to south, bordered by the Han Dynasty to the east with Yumen Pass and Yang Pass as fortresses, and Congling to the west. Its central river has two sources: one from Congling Mountain and another from Khotan, flowing north to merge and eastward into Puchang Sea (also known as Yanze), a stable lake about 300 miles wide. The lake water flows underground, emerging at Jishi Mountain as the Huanghui in the Central Plains. Two main roads lead from Yumen and Yang Pass to the Western Regions: the south road via Shanshan and Yarkand, crossing Congling to reach Dayuezhi and Anxi; the north road via Cheshi and Shule, crossing Congling to reach Dawan, Kangju, and Yancai.
