明经为郡文学，以孝廉为郎。
举方正，对策高第，迁谏大夫，行郎中户将事。
劾奏卫将军张安世子侍中阳都侯彭祖不下殿门，并连及安世居位无补。
彭祖时实下门，宽饶坐举奏大臣非是，左迁为卫司马。
先是时，卫司马在部，见卫尉拜谒，常为卫官繇使市买。
宽饶视事，案旧令，遂揖官属以下行卫者。
卫尉私使宽饶出，宽饶以令诣官府门上谒辞。
尚书责问卫尉，由是卫官不复私使候、司马。
候、司马不拜，出先置卫，辄上奏辞，自此正焉。
宽饶初拜为司马，未出殿门，断其禅衣，令短离地，冠大冠，带长剑，躬案行士卒庐室，视其饮食居处，有疾病者身自抚循临问，加致医药，遇之甚有恩。
及岁尽交代，上临飨罢卫卒，卫卒数千人皆叩头自请，愿复留共更一年，以报宽饶厚德。
宣帝嘉之，以宽饶为太中大夫，使行风俗，多所称举贬黜，奉使称意。
擢为司隶校尉，刺举无所回避，小大辄举，所劾奏众多，廷尉处其法，半用半不用，公卿贵戚及郡国吏繇使至长安，皆恐惧莫敢犯禁，京师为清。
平恩侯许伯入第，丞相、御史、将军、中二千石皆贺，宽饶不行。
许伯请之，乃往，从西阶上，东乡特坐。
许伯自酌曰： 盖君后至。
宽饶曰： 无多酌我，我乃酒狂。
丞相魏侯笑曰： 次公醒而狂，何必酒也？
坐者毕属目卑下之。
酒酣乐作，长信少府檀长卿起舞，为沐猴与狗斗，坐皆大笑。
宽饶不说，卬视屋而叹曰： 美哉！
translation: 
Gai Kuanrao, courtesy name Cigong, was from Weijun.
Because he was proficient in classics, he served as a county scholar, and because of his filial piety and integrity, he became a Langguan.
He was promoted to the title of Founder, took the imperial examination and achieved excellent results, was promoted to the imperial censor, and acted as the deputy general of the household.
He impeached Pengzu, the son of General Zhang Anshi of the Imperial Guard, for refusing to get out of the car in front of the palace gate, and because Anshi had no political achievements in his official position.
Peng Zu actually got off the car in front of the palace gate at that time. Kuan Rao committed the crime of reporting the minister's misrepresentation and was demoted to Sima Wei.
Before that, Sima Wei was in the official office. When he saw the Wei Wei, he wanted to pay homage and often went out to buy things for the Wei Wei.
After Kuanrao became the Sima of the Guards, according to the previous system, he bowed his hands and saluted the officials who were responsible for patrolling the guards.
Weiwei sent Kuanrao out privately, and Kuanrao went to Shangshu Yamen to submit an application according to the system.
The minister then questioned the Wei Wei, and from then on the Wei officials no longer sent Pai Hou and Sima without permission.
Hou and Sima no longer paid homage to the emperor. When the emperor traveled, Hou and Sima, as the forerunners, had to give memorials first, and this was institutionalized from then on.
Kuanrao was appointed as Sima Chu. He did not leave the palace gate, cut his single coat short so that it could leave the ground, wore a big hat, and carried a long sword. He personally visited the soldiers' houses, inspected their diet and daily life, personally comforted and asked the sick soldiers, gave them medicine, and treated them very kindly.
When the shift was handed over at the end of the year, the emperor personally rewarded the retired soldiers. Thousands of soldiers kowtowed and asked to stay and serve for another year to repay the generous kindness.
Emperor Xuan commended him for his kindness, appointed him as Taizhong doctor, and asked him to inspect the customs of various places. He commended, recommended, demoted, and deposed many people, and completed the mission in line with the emperor's wishes.
Therefore, he was promoted to the rank of Sili Xiaowei, and he was able to inspect and expose the mistakes of officials without hesitation. Small and big matters were reported to the public, and many people were impeached. The Tingwei dealt with them according to the law, and half of his opinions were adopted and half were not adopted. However, the ministers, nobles, and officials of the county and state who were envoys to Chang'an were all too afraid to violate the ban, and the capital was peaceful.
When Ping Enhou Xu Bo moved into his new residence, all the prime ministers, censors, generals, and officials with a salary of two thousand dan went to congratulate him, but Kuan Rao did not go.
Uncle Xu invited him, so he went up to the hall from the west steps and sat alone facing the east.
Uncle Xu poured him wine himself and said: "You are late."
Kuanrao said: Don't pour more wine for me. I'll go crazy if I drink too much.
Prime Minister Wei Hou smiled and said: "You are a little crazy when you are awake. Why do you have to drink?"
Everyone present looked at him with contempt.
When the wine was in full swing, the music started playing. Tan Changqing, the young master of Changxin, got up and danced, performing a monkey and dog fight, and everyone present laughed.
Kuangrao was unhappy, looked up at the roof and sighed, "How beautiful!"

summary:
summary line 0: Gai Kuanrao, from Weijun, rose through the ranks due to his classical knowledge and filial piety. As an imperial censor, he impeached Pengzu, son of General Zhang Anshi, for refusing to dismount at the palace gate. However, since Pengzu had actually dismounted, Kuanrao was demoted for misrepresentation. As Sima of the Guards, he reformed protocols, personally cared for soldiers' welfare, and earned such loyalty that retiring soldiers requested to serve another year. Emperor Xuan promoted him to Sili Xiaowei, where he fearlessly investigated officials, creating peace in the capital. At a celebration for Xu Bo's new residence, Kuanrao sat apart and warned about the impermanence of wealth. He later impeached a performer for disrespectful dancing. Despite his integrity and poverty—using half his salary to pay informants—his harshness and tendency to frame others created enemies. Wang Sheng advised him to be more cautious, but Kuanrao ignored this counsel. He submitted a memorial criticizing the emperor's reliance on eunuchs and suggesting that rulers should yield to the worthy. The emperor deemed this treasonous. Despite Zheng Chang's defense, Kuanrao was imprisoned and committed suicide.

里语曰： 腐木不可以为柱，卑人不可以为主。 天人之所不予，必有祸而无福，市道皆共知之，朝廷莫肯一言，臣窃伤心。

于是中朝左将军辛庆忌、右将军廉褒、光禄勋师丹、太中大夫谷永俱上书曰： 臣闻明王垂宽容之听，崇谏争之官，广开忠直之路，不罪狂狷之言，然后百僚在位，竭忠尽谋，不惧后患，朝廷无谄谀之士，元首无失道之愆。
窃见谏大夫刘辅，前以县令求见，擢为谏大夫，此其言必有卓诡切至，当圣心者，故得拔至于此。
旬日之间，收下秘狱，臣等愚，以为辅幸得托公族之亲，在谏臣之列，新从下土来，未知朝廷体，独触忌讳，不足深过。
小罪宜隐忍而已，如有大恶，宜暴治理官，与众共之。
昔赵简子杀其大夫鸣犊，孔子临河而还。

而行惨急之诛于谏争之臣，震惊群下，失忠直心。
假令辅不坐直言，所坐不著，天下不可户晓。
同姓近臣本以言显，其于治亲养忠之义诚不宜幽囚于掖庭狱。
公卿以下见陛下进用辅亟，而折伤之暴，人有惧心，精锐销耎，莫敢尽节正言，非所以昭有虞之听，广德美之风也。
臣等窃深伤之，唯陛下留神省察。
上乃徙系辅共工狱，减死罪一等，论为鬼薪。
终于家。
