高皇后吕氏，生惠帝。佐高祖定天下，父兄及高祖而侯者三人。
惠帝即位，尊吕后为太后。
太后立帝姊鲁元公主女为皇后，无子，取后宫美人子名之以为太子。
惠帝崩，太子立为皇帝，年幼，太后临朝称制，大赦天下。
乃立兄子吕台、产、禄、台子通四人为王，封诸吕六人为列侯。
语在《外戚传》。
元年春正月，诏曰： 前日孝惠皇帝言欲除三族罪、妖言令，议未决而崩。
今除之。
二月，赐民爵，户一级。
初置孝弟力田二千石者一人。
夏五月丙申，赵王宫丛台灾。
立孝惠后宫子强为淮阳王，不疑为恒山王，弘为襄城侯，朝为轵侯，武为壶关侯。
秋，桃李华。
二年春，诏曰： 高皇帝匡饬天下，诸有功者皆受分弟为列侯，万民大安，莫不受休德。
朕思念至于久远而功名不著，亡以尊大谊，施后世。
今欲差次列侯功以定朝位，臧于高庙，世世勿绝，嗣子各袭其功位。其与列侯议定奏之。
丞相臣平言： 谨与绛侯臣勃、曲周侯臣商、颍阴侯臣婴、安国侯臣陵等议：列侯幸得赐餐钱奉邑，陛下加惠，以功次定朝位，臣请臧高庙。
奏可。
春正月乙卯，地震，羌道、武都道山崩。
夏六月丙戌晦，日有蚀之。
秋七月，恒山王不疑薨。行八铢钱。
三年夏，江水、汉水溢，流民四千余家。
秋，星昼见。
四年夏，少帝自知非皇后子，出怨言，皇太后幽之永巷。
translation: 
Empress Gao, the Lu family, gave birth to Emperor Hui, who assisted Emperor Gaozu of the Han Dynasty to pacify the world. Her father and brother had three marquises under Emperor Gaozu.
After Emperor Hui succeeded to the throne, he honored Empress Lu as the Queen Mother.
The Queen Mother made the daughter of Princess Lu Yuan, the sister of Emperor Hui, the Queen. She had no children, so she made the son of a beauty in the harem the Prince.
Emperor Hui died, and the prince was established as emperor. The emperor was young, and Empress Dowager Lu came to the court to perform the emperor's affairs and grant amnesty to the world.
Later, four of his brother's sons, Lu Tai, Lu Chan, Lu Lu, and Lu Tai's son Lu Tong were made kings, and six Zhu Lu were made princes.
The specific details can be found in "Book of Han·Biography of Wai Qi".
In the first month of the spring of the first year of Empress Lu's reign, she issued an edict saying: It turned out that Emperor Xiaohui intended to abolish the harsh laws that punished serious crimes and three clans, and regarded wrong words as evil words. He died before the resolution was resolved.
It has now been announced that it will be abolished.
In February, the people were rewarded with titles of nobility, one level for each household.
For the first time, an official with an annual salary of two thousand shi was set up to encourage the world's filial piety.
On the fourth day of May in the summer, a fire broke out in the Congtai of Zhao Palace.
Liu Qiang, the son of Emperor Xiaohui's harem, was made the King of Huaiyang, Liu Buyi was the King of Hengshan, Liu Hong was the Marquis of Xiangcheng, Liu Chao was the Marquis of Zhi, and Liu Wu was the Marquis of Huguan.
In autumn, peaches and plums bloom.
In the spring of the second year of Empress Lu's reign, she issued an edict saying: Emperor Gao unified the world and granted fiefs and feudal lordships to all the meritorious officials. All the people were safe and sound, and no one was immune from his kindness.
I consider that in the future, due to the passage of time, no one will remember his fame and honor, and will not be able to respect his great righteousness and pass it on to future generations.
Now I want to rank the status of each prince in the court according to the size of his merits. I will hide the merit book in the ancestral temple of Emperor Gao and pass it down from generation to generation. His descendants will inherit their merits and titles hereditary. Please work with the princes to submit a proposal.
Prime Minister Chen Ping reported: I have discussed with Jianghou Zhoubo, Quzhouhou Lishang, Yinhou Guanying, Anguohou Wang Ling, etc., that the princes were favored and were rewarded with rents and food. Your Majesty also extended extra favors and assigned grades based on merit. Please keep the merit book in the high temple.
Empress Lu approved this memorial.
On the 27th of the first lunar month of spring, an earthquake occurred and a landslide occurred in Wudu Road.
On June 30th in summer, there was a partial solar eclipse.
In the seventh month of autumn, Liu Buyi, the king of Hengshan, passed away and eight baht coins were used again.
In the summer of the third year of Empress Lu's reign, the Yangtze River and the Han River overflowed, flooding more than 4,000 houses.
In autumn, stars appear during the day.
In the summer of the fourth year of Empress Lu's reign, the young emperor secretly knew that he was not the empress's biological child, so he complained. The Empress Dowager put him under house arrest in a long alley.

summary:
summary line 0: Empress Gao, of the Lu family, was the mother of Emperor Hui and assisted Emperor Gaozu in pacifying the realm. After Emperor Hui ascended, she became the Queen Mother. She arranged for her granddaughter, the daughter of Princess Lu Yuan, to be the Queen, who had no children, so a son from a harem beauty was made prince. Upon Emperor Hui's death, this prince became emperor, but due to his youth, Empress Dowager Lu ruled in his stead, granting amnesty and promoting her relatives: four of her brother's sons were made kings, and six others were made princes.

时齐悼惠王子朱虚侯章在京师，以禄女为妇，知其谋，乃使人告兄齐王，令发兵西。
章欲与太尉勃、丞相平为内应，以诛诸吕。

事已布告诸侯王，诸侯王以为宜。
今太后崩，帝少，足下不急之国守籓，乃为上将将兵留此，为大臣诸侯所疑。
何不速归将军印，以兵属太尉，请梁王亦归相国印，与大臣盟而之国？
齐兵必罢，大臣得安，足下高枕而王千里，此万世之利也。
禄然其计，使人报产及诸吕老人。
或以为不便，计犹豫未有所决。
禄信寄，与俱出游，过其姑吕嬃。
嬃怒曰： 汝为将而弃军，吕氏今无处矣！
乃悉出珠玉、宝器散堂下，曰： 无为它人守也！
八月庚申，平阳侯窋行御史大夫事，见相国产计事。
郎中令贾寿使从齐来，因数产曰： 王不早之国，今虽欲行，尚可得邪？
具以灌婴与齐、楚合从状告产。
平阳侯窋闻其语，驰告丞相平、太尉勃。
勃欲入北军，不得入。襄平侯纪通尚符节，乃令持节矫内勃北军。
勃复令郦寄、典客刘揭说禄，曰： 帝使太尉守北军，欲令足下之国，急归将印，辞去。不然，祸且起。
禄遂解印属典客，而以兵授太尉勃。
勃入军门，行令军中曰： 为吕氏右袒，为刘氏左袒。
军皆左袒。
