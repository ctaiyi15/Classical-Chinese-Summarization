The emperor Yi Liang died cruelly, and the funeral and burial rituals were simple and lacking, so he was buried in Chengguang Palace. She was posthumously named Queen Gonghuai, and the funeral system was supplemented.
He is the great-grandson of Emperor Yin, the elder brother of Queen Guanglie, who holds the Yin consciousness of Jin Wu.
Queen Yin was very smart when she was young, and was good at calligraphy.
In the fourth year of Emperor Yongyuan's reign, he was elected to Yeting. Because she was the great-niece of Queen Guanglie, she became a noble person.
There is a special favor.
In the eighth year of Yongyuan, she was established as queen.
Since He Xi and Deng Hou entered the palace, Queen Yin's love gradually declined, and she often had feelings of hatred.
Queen Yin's grandmother Deng Zhu went in and out of the palace.
In the summer of the 14th year of Yongyuan, some people said that Queen Yin and Deng Zhu were using witchcraft to deceive people's hearts. When the matter was discovered, Emperor He asked Zhang Shen, the Chang's Minister, and Chen Bao, the Minister, to question and examine them in Yeting Prison.
As a result, the confessions of Deng Zhu, his two sons Deng Feng and Deng Yi, and Yin Queen's younger brothers Yin Yi, Yin Fu, and Yin Chang were connected with each other, and they believed that the sacrifices were curses and treasonous.
Deng Feng, Deng Yi, and Yin Fu were tortured to death in prison.
Emperor He sent Situ Lugong to hold the policy letter given to Queen Yin, handed over the Queen's seal and ribbon, and moved to Tong Palace. He died of sorrow and anger.
He was established for seven years and was buried in Linping Pavilion.
Queen Yin's father committed suicide in Yin Gang. Yin Yi, Yin Chang and Deng Zhu's family members were exiled to Bijing County, Rinan. All the clan members, including Kun and his brothers, were dismissed from official positions and returned to their hometowns.
In the fourth year of Emperor An's Yongchu reign, Empress Dowager Deng issued an edict to return all the exiled people of the Sheyin family to their home county, and to return more than five million yuan in property.
Deng Sui, Queen of Hexi and Deng, is the granddaughter of Taifu Deng Yu.
His father, Deng Xun, was a military officer protecting the Qiang people; his mother, Yin, was the daughter of Empress Guanglie's cousin.
When Deng Sui was five years old, his wife loved her very much and cut her hair herself.
The lady was old and had poor eyesight, and accidentally injured Deng Sui's forehead. Deng Sui endured the pain and remained silent.
What he saw felt strange and he asked Deng Sui. Deng Sui said: "It's not painful. My wife cut off my hair because she loved me and couldn't bear to hurt the old man's feelings, so I endured it." Deng Sui could read "Historical Records" at the age of six, and mastered "The Book of Songs" and "The Analects of Confucius" at the age of twelve.
Whenever her brothers read the Bible, they often ask questions.
Her interest is in studying poetry and classics, not in household matters.
Her mother often criticizes her, saying: You don't learn how to work as a female worker for clothing, but you are devoted to studying. Do you want to become a doctor? Deng Sui listened to her mother's advice, trained as a female worker during the day, and recited classics at night. Her family called her "Zhu Sheng".
Her father, Deng Xun, thought she was different and would often discuss matters with her in detail, no matter how big or small.
In the fourth year of Emperor Yongyuan's reign, Deng Suiben was elected to the palace. It happened that Deng Xun passed away. Deng Sui cried day and night. She did not eat meat for three years, and her face was haggard. Even her family members no longer recognized her.
Deng Sui once dreamed that she reached out to touch the sky and saw something vast and green, like stalactites. She raised her head to suck and swallow.
People who asked about dream interpretations said that Tang Yao dreamed of climbing up to the sky, and Shang Tang dreamed of licking the sky. These are all omens before the sage king succeeds, and they are very auspicious.
Another fortune teller saw Deng Sui and said in surprise: Her bones are the same as Shang Tang's, very strange and expensive. The family members were secretly happy but did not dare to speak out.
Deng Sui's uncle Deng Ye said: It is usually said that if a thousand people survive, his descendants must be knighted.
My brother Deng Xun served as a pilgrim and commissioned the construction of the Shijiu River. Thousands of people survived every year.
God's law is trustworthy, and your family will definitely receive blessings. In the past, Taifu Deng Yu once said with emotion: I command millions of people and have never killed anyone indiscriminately. Some of my descendants will be prosperous.
In the seventh year of Emperor Yongyuan's reign, Deng Sui was selected into the palace together with the women of various families.
She was seven feet two inches tall, very beautiful and outstanding, and everyone was surprised.
In the winter of the eighth year of Yongyuan, he entered Yeting as a nobleman at the age of sixteen.
summary:
Queen Yin, great-niece of Queen Guanglie, was deposed for witchcraft and died in sorrow. Queen Hexi Deng, granddaughter of Tai Fu Deng Yu, was known for her intelligence and virtue. She entered the palace at sixteen, serving Queen Yin humbly. After Yin's deposition, she became queen, banning luxury and refusing to ennoble her family. As empress dowager, she ruled during crises, reducing expenses, pardoning exiles, and promoting education. She died at forty-one, buried with Emperor He. Historians criticize her for holding power too long, leading to resentment, but acknowledge her efforts to stabilize the state.
