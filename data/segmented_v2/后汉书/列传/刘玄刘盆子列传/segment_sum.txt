In July, Wang Kuang and others attacked Suixian but failed to capture it.
Chen Mu and Liao Zhan from Pinglin gathered more than a thousand people, known as "Pinglin soldiers", to respond to Wang Kuang's troops.
Liu Xuan defected to Chen Mu and others, and served as an Jipu in the army.
At the same time, Emperor Guangwu and his brother Liu Boxheng also raised troops in Chongling and joined forces with various insurrectionary forces to attack.
In the first month of the fourth year of Emperor Di Huang's reign, he defeated the troops commanded by Wang Mang's former officer Zhen Fu and Zhengliang Qiuci, and killed them. Liu Xuan was promoted to General Gengshi.
Although there were many troops in the uprising, there was no unified leadership, so the generals discussed together to make Liu Xuan the emperor.
On the Xinsi day of February, a mandala was set up on the beach of Feishui to gather troops and horses.
Liu Xuan ascended the throne of God and stood facing south to meet the officials.
Liu Xuan has always been a coward, and he was so ashamed that he was sweating profusely. He raised his hands but couldn't even say a word.
After that, a general amnesty was given to the whole country, and Jianyuan was the first year of Gengshi.
All the generals were granted official titles, including his uncle Liu Liang as the third elder of the country, Wang Kuang as the Grand Duke of Dingguo, Wang Feng as the Grand Duke of Chengguo, Zhu Kun as Grand Sima, Liu Bo was promoted to Grand Situ, Chen Mu was promoted to Grand Sikong, and the rest were granted the title of Jiuqing or General.
