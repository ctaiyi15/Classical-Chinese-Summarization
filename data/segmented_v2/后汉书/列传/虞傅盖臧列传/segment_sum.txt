summary:
summary line 0: Yu Xu, from Wuping in Chen, was known for his integrity. His grandfather, a prison officer, handled cases with compassion. Yu Xu was orphaned young and cared for his grandmother, refusing an official post until after her death. He later served as a doctor. When General Deng Zhi proposed abandoning Liangzhou due to Qiang rebellions, Yu Xu argued against it, stating it would expose the imperial tombs and that Liangzhou's people were a vital defense. He suggested controlling local leaders by giving their sons official posts to prevent rebellion. Deng Zhi, resentful of Yu Xu's opposition, sent him to govern Chaoge, a bandit-ridden area. Yu Xu used deception, recruiting former bandits to ambush others and marking thieves' clothes with silk threads, leading to their dispersal. Promoted to Wudu's prefect, he again used deception against the Qiang, increasing his campfires to feign strength and marching rapidly. He then used a strategy of weak arrows to lure the Qiang into range of his strong crossbows, defeating them. He improved the county by building fortifications, opening waterways for transport, and increasing the population. As Sili Xiaowei, he impeached powerful officials, earning their hatred. He was framed and sentenced to hard labor, but eunuch Sun Cheng defended him, leading to his release and promotion. He later abolished the practice of "righteous money" and argued for fair treatment of petitioners. He remained upright despite nine reprimands and three punishments, dying as Shangshu Ling. On his deathbed, he regretted killing many bandits at Chaoge, believing it brought divine punishment.

洪答曰：隔阔相思，发于寤寐。
相去步武，而趋舍异规，其为怆恨，胡可胜言！
translation: 
Now that the county is still intact and the people are prosperous, if the war drums sound, we can gather 20,000 people and lead them to kill the national traitors and hold high the flag of righteousness for the world. Wouldn't it be great?
Zhang Chao agreed with his opinion and went to Chenliu with Zang Hongxi to discuss with his brother Zhang Miao.
Zhang Miao first said to Zhang Chao: I heard that you are a prefect and leave government affairs to Zang Hong. What kind of person is Zang Hong?
Zhang Chao said: Zang Hong is a strange man in the country. He is more talented and resourceful than me.
Zhang Miao immediately talked to Zang Hong and greatly appreciated him.
He sent Zang Hong to see Liu Dai, the governor of Yanzhou, and Kong Danyou, the governor of Yuzhou, and they both became close friends with each other.
Zhang Miao originally had an agreement. When Zhang Chao arrived, he made the decision, so he held a meeting with the herdsmen in Suanzao.
They set up a altar and prepared to make an alliance, but they gave in to each other. No one dared to go first, and everyone recommended Zang Hong.
Zang Hong then raised his clothes to the altar, expressed his blood and made an alliance, saying: The Han Dynasty was unlucky, and the rules and regulations of the court were in chaos. The traitor Dong Zhuo took the opportunity to cause harm, and the disaster fell on the emperor and poisoned the people.
The most terrifying thing is that the Han Dynasty fell and the country was ruined.
Liu Dai, the governor of Yanzhou, Kong Qianyou, the governor of Yuzhou, Zhang Miao, the governor of Chenliu, Qiao Mao, the governor of Dongjun, Zhang Chao, the governor of Guangling, etc., gathered volunteers to fight against the national calamity.
All those who are in our alliance will work together to fulfill the loyalty of our ministers and kill their heads as they please without any second thought.
If he violates the oath, he will die and die without any descendants.
The emperor, the queen, the earth, the ancestors and the gods all learn from each other.
Zang Hong's poems were so passionate that everyone who listened to them got excited.
From then on, each army hesitated, and no one dared to go first, resulting in insufficient military supplies and the disintegration of the soldiers.
At this time, Gongsun Zan, the commander-in-chief of the imperial army, had a disagreement with Liu Yu, the commander-in-chief. Zhang Chao sent Zang Hong to find Liu Yu to discuss the plan to defeat the bandits. When they walked to the river, they encountered a war between You and Ji, and the road was blocked, so they went to Yuan Shao's place.
Yuan Shao met Zang Hong and admired his talent. He became friends with Zang Hong and asked Zang Hong to act as the governor of Qingzhou.
The former governor, Jiao He, had a false reputation and was a master of empty words.
At this time, yellow scarf thieves were scurrying everywhere, and the people in Qingzhou were rich and had many troops and equipment.
Jiao He wanted to go to the capital with various allies, but before he left, the Yellow Turban thieves had already arrived, conquered the city and massacred the people.
Jiao He didn't know anything about military affairs, so he just invited the witch Zhu Shi to his house to pray for God's blessing.
He was also afraid that yellow turban thieves would cross the river during the freezing period, so he ordered many ice-trap pills to be made and placed in the river.
His tribe was disbanded and Jiao He also died of illness.
Zang Hong gathered together to comfort the scattered tribes, and the people were at peace.
In the second year of his reign, Yuan Shao was afraid of his incompetence and appointed him as the prefect of Dongjun, with his capital in Dongwuyang.
At this time, Cao Cao surrounded Zhang Chao in Yongqiu, and the situation was very dangerous.
Zhang Chao said to the soldiers: Zang Hong is the only one who will come to save me today.
Some people say: Yuan Shao and Cao Cao are getting along well, and Zang Hong is Yuan Shao's man. I'm afraid Zang Hong will not come far away, and he will bring disaster instead of good fortune.
Zhang Chao said: Ziyuan is a righteous man in the world and will never deviate from his original aspirations. He may be held hostage by force, but there is nothing we can do!
Zang Hong began to hear that Zhang Chao was surrounded, cried barefoot, and prepared his troops to rescue Zhang Chao.
Thinking that his troops were too weak, he asked Yuan Shao for troops, but Yuan Shao refused. Zhang Chao's defense was breached and Zhang Chao's entire clan was killed.
Zang Hong resented Yuan Shao and severed ties with him.
Yuan Shao sent troops to surround Zang Hong, but could not capture it after many years. Yuan Shao sent Zang Hong to write a letter to Zang Hong from Chen Lin, a county resident, explaining the blessings and misfortunes to Zang Hong, and blaming each other with personal kindness and national justice.
Zang Hong replied: Missing each other after farewell often occurs when we are asleep.
We are not far apart, but each of us has taken a different path, and each of us has a different mood. How can we describe it in just one or two sentences?
