耿弇字伯昭，扶风茂陵人也。
其先武帝时以吏二千石自巨鹿徙焉。
父况，字侠游，以明经为郎，与王莽从弟伋共学《老子》于安丘先生，后为朔调连率。
弇少好学，习父业。常见郡尉试骑士，建旗鼓，肄驰射，由是好将帅之事。
及王莽败，更始立，诸将略地者，前后多擅威权，辄改易守、令。
况自以莽之所置，怀不自安。
时，弇年二十一，乃辞况奉奏诣更始，因赍贡献，以求自固之宜。
及至宋子，会王郎诈称成帝子子舆，起兵邯郸，弇从吏孙仓、卫包于道共谋曰： 刘子舆成帝正统，舍此不归，远行安之？ 弇按剑曰： 子舆弊贼，卒为降虏耳。
我至长安，与国家陈渔阳、上谷兵马之用，还出太原、代郡，反复数十日，归发突骑以辚乌合之众，如摧枯折腐耳。
观公等不识去就，族灭不久也！ 仓、包不从，遂亡降王郎。弇道闻光武在卢奴，乃驰北上谒，光武留署门下吏。弇因说护军朱祐，求归发兵，以定邯郸。
光武笑曰： 小儿曹乃有大意哉！ 因数召见加恩慰。
弇因从光武北至蓟。
闻邯郸兵方到，光武将欲南归，召官属计议。
弇曰： 今兵从南来，不可南行。
渔阳太守彭宠，公之邑人；上谷太守，即弇父也。
发此两郡，控弦万骑，邯郸不足虑也。 光武官属腹心皆不肯，曰： 死尚南首，奈何北行入囊中？ 光武指弇曰： 是我北道主人也。 会蓟中乱，光武遂南驰，官属各分散。
弇走昌平就况，因说况使寇恂东约彭宠，各发突骑二千匹，步兵千人。
弇与景丹、寇恂及渔阳兵合军而南，所过击斩王郎大将、九卿、校尉以下四百余级，得印绶百二十五，节二，斩首三万级，定涿郡、中山、巨鹿、清河、河间凡二十二县，遂及光武于广阿。
是时，光武方攻王郎，传言二郡兵为邯郸来，众皆恐。
既而悉诣营上谒。
光武见弇等，说，曰： 当与渔阳、上谷士大夫共此大功。 乃皆以为偏将军，使还领其兵。
加况大将军、兴义侯，得自置偏裨。
弇等遂从拔邯郸。
时，更始征代郡太守赵永，而况劝永不应召，令诣于光武。
光武遣永复郡。
永北还，而代令张晔据城反畔，乃招迎匈奴、乌桓以为援助。
光武以弇弟舒为复胡将军，使击晔，破之。
永乃得复郡。
时，五校贼二十余万北寇上谷，况与舒连击破之，贼皆退走。
更始见光武威声日盛，君臣疑虑，乃遣使立光武为萧王，令罢兵与诸将有功者还长安；遣苗曾为幽州牧，韦顺为上谷太守，蔡充为渔阳太守，并北之部。
时，光武居邯郸宫，昼卧温明殿。
弇入造床下请间，因说曰： 今更始失政，君臣淫乱，诸将擅命于畿内，贵戚纵横于都内。
translation: 
Geng Yan, courtesy name Bozhao, was from Maoling County, Fufeng County.
During the reign of Emperor Wu, his ancestors migrated from Julu to Fufeng with two thousand stones.
His father, Geng Kuang, was named Xiayou. He was a doctor who was proficient in Confucian classics. He and Wang Mang studied Laozi from his younger brother Wang Ji from Mr. Anqiu, who later became Shuo Tiaolian.
Geng Yan was eager to learn when he was young and learned from his father's studies. Because he often saw county captains taking exams for knights, setting up flags and drums, and practicing horse racing and archery, Geng Yan became interested in generals.
When Wang Mang was defeated and Geng Shi was established, the generals plundered the land and often used their own authority to replace the governors and magistrates at every turn.
Geng Kuang thought he was appointed by Wang Mang and felt uneasy.
Geng Yan was twenty-one years old at this time, so he resigned from his father's post and went to reform, so he brought tributes with him in order to consolidate himself.
When we arrived in the Song Dynasty, it happened that Wang Lang pretended to be Emperor Cheng's son Ziyu and raised troops in Handan. Geng Yan's subordinates Sun Cang and Wei Bao conspired together on the road and said: Liu Ziyu is the orthodox Emperor Cheng. If he doesn't surrender to him, where can he go? Geng Yan raised his sword and scolded: "Ziyu is a deceitful thief, and he will eventually become a prisoner."
I went to Chang'an to explain to the country the usefulness of the soldiers and horses of Yuyang and Shanggu. I also went out to Taiyuan and Daijun for dozens of days. When I came back, I suddenly attacked the mob with cavalry. It was as easy as destroying the dead. I see that if you don't know how to leave, the disaster of family execution is just around the corner.
Sun Cang and Wei Bao refused, so they went to surrender to Wang Lang. Geng Yan heard on the road that Guangwu was in Lunu, so he went north to pay a visit to Jin. Guangwu kept him as a subordinate. Geng Yan therefore persuaded Zhu, the army guard, to ask him to go back and send troops to pacify Handan.
Guangwu smiled and said: You are so ambitious at such a young age! Therefore, he was summoned several times to give him comfort.
Geng Yan then followed Guangwu as far north as Ji.
I heard that the Handan soldiers had just arrived, and General Guangwu was about to return south, so he summoned his officials to discuss it.
Geng Yan said: Now the soldiers are coming from the south, so we must not go south.
Peng Chong, the governor of Yuyang, is a fellow villager of Gong; the governor of Shanggu is my father.
If the men and horses from these two counties are mobilized and there are tens of thousands of horses, Handan will be easy to capture. Even the military attache and his confidants refused, saying: "If you want to die, you have to head south. How can you go north and enter the bag?" Guangwu pointed at Geng Yan and said: He is my master in the North Road. It happened that a commotion broke out in Ji at this time, Guangwu rushed to the south, and the officials and subordinates dispersed.
Geng Yan arrived in Changping and found his father Kuang, so he persuaded Kuang to let Kou Xun make an appointment with Peng Chong, each with 2,000 fast cavalry and 1,000 infantry.
Geng Yan joined forces with Jing Dan, Kou Xun and Yu Yang and headed south. Along the way, they killed more than 400 people below General Wang Lang, Jiu Qing, and Xiao Wei. They obtained 125 seals and ribbons, two talismans, and beheaded 30,000 people. They defeated a total of 22 counties in Zhuojun, Zhongshan, Julu, Qinghe, and Hejian. Then they went to Guang'a to catch up with Guangwu.
At that time, Guangwu was attacking Wang Lang. Legend has it that the soldiers and horses from the two counties came from Handan. They all thought they were Wang Lang's reinforcements and were frightened.
When the troops arrived, they all went to the barracks to pay homage to Guangwu.
When Guangwu saw Geng Yan and others, he was very happy and said, "I should share this great achievement with the scholar-bureaucrats in Yuyang and Shanggu." So they were all promoted to generals and sent back to lead their troops.
Geng Kuang was granted the title of General and Marquis of Xingyi, and he placed all the generals and subordinates on his own.
Geng Yan and others took advantage of the situation to attack Handan.
At this time, Gengshi summoned Zhao Yong, the prefect of Daijun, but Geng Kuang advised Zhao Yong not to answer the call and let him go to see Guangwu.
Guangwu sent Zhao Yong back to his dynasty and still served as the prefect.
When Zhao Yongbei returned, Zhang Ye, the magistrate of the county, rebelled in the city and recruited the Xiongnu and Wuhuan for aid.
Guangwu appointed Geng Yan's younger brother Geng Shu as General Hu, and ordered him to attack Zhang Ye and break through.
Zhao Yong had to resume his duties as county governor.
At this time, more than 200,000 thieves from the five schools invaded Shanggu from the north. Geng Kuang and Geng Shu defeated the thieves in succession, and they all retreated.
Gengshi saw that Guangwu's reputation was growing, and the emperor and his ministers had doubts, so he sent envoys to make Guangwu the king of Xiao, and ordered him to stop his troops and return to Chang'an with the meritorious generals. He also sent Miao Zeng to be the shepherd of Youzhou, Wei Shun to be the governor of Shanggu, and Cai Chong to be the governor of Yuyang, and annex the north.
At this time, Guangwu lived in Handan Palace and slept in Wenming Palace during the day.
Geng Yan entered the bedroom and went to the bed to take advantage of Guangwu's free time, so he said: Today is the beginning of political chaos, the monarchs and ministers are promiscuous, the generals are arrogant and domineering in the capital, and the princes and nobles are tyrannical in the capital.
