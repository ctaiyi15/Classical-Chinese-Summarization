Pi Tong later led troops to defeat bandits and often fought alongside Shizu. When Wang Lang captured Xindu, Pi Tong's family was imprisoned, but he refused to surrender, saying he must not care for his family. His family was spared when Gengshi's generals captured Xindu. Pi Tong was made a marquis and held various official positions, including Taichang and Shaofu. He died, and his title passed to his descendants, though the fief was later abolished and re-granted.
