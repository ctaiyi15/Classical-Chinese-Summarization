◆The biography of Chen Fan, whose courtesy name is Zhongju, was born in Pingyu, Runan.
My grandfather served as the governor of Hedong.
When Chen Fan was fifteen years old, he lived in a room with nothing to do, and it was very dirty inside and outside. His father's friend Xue Qin, who was from the same county, came to see him and said to Chen Fan: "Child, why don't you clean up to welcome the guests?"
Chen Fan said: When a real man is alive, he should sweep away the rubbish in the world. How can he only care about his own room?
Xue Qin knew that he had the ambition to clarify the world and admired him very much.
At first, he served as an official in the county and was elected as Xiaolian and awarded the title of doctor.
Because his mother passed away, he resigned from office and lived in mourning.
When the mourning period expired, the governor Zhou Jing called him to serve as a chariot, but because he had different opinions on his advice, he abandoned the talisman and left.
Later, the government recruited Fang Zheng, but he didn't go.
Taiwei Li Gu recommended him and promoted him to Yilang, and then he was promoted to the prefect of Le'an.
At this time, Li Ying was appointed as the governor of Qingzhou, and he was famous for his strict governance. After hearing the news, everyone in the subordinate city asked to leave. Chen Fan stayed alone because of his clean and honest performance.
Zhou Chu, a county man, kept himself clean and cared for himself. He was invited by the county guards before and after him, but he refused to go.
Only Chen Fan can recruit him. Chen Fan called him by his name instead of his name and respected him very much.
A bed was specially built for him. After Zhou Chu left, the bed was hung up.
The common man Zhao Xuan buried his relatives without closing the tomb passage, and lived in it for more than 20 years. The towns and cities praised his filial piety, and the states and counties treated him with courtesy several times. The county recommended him to Chen Fan and asked about his wife and children. They found out that Zhao Xuan's five children were all born during his mourning period.
Chen Fan then said angrily: "Sages make rituals, and moral people must abide by them, and unworthy people should also strive to do so."
And the number of sacrifices does not need to be too many, as too much would be disrespectful.
You are sleeping in the tomb, raising your children in the tomb, deceiving the world, stealing your reputation, confusing the people, and insulting the ghosts and gods. How is this unreasonable!
So his crime was punished.
General Liang Ji was a powerful man in the world. At that time, he sent a letter to Chen Fan, asking Chen Fan to handle private matters. The messenger was not allowed to see him, so he lied and asked to see Chen Fan. Chen Fan got angry and beat the messenger to death, and was sentenced to military service for his crime.
After a slight promotion, he was appointed as minister.
At this time, mountain bandits in Lingling and Guiyang were in harm's way, and the minister considered sending troops to fight. The emperor also ordered the prefectures and counties to select filial piety, integrity, and maocai as appropriate.
Chen Fan went to the Supreme Court to argue and refute, saying: In the past, when Emperor Gaozu established a great cause, the people in the country were relieved, and the officials raised the people as if they were their own sons.
Now the common people in the two counties are all the sons of the emperor. Isn't the cause of the harm to the sons caused by the corruption and cruelty of the local officials?
