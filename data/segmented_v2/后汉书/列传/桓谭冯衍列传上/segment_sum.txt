◆The Legend of Huan Tan
Huan Tan, whose courtesy name is Junshan, was born in Xiangxian County, Peiguo County.
His father was Tai Leling when he became emperor.
Huan Tan was appointed as a minister due to his father's relationship, so he loved music and was good at drums and harp.
He is very knowledgeable, has read all the Five Classics, and can explain the great meanings of ancient books. Distinguish sentences without leaving chapters.
He wrote good articles, and especially loved ancient learning. He repeatedly analyzed difficult and different meanings through debates between Liu Xin and Yang Xiong. He also loved miscellaneous dramas. He was a simple man and did not put on official airs. He liked to slander secular Confucian scholars, so he was often ostracized and attacked.
During the reigns of Emperor Ai and Emperor Ping, his status was only that of a man.
Queen Fu's father, Fu Yan, the Marquis of Kongxiang, thought highly of Huan Tan.
At that time, Gao'an Hou Dong Xian was favored by the emperor, and his sister was named Zhaoyi. The emperor became increasingly alienated from Empress Fu, and Fu Yan was silently dissatisfied.
Huan Tan said to Fu Yan: In the past, Emperor Wu wanted to appoint Wei Zifu, and secretly collected Empress Chen's faults. Empress Chen was finally deposed, and Wei Zifu was actually appointed.
Now Dong Xian is favored and his sister is especially favored. There may be another change of "Wei Zihu". Don't you worry?
Fu Yan was alarmed and said: Yes, what should we do?
Huan Tan said: Punishment cannot be imposed on innocent people, and evil spirits cannot defeat righteous people in vain.
A scholar serves his master with his intelligence, and a woman seeks his master with a charming way.
The queen is young and has not experienced many hardships. There are people who are driving witch doctors inside, seeking magic skills outside, and practicing evil ways. We must be on guard against these.
Moreover, because the king is respected as the father of the queen, he communicates with the guests. The guests will definitely use this to enhance their power, which will attract people's ridicule.
Why don't you thank the guests and send the disciples away? You must be humble and cautious. This is the way to cultivate yourself and your family to avoid disaster.
Fu Yan said: OK.
So he dismissed the guests and went to the palace to tell the queen, just as Huan Tan said.
Later, Dong Xian indeed hinted to the imperial physician Ling Zhenqin to collect the Fu family's crimes and arrest Fu Xi, Empress Fu's younger brother. During the interrogation, no evidence of guilt was found, and the case was eliminated, so the Fu family was finally saved during the reign of Emperor Ai.
Later, Dong Xian became the Grand Sima. Hearing about Huan Tan's reputation, he wanted to make friends with him.
Huan Tan first wrote to Dong Xian, saying that Dong Xian could not use the method of assisting the country and protecting himself, so Huan Tan would not have any contact with him.
When Wang Mang was regent and usurped Emperor Ping, the intellectuals at that time all praised Wang Mang. Or they described auspicious signs to create public opinion to please Wang Mang, but Huan Tan kept his innocence and remained silent.
During the reign of Wang Mang, he was the official in charge of music. After Gengshili, he became the official of Taizhong.
When Shizu ascended the throne, he was summoned to wait for the imperial edict. Huan Tan wrote a letter and said something that was inconsistent with Shizu's will, so he did not adopt it.
Later, Song Hong, the Grand Sikong, recommended Huan Tan and paid homage to Yilang, so he wrote a letter stating the matters that should be paid attention to in political affairs at that time, saying: I heard that the failure of a country depends on political affairs; and the gains and losses of political affairs depend on whether the assistants are recruited.

I, Huan Tan, have observed your Majesty's use of troops, and found that for those who surrendered, there were no heavy rewards to induce kindness, and some even plundered their property. Therefore, the generals, large and small, were suspicious and colluded with each other, and could not solve the problem after many years.
The ancients said: Everyone in the world knows that taking is taking, but they don’t know that giving is also taking.
If Your Majesty can be more considerate than the official title but more important than the reward, and share it with the soldiers, then who will be unable to recruit, and who will not be able to explain it? Where does it point and where does it not open? Conquer the enemy, which enemy cannot be conquered!
In this way, narrowness can be used as broadness, delay can be used as speed, death can be reborn, and loss can be gained again.
The emperor was even more unhappy after reading the memorial.
Later, there was an edict to convene ministers to discuss the location of the spiritual platform. The emperor said to Huan Tan: I want to use prophecies to decide, how about it?
Huan Tan was silent for a long time and said: I don't read prophecies.
The emperor asked the reason, and Huan Tan once again argued that the prophecy was grotesque and unreasonable.
The emperor was furious and said: "Huan Tan is not a saint, so he should be taken down and beheaded."
Huan Tan kowtowed and the bleeding lasted for a long time.
Transferred to Liu'an County Cheng. Huan Tan was in a bad mood and died of illness on the road. He was over seventy years old.
At first, Huan Tan wrote twenty-nine articles on how to deal with the current situation, named it "New Treatise", and presented it to the imperial court, which was praised by his ancestors.
One chapter of "Qin Dao" was not completed, so Suzong ordered Ban Gu to continue completing it.
He wrote a total of twenty-six poems, admonitions, books and memorials.
During the Yuan Dynasty, Suzong went on a hunting tour to the east. When he arrived at Pei, he sent envoys to pay homage to Huan Tan's tomb, which made the village proud.
◆Feng Yan's biography: Feng Yan, courtesy name Jingtong, was from Duling, Jingzhao.
The ancestor Feng Yewang was Dahonglu during the Yuan Dynasty.
Feng Yan was a genius when he was young. He was able to recite "Poetry" at the age of nine. By the time he was twenty, he was well versed in many books.
During Wang Mang's reign, many people recommended him to be an official, but Feng Yan declined.
At this time, the army was rising, and Wang Mang sent Gengshi General Lian Dan to attack Shandong.
Lian Dan recruited Feng Yanshou as an official and went to Dingtao with him.
Wang Mang issued an edict to Lian Dan, saying: "The food in the warehouse has been exhausted, and the money in the treasury is empty. It's time to get angry and fight."
A general has a heavy responsibility for his country, and he cannot repay his kindness without sacrificing his life in the wilderness.
Lian Dan was frightened and summoned Feng Yan at night to read him the edict.
Feng Yan then said to Lian Dan: I heard that those who follow the right path are the greatest; those who go against the right path but can succeed, this should be taken seriously when weighing.
Therefore, if you hope to achieve success, don't ask about the reasons; if the principles are consistent with the general principles, don't stick to the details.
In the past, Feng Chou's father asked the Marquis of Qi to pretend to be a charioteer and go to Huaquan to drink, thus saving the Marquis of Qi from disaster, and was praised by the princes. Zheng Zhong's Ji Zhong succumbed to the intimidation of the Song Dynasty and made the prince Hu finally return to his throne, which was praised in the "Spring and Autumn Annals".

I, Huan Tan, have observed your Majesty's use of troops, and found that for those who surrendered, there were no heavy rewards to induce kindness, and some even plundered their property. Where does it point and where does it not open? Conquer the enemy, which enemy cannot be conquered!
In this way, narrowness can be used as broadness, delay can be used as speed, death can be reborn, and loss can be gained again.
The emperor was even more unhappy after reading the memorial.
Later, there was an edict to convene ministers to discuss the location of the spiritual platform. The emperor said to Huan Tan: I want to use prophecies to decide, how about it?
Huan Tan was silent for a long time and said: I don't read prophecies.
The emperor asked the reason, and Huan Tan once again argued that the prophecy was grotesque and unreasonable.
The emperor was furious and said: "Huan Tan is not a saint, so he should be taken down and beheaded."
Huan Tan kowtowed and the bleeding lasted for a long time.
Transferred to Liu'an County Cheng. Huan Tan was in a bad mood and died of illness on the road. He was over seventy years old.
At first, Huan Tan wrote twenty-nine articles on how to deal with the current situation, named it "New Treatise", and presented it to the imperial court, which was praised by his ancestors.
One chapter of "Qin Dao" was not completed, so Suzong ordered Ban Gu to continue completing it.
He wrote a total of twenty-six poems, admonitions, books and memorials.
During the Yuan Dynasty, Suzong went on a hunting tour to the east. When he arrived at Pei, he sent envoys to pay homage to Huan Tan's tomb, which made the village proud.
◆Feng Yan's biography: Feng Yan, courtesy name Jingtong, was from Duling, Jingzhao.
The ancestor Feng Yewang was Dahonglu during the Yuan Dynasty.
Feng Yan was a genius when he was young. He was able to recite "Poetry" at the age of nine. By the time he was twenty, he was well versed in many books.
During Wang Mang's reign, many people recommended him to be an official, but Feng Yan declined.
At this time, the army was rising, and Wang Mang sent Gengshi General Lian Dan to attack Shandong.
Lian Dan recruited Feng Yanshou as an official and went to Dingtao with him.
Wang Mang issued an edict to Lian Dan, saying: "The food in the warehouse has been exhausted, and the money in the treasury is empty. It's time to get angry and fight."
A general has a heavy responsibility for his country, and he cannot repay his kindness without sacrificing his life in the wilderness.
Lian Dan was frightened and summoned Feng Yan at night to read him the edict.
Feng Yan then said to Lian Dan: I heard that those who follow the right path are the greatest; those who go against the right path but can succeed, this should be taken seriously when weighing.
Therefore, if you hope to achieve success, don't ask about the reasons; if the principles are consistent with the general principles, don't stick to the details.
In the past, Feng Chou's father asked the Marquis of Qi to pretend to be a charioteer and go to Huaquan to drink, thus saving the Marquis of Qi from disaster, and was praised by the princes. Zheng Zhong's Ji Zhong succumbed to the intimidation of the Song Dynasty and made the prince Hu finally return to his throne, which was praised in the "Spring and Autumn Annals".

Bao Yong sent his younger brother Bao Sheng and son-in-law Zhang Shu to lure him back to Niecheng County. Zhang Shu's family was in Shangdang, and his family was imprisoned in Tianyi.
He also wrote a letter to persuade Bao Yong to surrender, but Bao Yong never responded. From then on, Bao Yong and Tianyi had a grudge.
Tianyi, named Boyu, was born in Feng Yi, and later served as the prefect of Yuyang.
Bao Yong and Feng Yan knew for sure that Gengshi was dead, so they jointly stopped their troops and surrendered to Hanoi without any hats.

There was a solar eclipse in the sixth year of Jianwu, and Feng Yan wrote a petition asking for eight things: first, to show literary virtue, second, to praise military heroes, third, to repair old merits, fourth, to recruit heroes, fifth, to clarify likes and dislikes, sixth, to simplify laws, seventh, to improve rank and salary, and eighth, to care for borders.

Wang Hu and others were afraid, so they united to exclude Feng Yan. Therefore, Feng Yan could not be received by Guangwu.

Later, Guangwu punished his relatives and guests in Xijing, and most of them were brought to justice. The most serious ones were even put to death and relocated, while the rest were demoted.
Feng Yan was offended by this. He went to prison to plead guilty, but was pardoned by an imperial edict.
Feng Yan then returned to his hometown in the west and closed the door to protect himself. He did not dare to interact with his relatives and old friends again.
