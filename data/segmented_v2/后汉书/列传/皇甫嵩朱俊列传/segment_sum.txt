Written in white clay on temple gates in the capital and on state and county government offices, they are all marked with the character Jiazi.
In the first year of Zhongping, Dafang Ma Yuanyi and others first collected tens of thousands of people from Jing and Yang, and gathered at Ye regularly to set off.
Ma Yuanyi visited the capital many times, using Zhongchang Shifeng and Xu Feng as internal agents, and agreed to start an uprising both inside and outside the capital on March 5.
Before the rebellion broke out, Zhang Jiao's disciple Jinan Tang Zhou wrote a letter to report, so he drove Ma Yuanyi to Luoyang.
Emperor Ling used Zhou Zhang to issue orders to the three princes and officials, and ordered Zhou Bin to take the three government officials with him. He asked the palace guards and the people if they believed in Zhang Jiao, killed more than a thousand people, tracked down Jizhou, and arrested Zhang Jiao and others.
Zhang Jiao and others knew that the matter had been exposed, so they notified all parties day and night and started revolts at the same time.
They all wore yellow scarves as a symbol. At that time, they were called "yellow scarves" and also "moth thieves".
Kill people and sacrifice to heaven.
Zhang Jiao was called General Tiangong, Zhang Jiao's younger brother Zhang Bao was called General Digong, and Zhang Bao's younger brother Zhang Liang was called General Renggong.
Wherever he went, government offices were burned, villages and towns were robbed, states and counties lost their basis, and many officials fled.
Within ten days, the whole country responded and the capital was shaken.
The imperial court ordered the prefectures and counties to repair offensive and defensive positions, select and prepare ordnance, and set up captains at Hangu, Dagu, Guangcheng, Yique, Longyuan, Xuanmen, Mengjin, and Xiaopingjin passes.
Call the ministers to a meeting.
Huangfu Song believed that the ban on the party should be lifted, and he gave Zhongzang money and Xiyuan stable horses to the sergeants.
Emperor Ling adopted his opinion.

summary:
summary line 0: Huangfu Song, styled Yizhen, was a nephew of the general Huangfu Gui. His father served as the Prefect of Yanmen. From a young age, Huangfu Song was learned in the *Classic of Poetry* and *Book of Documents*, and skilled in archery, swordsmanship, and horsemanship. He was recommended as a Xiaolian and Maocai, but declined summons from Grand Commandant Chen Fan and General Dou Wu. During Emperor Ling's reign, he was appointed Yilang and later Governor of Beidi.

summary:
summary line 1: When Zhang Jiao founded the Way of Great Peace, claiming to cure illness through faith and talismanic water, he amassed hundreds of thousands of followers across eight provinces over a decade. He organized them into thirty-six "squares," each led by a general, and spread the prophecy: "The Azure Heaven is dead; the Yellow Heaven shall rise. In the year of Jiazi, the world will be blessed." In the first year of Zhongping (184 AD), his disciple Tang Zhou betrayed the planned uprising, leading to the execution of Ma Yuanyi and a crackdown in Luoyang. Zhang Jiao then launched a nationwide rebellion, with followers wearing yellow scarves—hence called the "Yellow Turbans"—burning government offices and terrorizing provinces.

◆The biography of Zhu Jun, whose courtesy name is Gongwei, was from Shangyu County, Kuaiji County.
My father died when I was young, and my mother used to sell silk fabrics.
Zhu Jun was famous for being a filial piety to his mother, and he served as a secretary in the county. He loved justice more than wealth, and the villagers admired him very much.
At that time, Zhou Gui of the same county summoned the government, and when he set out on his journey, he borrowed a million dollars from the county treasury to pay for clothing. Later, the county urgently demanded payment, and Zhou Gui's family was too poor to pay back. Zhu Jun stole his mother's silk to prepare money for Zhou Gui to solve the problem.
As a result, his mother lost her inheritance and became furious and scolded him.
Zhu Jun said: A small loss will lead to a big benefit. It is inevitable to be poor first and then rich.
Yangdu Shang, the magistrate of Shangyu County, met Zhu Jun and thought he was not an ordinary person. He introduced him to Wei Yi, the prefect, and he took up some positions in the county. The succeeding prefect Yin Duan took Zhu Jun as his chief official.
In the second year of Xiping's reign, Yin Duan was impeached by the state because he failed to attack the traitor Xu Zhao. He was guilty of abandoning the city.
Zhu Jun wore rags, walked along the narrow road, brought hundreds of gold to the capital, and bribed the officials in charge of the memorial. Because he was able to change the memorial to Dingzhou, Yin Duan was sent to Zuo School for labor reform.
Yin Duan was very happy that he was exempted from the exemption, but he didn't know the reason, and Zhu Jun never said it.
Later, the prefect Xu Wanggui promoted Zhu Jun to be filial and honest, and was promoted to the Lanling Order. His political achievements were very good, and he was commended by the Prime Minister of the East China Sea.
At this time, a group of thieves broke out in Jiaozhi tribe, but Mu Shou was too weak to stop them.
He also handed over the rebels Liang Long and more than 10,000 people, who rebelled with Kong Zhi, the prefect of Nanhai, and conquered the counties.
In the first year of Guanghe, he appointed Zhu Jun as the governor of Jiaochengzhi. He ordered Zhu Jun to recruit 5,000 soldiers in the county plus the ones he had dispatched. They marched in two directions and arrived at the state border. He stopped advancing. He first sent envoys to the county to observe the strength of the thieves and publicize the power of the court to shake their hearts. Then he joined forces with the soldiers of the seven counties to force the thieves. He killed Liang Long and tens of thousands of people surrendered. They were all pacified within a month.
Zhu Jun was granted the title of Marquis of Tingting in the capital for his meritorious service, with 1,500 households, 50 jins of gold, and conscription as an admonishment official.
When the Yellow Turban bandits arose, many ministers recommended Zhu Jun as a talented man. He was appointed as the Right Zhonglang General, holding the emperor's festival, and he and the Zuo Zhonglang General Huangfu Song attacked the Yingchuan, Runan, and Chen Kingdom thieves, and they were all put down.
Huangfu Song went up to report on the situation of pacifying the thieves, and attributed the credit to Zhu Jun. Therefore, the Jin Dynasty granted Zhu Jun the title of Marquis of Xixiang, and promoted him to the rank of Lang General in the town of thieves.
At this time, Zhang Mancheng, a yellow turban in Nanyang, raised an army. He claimed to be the envoy of the gods and had tens of thousands of people. He killed Chu Gong, the governor of the county, and stayed in Wanxia for more than a hundred days.
