明府剖符大臣，千里重任，举止进退，其可轻乎？
太守不听，遂便升车。
章前拔佩刀绝马鞅，于是乃止。
及宪被诛，公卿以下多以交关得罪，太守幸免，以此重章。
举孝廉，六迁为五官中郎将。
延平元年，为光禄勋。
永初元年，代魏霸为太常。
其冬，代尹勤为司空。
是时中常侍郑众，蔡伦等皆秉势豫政，章数进直言。
初，和帝崩，邓太后以皇子胜有痼疾，不可奉承宗庙，贪殇帝孩抱，养为己子，故立之，以胜为平原王。
及殇帝崩，群臣以胜疾非痼、意咸归之，太后以前既不立，恐后为怨，乃立和帝兄清河孝王子祐，是为安帝。
章以众心不附，遂密谋闭宫门，诛车骑将军邓骘兄弟及郑众、蔡伦，劫尚书，废太后于南宫，封帝为远国王，而立平原王胜。
事觉，策免，章自杀。
家无余财，诸子易衣而出，并日而食。
translation: 
Zheng Hongzou thought that although the position was prestigious, the reward was very meager. When the election came, most of them were unhappy and asked Shangshu Lang to supplement the Thousand Stone Order to make history longer.
The emperor followed his advice.
Things that Zheng Hong mentioned before and after that were beneficial to the king's government were all recorded in Nangong as stories.
He came out to be the king of Pingyuan and worshiped his ministers.
In the eighth year of the founding of the People's Republic of China, he replaced Zheng Zhong as the chief agricultural officer.
The goods contributed by the seven counties of old Jiaozhi were transferred across the sea from Dongye. The storms were difficult and shipwrecks often occurred.
Zheng Hongzou opened the Lingling and Guiyang mountain roads, so the mountain roads were widened and leveled to become future passages.
During Zheng Hong's two years in office, he saved 3 trillion yuan in assets.
At that time, the world was suffering from drought, there were police on the border, people had insufficient food, but there was a lot of money accumulated.
Zheng Hong also reported that the province should contribute and reduce corvee fees to benefit the hungry people.
The emperor accepted his suggestion.
In the first year of Yuanhe, Zheng Hong replaced Deng Biao as Taiwei.
At that time, Zheng Hong had elected his fifth Lun as Sikong, who was below him. Every time he went to court at Zhengshuo, Zheng Hong would bow down and feel inferior.
The emperor asked and knew the reason, so he listened to his words and put up a mica screen to separate Zheng Hong from the Fifth Lun. This became a system.
In the fourth year of his reign, he reported to Shangshu Zhang Lin that he flattered and supported Dou Xian, his attendant, but had always harbored filth. He also reported to Luoyang Ling Yang Guang that he was Dou Xian's guest. He was corrupt and disabled in office and should not stay in his original position.
The official who was in charge of the memorial was an old friend of Yang Guang, so he informed Yang Guang.
Yang Guang reported to Dou Xian, so Dou Xian reported that Zheng Hong had leaked secrets as a minister.
The emperor ordered Zheng Hong to take back his seal and ribbon.
Zheng Hong came to Tingwei to plead guilty and ordered him to leave. Because he asked to resign, he was not allowed to do so.
When he was seriously ill, he wrote a letter to express his gratitude and pointed out Dou Xian's shortcomings.
The emperor read the memorial and sent a doctor to see Zheng Hong's illness. By the time the doctor arrived, Zheng Hong had already died.
Before his death, he returned all the things the emperor had given him and ordered his wife and children to be buried in brown turbans and plain coffins before returning to his hometown.
◆Zhou Zhangzhuan, also known as Uncle Zhou Zhang, was a native of Sui County, Nanyang County.
At first, he was a meritorious official in the county.
At this time, the general Dou Xian was granted the title of champion and returned to the country.
Zhou Zhang went from the prefect to the championship, and the prefect also wanted to pay homage to Dou Xian.
Zhou Zhangjin remonstrated: "Today we are traveling for official business, how can we go beyond etiquette and make private friends with powerful people?"
Moreover, Dou Xian is a relative of the concubine and has great influence on the royal family. Now that he has retired to the vassal state, it is difficult to measure the blessings and misfortunes.
You are the Minister of Talisman Dissection, and you have a heavy responsibility of thousands of miles. How can you act rashly when you move forward or retreat?
The prefect refused to listen and wanted to raise the chariot.
Zhou Zhang went to draw his sword and cut the leather around the horse's neck, and the prefect was forced to stop.
When Dou Xian was executed, most of the officials below the public minister were offended for associating with Dou Xian, but the prefect was spared, so they valued Zhou Zhang.
He was promoted to be filial and honest, and he was promoted to the rank of Langjiang with five sense organs in the sixth year.
In the first year of Yanping, he was named Guang Luxun.
In the first year of Yongchu, he succeeded Wei Ba as Taichang.
In the winter of this year, Dai Yinqin was appointed Sikong.
At this time, the ministers Zheng Zhong and Cai Lun were using their power to intervene in the government affairs, and Zhou Zhang spoke out several times.
At first, after the death of Emperor He, Empress Dowager Deng thought that the prince Liu Sheng had a chronic illness and could not flatter the ancestral temple. Emperor Shang was a young child and was raised as her own son, so she made him the prince and made Liu Sheng the king of Pingyuan.
After the death of Emperor Shang, the ministers thought that Liu Sheng's illness was not a chronic illness, and all advocated the appointment of Liu Sheng. Since the Queen Mother had not established Liu Sheng before, fearing that the queen would resent her, she appointed Liu Sheng, the son of Prince Xiao of Qinghe, the emperor's brother, as Emperor An.
Zhou Zhang thought that the public was unwilling to follow him, so he conspired to close the palace gates, kill the brothers of Chariot and Cavalry General Deng Zhi, Zheng Zhong, and Cai Lun, hijack the minister, deposed the Queen Mother in the South Palace, made Emperor An the king of Yuan, and made Liu Sheng, the king of Pingyuan, the emperor.
The plot was discovered, the plan was dismissed, and Zhou Zhang committed suicide.
The family had no money left, so they changed their clothes and went out to eat by the sun.
summary:
Zheng Hong, from Kuaiji, was a misfit as a youth but was recognized by Prefect Wu Lun. He studied under Jiao Kuang, who was implicated in Liu Ying’s rebellion. When Jiao Kuang died, Zheng Hong shaved his head and pleaded for his family, gaining fame. As magistrate of Zuxian, he governed benevolently. He became Shangshu Ling and proposed that Shangshu Lang supplement Thousand Stone Orders, which the emperor accepted. As chief agricultural officer, he opened mountain roads in Lingling and Guiyang, saving three trillion in assets. He reduced contributions and corvee fees during drought. As Taiwei, he deferred to the Fifth Lun, and the emperor set up a screen between them. He reported Zhang Lin and Yang Guang for corruption, but Dou Xian accused him of leaking secrets. Zheng Hong resigned and died, leaving instructions for a simple burial. Zhou Zhang, from Nanyang, was a meritorious official. He advised the prefect against privately visiting Dou Xian, cutting the horse’s leather to stop him. When Dou Xian was executed, the prefect was spared. Zhou Zhang became Guang Luxun and later Sikong. He criticized Zheng Zhong and Cai Lun’s power. After Emperor He’s death, Empress Dowager Deng made Liu Sheng king of Pingyuan and later Emperor An. Zhou Zhang plotted to kill Deng Zhi, Zheng Zhong, and Cai Lun, depose the queen, and make Liu Sheng emperor. The plot was discovered, and Zhou Zhang committed suicide, leaving no wealth. The historian warned against such usurpation, citing Mencius.
