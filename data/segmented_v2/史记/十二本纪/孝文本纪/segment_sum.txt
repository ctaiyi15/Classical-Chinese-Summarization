上曰： 百金中民十家之产，吾奉先帝宫室，常恐羞之，何以台为！
上常衣綈衣，所幸慎夫人，令衣不得曳地，帏帐不得文绣，以示敦朴，为天下先。
治霸陵皆以瓦器，不得以金银铜锡为饰，不治坟，欲为省，毋烦民。
南越王尉佗自立为武帝，然上召贵尉佗兄弟，以德报之，佗遂去帝称臣。
与匈奴和亲，匈奴背约入盗，然令边备守，不发兵深入，恶烦苦百姓。
吴王诈病不朝，就赐几杖。
群臣如袁盎等称说虽切，常假借用之。
群臣如张武等受赂遗金钱，觉，上乃发御府金钱赐之，以愧其心，弗下吏。
专务以德化民，是以海内殷富，兴於礼义。
後七年六月己亥，帝崩於未央宫。
translation: 
In the second year of the Yuan Dynasty, the emperor said: I am not wise and cannot spread virtue far away, so the countries outside the Central Plains are unrest.
The people in the remote areas around me cannot live peacefully, and the people in the country work hard and cannot live in peace. Both of these faults are due to my lack of morality and my inability to spread my virtue far away.
Recently, the Huns have been invading the border for many years, killing many officials and people. The officials and generals in the border area cannot understand my inner thoughts, which aggravates my bad behavior.
With such long-term wars and inexplicable disasters, how can the countries inside and outside the Central Plains find peace?
Now I get up early and go to bed late, working hard on the world's major affairs, feeling sad for thousands of people, feeling worried and uneasy in my heart, and I can't forget this matter even one day. Therefore, I sent envoys, with crowns and canopies facing each other on the road and rutted roads, to explain my thoughts to the Xiongnu Chanyu.
Now the Chanyu has returned to the correct path of ancient times, considering the peace of the country, seeking the interests of millions of people, personally abandoning those small mistakes with me, uniting on the correct principles, and forming a brotherly relationship to protect the good people of the world.
The marriage has been decided and will start this year.
In the winter of AD 6, 30,000 Xiongnu entered Shangjun, and 30,000 Xiongnu entered Yunzhong.
The emperor appointed Ling Mian, a senior official in the army, as the general of chariots and cavalry, stationed at Feihu; he appointed Su Yi, the former Prime Minister of Chu, as the general, stationed at Juzhu; General Zhang Wu garrisoned the north; Zhou Yafu, the governor of Hanoi County, garrisoned at Xiliu; Zongzheng Liu Li as the general, garrisoned at Ba Shang; Zhu Zihou garrisoned at Jimen to jointly defend against the Huns.
After a few months, the Huns withdrew, and the various armies also withdrew.
There was a drought and locust infestation across the country.
The emperor favored the world, ordered the princes not to pay tribute to the court, abolished the ban on mountains, forests and lakes, reduced the emperor's clothing, utensils, and dogs and horses for play, reduced the number of ministers, opened granaries, provided relief to the poor, and the people could sell their titles to buy food.
Emperor Xiaowen came to the capital from Daiguo and reigned for twenty-three years. Nothing was added to the palaces, gardens, dogs and horses, clothing, and utensils. If there were any inconveniences to the people, they were changed to benefit the people.
I once planned to build a terrace and asked a craftsman to calculate the cost. One hundred pounds of gold was needed.
The emperor said: One hundred kilograms of gold is equivalent to the property of ten families of ordinary people. I enjoy the palace of the late emperor, and I am often worried that I will humiliate the late emperor. Why build this terrace?
The emperor often wore thick silk clothes, and his beloved Mrs. Shen was not allowed to drag her clothes to the ground, and her draperies were not allowed to have embroidery or brocade in order to show her honesty and simplicity, and to set an example for the world.
All mausoleums are built with earthenware, and gold, silver, copper, and tin are not allowed to be used for decoration. No tall tombs are built, in order to save some money and not disturb the people.
The king of Nanyue, Wei Tuo, proclaimed himself Emperor Wu, but the emperor called Wei Tuo's brother and gave high-ranking officials generous salaries in return. Wei Tuo canceled his imperial title and professed himself to the Han Dynasty.
After making peace with the Xiongnu, the Xiongnu violated the treaty and invaded the border to plunder. However, the emperor only ordered the border fortresses to strengthen their defense and did not send troops deep into the hinterland of the Xiongnu for fear of disturbing the people.
King Wu pretended to be ill and did not go to the capital to attend court. The emperor immediately rewarded him with a sitting table and a cane.
Although ministers such as Yuan An and others discussed matters sharply and urgently, the emperor often adopted their suggestions with a tolerant attitude.
Among the ministers, Zhang Wu and others accepted money bribes. When they found out, the emperor took out the money from his treasury and rewarded them, which made them feel ashamed and did not hand it over to the officials for punishment.
The emperor devoted himself wholeheartedly to educating the people with morality. Therefore, there was prosperity and a culture of paying attention to etiquette and justice in the four seas.
On June 1, the seventh year of AD, the emperor died in Weiyang Palace.

summary:
summary line 3: The emperor's reign was marked by frugality; he avoided building a terrace costing 100 pounds of gold, wore simple clothes, and forbade extravagance. He maintained peace with Nanyue and the Xiongnu through diplomacy, and tolerated critical ministers. His policies fostered moral education and stability. He died in Weiyang Palace in the seventh year of his reign, leaving a will urging simple mourning, minimal burial, and the release of harem women. He was honored as Emperor Xiaowen, and his temple was designated Taizong, with the "Zhaode Dance" created to commemorate his virtues. Historians praised his benevolence, humility, and effective governance, noting that his reign brought prosperity and moral clarity to the Han dynasty.
