summary:
summary line 2: Zhou Bo's son Zhou Shengzhi inherited the title but lost it due to murder. Emperor Wen then selected Zhou Yafu, another son, as Marquis of Tiao. Xu Negation predicted Zhou Yafu would be a marquis, general, and prime minister but would starve to death. Zhou Yafu became a general, famously commanding the Xiliu camp with strict discipline, impressing Emperor Wen. During the Rebellion of the Seven States, he served as Taiwei, successfully defeating Wu and Chu by cutting off their food supplies, though this earned him the enmity of King Xiao of Liang. He later became prime minister but fell out of favor with Emperor Jing over disputes regarding the appointment of marquises, including opposing the enfeoffment of the queen's brother Wang Xin and Xiongnu defectors.
