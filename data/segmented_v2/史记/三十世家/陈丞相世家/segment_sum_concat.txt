During the Chen Sheng uprising, he served Wei Wang Jiu but left after his advice was ignored. He then joined Xiang Yu, who appointed him Lord Xinwu. Fearing execution after a failed campaign, Chen Ping fled to the Han king, Liu Bang, who made him a captain. Despite slander from generals like Zhou Bo and Guan Ying about his past and alleged bribery, Chen Ping defended himself, and Liu Bang rewarded him generously, appointing him to supervise the army.
