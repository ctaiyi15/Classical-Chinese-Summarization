Li Shiqi was from Gaoyang, Chenliu.
He likes to study very much, but his family is poor and destitute. He doesn't even have a property that can afford to clothe and feed himself, so he has to work as a lowly clerk looking after the inner door.
But despite this, the wise men and powerful men in the county did not dare to use him casually. People in the county called him "Kangsheng".
When Chen Sheng, Xiang Liang and others rebelled against the Qin Dynasty, dozens of generals passed through Gaoyang when they attacked the city. However, Li Shiqi heard that these people were narrow-minded, liked trivial etiquette, were self-willed, and could not listen to open-minded words. So he lived in seclusion and hid himself, not to cater to these people.
Later, he heard that Pei Gong led troops to capture the city and came to the outskirts of Chenliu. One of Pei Gong's cavalrymen happened to be the son of an old friend of Li Shi's neighborhood. Pei Gong often asked him about the wise men and heroes in his hometown.
One day, when the cavalry came home, Li Shiqi saw him and said to him: I heard that Pei Gong was arrogant and looked down upon others, but he had many far-reaching strategies. This is the person I really want to follow, but I have no one to introduce him to him.
When you see Pei Gong, you can say this to him: There is a Mr. Li in my hometown who is over sixty years old and eight feet tall. People call him a crazy person, but he himself says that he is not a crazy person.

Li Shiqi said: Just say it like I taught you.
After the knight returned, he told Pei Gong according to Li Sheng's instructions.
Later, Pei Gong came to Gaoyang, stayed in an inn, and sent someone to summon Li Shiqi to come and see him.
When Li Sheng came to the hotel, Pei Gong sat on the bed with his legs spread apart and asked two women to wash his feet. He called Li Sheng to see him.
Li Sheng went in, only made a long salute without leaning down to worship, and said: "Do you want to help Qin attack the princes, or do you want to lead the princes to destroy Qin?"
Pei Gong scolded: You Confucian scholar!
The people of the world have been suffering from the Qin Dynasty for a long time, so the princes have raised armies to resist the violent Qin one after another. How can you help the Qin Dynasty attack the princes?
Li Sheng said: If you are determined to gather the people and call for volunteers to overthrow the tyrannical and cruel Qin Dynasty, you should not receive the elders in such a arrogant and disrespectful manner.
So Pei Gong immediately stopped washing his feet, tidied his clothes, invited Li Sheng to the guest's seat, and apologized to him.
Li Sheng talked about the strategy used by the six kingdoms to unite vertically and horizontally. Pei Gong was very happy and ordered people to bring rice for Li Sheng to eat. Then he asked: "Then how do you think we should formulate our strategy today?"
Li Sheng said: If you gather the rabble and scattered soldiers, the total is less than 10,000 people. If you use this to directly confront the powerful Qin, it will be what people often say is exploring the tiger's mouth.
Chenliu is an important transportation thoroughfare in the world, extending in all directions. Now there is a lot of grain in the city.
I am very close to the magistrate of Chenliu. Please send me to him to ask him to surrender to you.
If he doesn't obey, you can send troops to attack the city again, and I can act as an internal response in the city.
So Pei Gong sent Li Sheng there and followed closely with his own troops. In this way, he captured Chenliu and gave Li Sheng the title of Lord of Guangye.
Li Sheng recommended his younger brother Li Shang to lead thousands of people to follow Pei Gong to the southwest to capture the city.
Li Sheng himself often served as a lobbyist and traveled among the princes as an envoy.
summary:
Li Shiqi, a learned man from Gaoyang, Chenliu, lived in poverty and worked as a lowly clerk. Despite his humble status, local elites dared not employ him casually, calling him "Kangsheng." During the rebellions against Qin, he avoided serving narrow-minded generals until he heard of Pei Gong (Liu Bang). Through a cavalryman, he arranged a meeting. When they met, Pei Gong was washing his feet with two women, but Li Shiqi criticized his disrespect, prompting Pei Gong to apologize. Li Shiqi advised capturing Chenliu for its strategic location and grain stores, offering to persuade the magistrate to surrender. Pei Gong agreed, and with Li Shiqi's help, Chenliu was taken. Li Shiqi was titled Lord of Guangye and later recommended his brother Li Shang to serve Pei Gong. An alternate account describes Li Shiqi's earlier meeting with Pei Gong, where he insisted on being called a "drunkard" rather than a scholar, criticized Pei Gong's disrespect, and again advised capturing Chenliu. He killed the magistrate at night, enabling Pei Gong to take the city and raise troops. Sima Qian notes that later accounts misplace Li Shiqi's role, but he confirms the details through his friendship with Pingyuanjun's son.

Pingyuanjun (Zhu Jian), a Chu native, served Yingbu but opposed his rebellion. After Yingbu's defeat, he was spared. He was upright, refusing to associate with the corrupt Piyanghou. When Pingyuanjun's mother died, Lu Jia advised Piyanghou to give generous funeral gifts, winning his favor. Later, when Piyanghou was imprisoned, Pingyuanjun persuaded the emperor's favorite Hong Jiru to intercede, securing his release. After Empress Dowager Lü's death, Pingyuanjun helped save Piyanghou again. Under Emperor Wen, Pingyuanjun was implicated in Piyanghou's crimes and committed suicide to protect his family. His son was appointed Taizhong doctor but died on a mission to the Xiongnu.
