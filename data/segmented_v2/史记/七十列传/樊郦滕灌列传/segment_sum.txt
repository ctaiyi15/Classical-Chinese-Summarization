summary:
summary line 0: Fan Kuai, a native of Peixian County, initially made a living by killing dogs and selling meat. He joined Liu Bang (later Emperor Gaozu of Han) in raising troops, capturing Peixian, and serving as a servant. Throughout numerous battles, Fan Kuai consistently demonstrated valor, often leading assaults on cities and beheading enemy soldiers. His military exploits included defeating Qin forces at Puyang, Chengyang, and Kaifeng, among others, earning him successive titles and promotions from Pei Gong (Liu Bang). A pivotal moment occurred at the Hongmen Banquet, where Xiang Yu plotted to kill Pei Gong. Fan Kuai, stationed outside, burst into the camp with an iron shield, confronted Xiang Yu, and condemned his actions, ultimately saving Pei Gong's life. Following Xiang Yu's defeat, Fan Kuai continued to serve Gaozu, suppressing rebellions by Zang Tu, Han Xin, Chen Xi, and Lu Wan, and was enfeoffed as Marquis of Wuyang with 5,400 households. He married Empress Lü's sister, Lü Xu, and remained close to Gaozu. During Gaozu's illness, Fan Kuai boldly entered the palace to urge him to attend to state affairs. Later, slandered for conspiring with the Lü clan, Gaozu ordered his execution, but Chen Ping delayed, and after Gaozu's death, Empress Lü released him. Fan Kuai died in the sixth year of Emperor Hui's reign, posthumously titled Marquis Wu. His descendants held the title until it was revoked in Emperor Jing's time due to legal transgressions.
