When King Hui of Wei was about to leave, the uncle withdrew his entourage and said: If the king does not use Gongsun Yang, he must be killed and not allowed to leave the country.

Shortly after Gongshu Zuo's death, Gongsun Yang heard that Duke Xiao of Qin ordered to search for talented people throughout the country in order to restore the hegemony of Duke Mu of Qin and regain the lost territory in the east. He went west to enter the Qin State and relied on Duke Xiao's favored minister Jing Jian to seek an audience with Duke Xiao.
Duke Xiao summoned Wei Yang and asked him to talk about state affairs for a long time. Duke Xiao fell asleep while listening and couldn't listen at all.
Afterwards, Duke Xiao was angry with Jingjian and said: "Your guest is a bully. How can you appoint such a person?"
Jing Jian again used Xiao Gong's words to blame Wei Yang.
Wei Yang said: I used the methods of Yao and Shun to persuade the king, but his mind could not understand.
A few days later, Jing Jian asked Duke Xiao to summon Wei Yang.
When Wei Yang met with Duke Xiao, he explained the way of governing the country incisively and vividly, but it was not in line with Duke Xiao's wishes.
Afterwards, Duke Xiao blamed Jing Jian again, and Jing Jian also blamed Wei Yang.
Wei Yang said: I used the methods of Yu, Tang, Wen and Wu to persuade the king, but he would not listen.
Ask him to summon me again.
Wei Yang met Duke Xiao again. Duke Xiao was very friendly to him, but he did not use him.
After exiting the meeting, Xiaogong said to Jingjian: Your guest is good. I can talk to him.
Jing Jian told Wei Yang, and Wei Yang said: I used the Five Hegemons' methods of governing the country to convince the king, and I saw that he was ready to adopt it.
If you summon me again, I'll know what to say.
So Wei Yang met Duke Xiao again, and Duke Xiao had a very good conversation with him. He unknowingly moved his knees forward on the mat, and they talked for several days without feeling tired.
Jing Jian said: How can you win over the king? Our king is very happy.
Wei Yang replied: I advised the king to adopt the imperial method of governing the country and establish a prosperous age like Xia, Shang and Zhou. However, the king said: The time is too long, I can't wait, let alone a wise king. Who doesn't want to be famous when he is in power? How can I be asked to wait in obscurity for decades or hundreds of years to achieve the great cause of becoming an emperor?
Therefore, I used the method of enriching the country and strengthening the army to persuade him, and he was very happy.
However, this cannot be compared with the virtues of Yin and Zhou.
Shortly after Qin Xiaogong appointed Wei Yang, he planned to change the laws, but he was also worried that people in the world would talk about him.

summary:
Lord Shang, originally named Gongsun Yang, was the son of a concubine of the Kingdom of Wei. As a youth, he studied criminal law and magic, serving under his uncle, the Prime Minister of Wei. When his uncle fell ill, King Wei Hui visited and asked about a successor. The uncle recommended Gongsun Yang, but the king remained silent. The uncle then privately advised the king to kill Gongsun Yang if he was not appointed, to prevent him from leaving the country. However, Gongsun Yang refused to flee, reasoning that the king would not heed advice to kill him if he ignored advice to appoint him. After his uncle's death, Gongsun Yang heard that Duke Xiao of Qin sought talented individuals to restore Qin's former glory. He entered Qin through the recommendation of Duke Xiao's favored minister, Jing Jian.

其明年，卫鞅说孝公曰： 秦之与魏，譬若人之有腹心疾，非魏并秦，秦即并魏。
何者？

今以君之贤圣，国赖以盛。

孝公以为然，使卫鞅将而伐魏。
魏使公子卬将而击之。
军既相距，卫鞅遗魏将公子卬书曰： 吾始与公子驩，今俱为两国将，不忍相攻，可与公子面相见，盟，乐饮而罢兵，以安秦魏。

Unify the national weights and measures system.

Five years later, when the Qin state became prosperous and powerful, the Emperor of Zhou gave the sacrificial meat to Duke Xiao of Qin, and princes from all over the world came to congratulate him.

Another year later, Wei Yang persuaded Qin Xiaogong: The relationship between Qin and Wei is like a person suffering from a heart disease. Either Wei annexes Qin, or Qin annexes Wei.
Why do you say that?
The Wei State was located to the west of the high mountains, with its capital in Anyi. The Yellow River was the boundary with the Qin State and it monopolized the geographical advantage east of Mount Wei.
When the situation is favorable, they will invade the Qin State to the west, and when the situation is unfavorable, they will expand their territory eastward.
Now, with the help of the king's virtuous and sage wisdom, Qin is prosperous.
The Wei State was severely defeated by Qi last year, and the princes betrayed it one after another. They can take this opportunity to attack Wei State.
Wei cannot resist Qin and will definitely retreat eastward.
Once it retreats to the east, Qin will occupy the dangerous terrain of the Yellow River and Huashan Mountain, and can control the princes and countries to the east. This is the great cause of the emperor to unify the world.
Qin Xiaogong thought it was right and sent Wei Yang to lead an army to attack Wei.
The state of Wei sent its son Yan to lead troops to attack the Qin army.
When the two armies met and confronted each other, Wei Yang sent someone to send a letter to the general of the Wei army, Gongzi Yan, writing: I had a good relationship with the young master at the beginning, but now you and I are generals of two hostile countries and cannot bear to attack each other. Can we meet the young master in person, conclude an alliance, have a few drinks, and then withdraw our troops, so that both Qin and Wei will be in peace.
